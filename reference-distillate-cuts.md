## Cut 1 — §0 THE ENGINE

**Removed:** 2026-08-27 · **Replaced by:** §0 Recurring shapes

**Why:** the section asserted that all six sources run one machine, and stated a single sentence as *the* engine of the corpus. As a description of the early slate that was accurate. As a standing claim it had become false, and it was reading as a formula a pitch had to satisfy rather than a pattern the corpus had noticed about itself — which is the opposite of what the distillate is for. Amendment A3 already says the profile is evolving and no prior rule is canonical against a live instinct; this section was the one most in tension with that.

**The evidence that it stopped being true.** Checked against all 21 stories in `ideas-v2.md`:

- *Fits cleanly* — Fully Vested, The Queue, Runway, Root of Title, Half-Life, Offsets, Suppression, Founder's Day, Chime Generator, Threshold, Recognition.
- *Fits partially* — Right Concentration, Continuity of Care, Viability, Verbatim, Super-Donor. The maintained schedule is present but is not where the horror sits.
- *Does not fit* — Bloom (nothing is maintained; a passive safety simply ran out), "Without Regards" (months, not decades, and no schedule at all), Designated Pathogen Free (the process escaped its maintainers rather than being kept by them), Dwell Time (arrived nineteen years ago and is maintained by nobody; the institution is a delay, not a maintenance).
- *Contradicts it outright* — Second Circulation. The formula requires "competent people who are not deceived." In that story everyone is deceived, by design, and the deception is the premise.

**Also removed with it:** the per-source attribution sentence. It said the same thing as the Quick lookup at the foot of the distillate, less precisely, and it carried the one-machine framing in its structure.

### The section as it stood

```markdown
## 0\. THE ENGINE

All six sources run one machine in different housings:

> **Something has been operating for a long time on a correct principle nobody remembers, maintained by competent people who are not deceived, at a cost that is scheduled, documented, and — on the evidence — worth paying.**

Chiang supplies the correctness. Watts supplies the reason the cost is worth paying. setting-c supplies the duration and the maintenance. setting-b supplies the institutions' rightness. djkaktus supplies the document. Evangelion supplies the instrument that is a person, and the delivery date.
```

**What survived into the replacement:** the formula itself, demoted from *the* engine to the most common of several configurations, with its coverage stated honestly. **What did not:** the claim that the sources run one machine, and the attribution sentence.

## Cut 2 — §THE SIX TESTS

**Removed:** 2026-08-27 · **Replaced by:** §The six questions

**Why:** same problem as Cut 1, one section further down. A numbered suite called *tests*, several phrased as pass/fail ("if it exists to tell the story, it fails"), reads as a gate a pitch has to clear. It had stopped describing what the corpus does: by the time of the 2026-08-27 stories, three of the six were being declined deliberately and one had become conditional on a caution elsewhere in the document.

**Not dropped, because the corpus leans on them.** The names are referenced 18 times across `ideas-v2.md` — story notes say "five tests not yet run", "Chiang test passes clean", "the djkaktus test on the memorandum format". Deleting the section would orphan all of it. So the names and substance are kept and the gate framing removed.

**What had stopped being true:**

- **Chiang** demanded no second impossibility. *Second Circulation* fails it by design and says so — aliens are explicitly permitted. *Dwell Time* spends exactly one impossibility and pays for it, with everything downstream dose response and arithmetic. The live question is how many impossibilities and whether they are paid for, not whether there are none.
- **Watts** was never universal. *"Without Regards"* carries an open item asking whether the Watts layer is present at all, and answering that it may be fine if not.
- **setting-b** is inapplicable where there is no metaphysics. *Verbatim* is marked complete with nothing supernatural, by design.
- **djkaktus** demanded a load-bearing artifact. That is now conditional on the standing caution that document-as-monster is at capacity — *Second Circulation* answers it by having no document at all, and names that as the point.
- **setting-c** is the one that held everywhere, and it is the only one of the six that is genuinely diagnostic rather than stylistic.
- **Evangelion** applies only where there is an instrument.

**Also noted:** seven references in `ideas-v2.md` still say *five* tests, predating the addition of the Evangelion question. Left as written rather than silently renumbered.

### The section as it stood

```markdown
## THE SIX TESTS

1. **Chiang.** Does the premise, followed rigorously with no cheating, produce the dread by itself? No second impossibility in act three.  
2. **Watts.** Does it threaten the assumption that having a self is necessary or advantageous? Strong form: *the ones without selves outperform the ones with them, the data supports it, and the market noticed.*  
3. **setting-b.** If the metaphysics is materially true, the institutions built on it are correct, not deluded. Nobody is a fanatic; everybody is a ratepayer.  
4. **setting-c.** Is the awful thing load-bearing? Remove it and does the system stop? The reformer must be right and lose anyway.  
5. **djkaktus.** Does the artifact exist to *do a job*, or to tell the story? If it exists to tell the story, it fails.  
6. **Evangelion.** Is the instrument a person, and does the institution know? If the terrible thing could be done by a machine instead, it is engineering. If it requires a specific person, and the paperwork for using them is complete and correct at every step, it is this register.
```

**What survived:** all six names, and the substance of each question. **What did not:** the numbering, the word *test*, the pass/fail phrasing, and the implication that a story must satisfy all six.

## Cut 3 — M27 and the radio play-by-play

**Removed:** 2026-08-28 · **Replaced by:** nothing. The comic register is withdrawn from the toolkit.

**Why:** a survey of the reference files for articles whose net register is comic or heartwarming rather than horrific found eleven comic articles and two warm ones, and the register does not want any of them. The distillate carried two entries that licensed that mode: a craft move recommending comedy as an anaesthetic, and an artifact form whose whole content was a comic register. Both are gone. This is a taste decision, not a finding about the sources — djkaktus is genuinely good at comedy and the annexes record that faithfully. It is simply not available here.

**What was removed, verbatim:**

```markdown
- **M27. The comic register as anaesthetic.** Real comedy, well built, so the guard is fully down when the thing arrives. Load-bearing, not decorative. `[K]`

**Radio play-by-play.** A comic register played dead straight over the unspeakable. `[K]`
```

**M27 is not reissued.** The number stays withdrawn rather than being reused, so that any story note referring to it still resolves. The move count in §2 drops from 79 to 78.

**Not removed, and not to be confused with this cut.** Warmth as a delivery mechanism is untouched and remains central: the warm-professional voice (M32), the captured human voice (M35), the interviewer who apologises first (M72), *warm the whole time, aimed at nothing*, and the guardrail requiring every narrator to be good at their job and warm. The distinction is that those are warmth doing harm, not warmth as the article's net register.

**The articles this rules out**, so the ground is not re-walked. Net comic: SCP-80s-J, SCP-4000-J, SCP-001-DJK-J, SCP-001-CK-J, SCP-049-J, SCP-7143-J, SCP-2090, SCP-2398, SCP-2522, SCP-1781, SCP-4498. Net warm: SCP-3355 (*St. Nick*), SCP-2576 (*Joseph and the Technicolor Dream Goat*). Warm-then-turning, and therefore still available: SCP-2980, SCP-4173, SCP-4390, SCP-4489. Annex F contains none of either.
