# Fog Belt — format and provenance

*Everything that belongs to the slate rather than to one story. Preserved from the four source documents; where two of them said the same thing at different times, both are kept and dated, because the difference between them is itself a record.*

---

## The story schema

Each file under `stories/` has three sections:

- **Summary** — one paragraph.
- **Synopsis** — the story in prose, at length.
- **Notes** — metadata, status, open items, differentiation warnings, decisions owed.

`stories/00-undeveloped.md` is the exception: it is the bench, not a story, and holds the greenlit concepts that were never developed, the parked attempts with the reasoning that parked them, the held pairs and the passed-on list.

Synopses for stories 1–21 run to the four-to-six paragraph convention. Stories 22–24 run longer; the author's prose was preserved rather than compressed.

---

## The format rule

*The artifact must be load-bearing, not a testimony wrapper.* Checked story by story across both passes:

| Story | The artifact is |
| :-- | :-- |
| Fully Vested | the schedule |
| The Queue | the ordination |
| Runway | the walk |
| Right Concentration | the app itself |
| Continuity of Care | the instrument |
| Root of Title | the teaching session in which establishment occurs |
| Half-Life | the dive log |
| Viability | the grading log plus the audit |

*(The first-pass note listed six; the second listed eight. Both lists are folded together above.)*

The rule has since been amended twice — one narrator is a default rather than a constraint, and having no document at all is a legitimate answer. Both amendments are recorded in `distillate.md`, which is the live statement of doctrine; this page records only the form the slate was written in.

## Provenance

The slate was assembled from four source documents, standardised into one three-part form per story. Stories 1–8 were the original remix pass over the earliest picks (R1 and C1), which predate the intellectual spine added in cycle 6. Stories 9–15 were the addendum written after it. Story 16 is a rev-2 rebuild reconstructed from a lost voice session. Story 17 is a new original, not on the greenlit slate. Story 18 is a later dark remix and the first to depart from the single-narrator format. Story 19 is from the same session and departs again, using two registers. Story 20 returns to a single voice. Story 21 is a clean-origin new pitch and the first to abandon the document form entirely. Stories 22–24 are new originals, none of them remixes and none on the greenlit slate.

The originals' own framing is preserved below, because each says something about the conditions it was written under.

### Part one — stories 1–8 (the original remix pass)

> Seven expanded outlines, plus one in progress. Companion to Series Bible v0.3 and Greenlit Slate v3. August 2026.
>
> Remix pass over the earliest picks (R1 and C1), which predate the intellectual spine added in cycle 6 — the Chiang test (does the premise alone, followed rigorously, produce the dread?) and the Watts test (does this threaten the assumption that having a self is necessary or advantageous?). New reference register this pass: setting-c and setting-b — theology as engineering, hell as territory, institutions maintaining sacramental machinery without believing in it.
>
> Five are darker rebuilds of existing picks. One — Root of Title — is a ground-up replacement for The Fog Is a Waiting List, keeping only that story's core (accrued displacement coming due) and discarding its mechanics entirely.
>
> All are stated from scratch, not as deltas.

### Part two — stories 9–15 (the addendum)

> Companion to "Fog Belt — Dark Remix v2" (sections 1–8). The Drive master could not be updated in place: the Drive connector's update operation handles title and parent only, not content. This addendum carries everything written since.
>
> New this addendum: Offsets, Suppression, Bloom, Founder's Day (in progress), Verbatim, Chime Generator (parked), and a new pitch derived from SCP-8980.

### Part three — story 16 (Threshold, rev 2)

Reconstructed from a voice session whose chat history was lost to a client-side crash before it reached Drive; written up from the surviving Jaynes annex and recollection. Rev 2 carries two corrections and supersedes that first reconstruction.

*A session note from the rev-1 write-up, worth keeping:* save early and often during long remix sessions.

### Part four — story 17 (Recognition)

New original, developed in voice session 2026-08-26, not on the greenlit slate. Carries the three doctrine amendments now recorded in `distillate.md`.

### Part five — story 18 (Designated Pathogen Free)

A later dark remix, developed 2026-08-27, superseding a C8 one-line greenlit pitch that was never expanded. The first story on the slate to depart from the single-narrator house style; its format note carries a standing implication that the one-narrator rule is now a default rather than a constraint.

### Part six — story 19 (Dwell Time)

Developed 2026-08-27, first pass, from the "Heaven withheld" open thread in Reference Register v2. Uses two registers rather than one voice — a filed service record, and a present-tense account past the line that has no reporting format.

### Part seven — story 20 (Super-Donor)

Developed 2026-08-27, superseding a C8 one-line greenlit pitch that was never expanded. Deliberately the corpus's body-horror extreme, and named in its own doctrine checks as the test case for the register's unresolved calibration question of where disturbing becomes cheap.

### Part eight — story 21 (Second Circulation)

A clean-origin new pitch developed 2026-08-27 — not a remix, and not on the greenlit slate. The first story on the slate whose form is not a document at all, taken as the deliberate exit from the standing caution that document-as-monster is at capacity. Its arrival also resolves a dangling reference: stories 20 and 21 flag each other, and until this was written the pointer from Super-Donor had no target in the corpus.

### Part nine — stories 22–24 (Provisional Forms, Residue, Arm Ninety-One)

Developed 2026-08-28 and delivered already in the three-part form, so they were folded in as written rather than restated. All three are new originals: no greenlit-slate ancestor, no remix. *Residue* and *Arm Ninety-One* flag each other explicitly as the corpus's closest neighbours and carry their own differentiation instructions.
