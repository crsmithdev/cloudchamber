# World A: The Tide Table

The assigned direction. The draw page rendered as a nautical almanac read at
a chart table at night. Mockup target for phase B.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The draw is a table before it is a page. Five premises are five rows
of one ruled table, ranked by a column of numbers, and the prose is a row
that opens. It refuses the card-and-pill dashboard and the chat transcript.

OWN-WORLD: Chart-table night. A cold near-black desk; panes as dusky sheets a
step lighter, never cream. Rules in two weights: a hairline between rows, a
heavy rule under every column head. Column heads in small-caps sans; every
number tabular, in mono; prose in the serif at reading size only when a row
is open. State is a mark in a fixed cell: filled holds, hollow does not, a
strike passes, a tick confirms, colour on the mark and nowhere else. The
chosen or opened row takes the raised fill and the inset gold bar; a
reversed light line was tried and read wrong on the desk (Chris, phase C,
2026-09-15). Gold on the focused control and the opened row's bar.

STORY: He reads the distribution as a column of tides, sees the lowest and
highest at once, opens one row to read its vignette at full width beneath
the row, and presses choose in that row's last cell.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Rail and list as the dark desk. Reading pane: a two-line
head note for the seed; then the table, five rows of two lines each, columns
# · p · bar · premise · state · choose, with the opened vignette spanning
under its row; then examples as a second, smaller table. Right column: the
step log as a time table, stage · started · seconds, the running row's
seconds cell ticking. Choose sits at the end of every row while gating.

FORM: The nautical almanac, position 5 of 7 on the grounded list, seed key
de67593c, assigned.

Raised by the hand it beat, one line per donor:

- From the timetable slide rack, declined: rank without size. One chrome
  size; weight, case, reversal and rule carry hierarchy.
- From the timetable's departures board, declined: state lives in a fixed
  column, and a state word is never a pill.
- From Ikeda datamatics, declined: focus drops the rest. Opening one row
  fades every other row to hairline and number.
- From the oscilloscope bench, declined: the running state is a sweep, not a
  dot. A live seconds cell and a moving rule under the step in flight.
- From the viewfinder HUD, declined: the centre belongs to the subject. Chrome
  keeps to the edges; the opened vignette owns the middle.
- From the cloud quarry and the sneaker wall, declined: nothing kept.

Honest risk: a table of prose is a table of long cells. If the premise column
wraps past two lines at 1920, the gate scrolls and the world fails question
one.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the
finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its
provenance.
