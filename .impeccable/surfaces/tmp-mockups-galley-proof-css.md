# World B: The Galley Proof

Impeccable's pick: the top grounded candidate, not the roll. The draw page
rendered as a galley proof under a desk lamp, judged in the margin. Mockup
target for phase B.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Judgement lives in the margin. The text block is set once and never
decorated; every score, verdict, control and note is marginalia in the
proofreader's hand. It refuses the dashboard and the calm cream writing app
alike: the sheet is a working proof, not a page to admire.

OWN-WORLD: A lit sheet on a dark desk. Rail, list and inspector are the desk,
near-black and cool. The reading pane is the proof: bright cool proof stock,
not cream, with the galley set in the serif at a 60ch measure. The left
margin carries the compositor's slug in mono: index, probability, a thin
bar. The right margin carries the marks in the blue pencil that is the one
accent: choose as a marginal tick, keep as stet, pass as a delete loop, an
artifact as a query. Verdict hues colour the mark only. Running work shows
as a slug line across the head of the sheet, the step in flight underlined
and moving.

STORY: He reads five galleys down one sheet, each premise as a set paragraph
with its vignette folded under a fold mark, and marks the one he wants in
the margin.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Dark rail and list. The sheet spans reading pane and
inspector. Across its head, the slug line: stage · seconds for every step.
Under it the seed as an italic head note, then five galleys, each with its
slug on the left, its premise and two folded lines on the right, and the
choose tick in the outer margin. Examples as a sources block at the foot.
Choose sits in the margin of every galley while gating.

FORM: The galley proof, position 1 of 7 on the grounded list, seed key
de67593c, the pick.

Honest risk: this is the familiar destination. Editorial tools land on a
proof sheet, and a lit sheet at night is glare; if the sheet cannot be
dimmed it fails the operator's scene.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the
finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its
provenance.
