The twelfth envoy was not sent.

Okonkwo's report went up on the fourth of June, three pages, the last line verbatim: *Tell them to send someone next month who's stolen something.* The director read it in his office with the Van photographs face-down on the blotter, then read it again, then asked for the file's transit log. Eleven entries. Eleven months. A phalanx, a rib, a fragment of parietal from Ghent, each catalogued with a date that fell within a week of an envoy's visit. He counted the envoys and then he counted the elements and then he sat for a while.

His confessor was booked for Friday, as every month. He cancelled it. Then, because he was a careful man and the sequence mattered, he took the July order from the drawer, the one already typed with a blank for the name, and signed it before he did anything else.

The crate was in a cold room under the library. He had it opened. He had not looked at her since Hakkari; nobody senior had. Fifty-two pieces still held. The wire had gone dull where the wax had let go, and the Lyon socket was empty, a small neat absence at the hip like a tooth missing from a child's smile.

He had the room cleared. He did not kneel; that was theatre, and Lauro had been right that theatre was what they'd been sending. He held the July order in one hand and the commissioning order in the other, his own signature on both, and he said it the way one reads a charge sheet.

"I ordered the femur taken from Lyon. I ordered all of them. Thirty-eight churches. I was sorry each time. I have been absolved eleven times for it and I have just signed the twelfth."

He waited for the part that would make it true, and found it was already there. He did not want to have done it. He would do it again; the paper in his right hand was the proof. Both at once, in one sentence, in one room, with no grille between them and no one to say the words that would close it.

She did nothing visible. He had not expected her to. Lauro's field had been a valley and hers had been a regiment; his was a column thirty-four vehicles long, four hundred kilometres east, and the Office would learn what she'd done to it the way it learned everything, from a morning report.

He had the crate sealed. He went back upstairs. He did not rebook Friday. He understood, walking, what Lauro had understood in April: that the thing could be fed exactly as long as he refused to be forgiven for it, and that a man who keeps the gap open is not a man who can be a Jesuit, and that this was the same year either way.
