# ANNEX — MEASUREMENT

*Mechanisms, not stories. Eleven domains of physical and informational processes concerned with measuring, dating, recording and computing: what each process actually is, the figure that pins it, and the body and instrument that administer it — a standard, a permit, a guidance document, a compensation formula, a reliability rule, a certificate on a wall.*

*This is the domain in which the arithmetic is checkable. Where an entry carries a number, the number is the point; where it carries an institution, what is stated is what that body is and what its instrument does.*

*Items marked `[?]` are specifics a fact-check should confirm before they carry weight.*

## 1. Metrology and the traceability chain

- Metrological traceability is defined as an unbroken chain of documented calibrations, each contributing to measurement uncertainty, linking a result to a stated reference — the definition is in the international vocabulary of metrology (JCGM 200 / VIM), and the chain is the artefact, not any one instrument in it.
- Since **20 May 2019** the entire SI has been defined by fixed numerical values of seven constants, among them the Planck constant **h = 6.62607015 × 10⁻³⁴ J s exactly** and the caesium hyperfine transition frequency **Δν_Cs = 9,192,631,770 Hz exactly**.
- The International Prototype of the Kilogram, a platinum-iridium cylinder in a vault at Sèvres that had been the definition of mass since 1889, was retired after 130 years; it had six official sister copies (K1, 7, 8(41), 32, 43, 47) and roughly ten working copies.
- Comparisons across the twentieth century showed the IPK and its copies diverging by on the order of **50 µg**, with no established explanation and no way to say which had changed — the definition could not drift by construction, because it *was* the definition.
- The kilogram is now realised independently by the Kibble balance, which weighs against electrical power traceable to h, and by the X-ray-crystal-density route on an isotopically enriched silicon sphere; NIST disseminates it downward by mass comparison.
- The design criterion for the 2019 redefinition was continuity: nothing anyone weighed changed, and the achievement is that no measurement anywhere moved.
- In external-beam radiotherapy the chain runs BIPM → NIST primary standard water calorimeter → an Accredited Dosimetry Calibration Laboratory → the clinic's reference ionisation chamber → the linear accelerator's output measured under **AAPM TG-51** reference conditions; every link is documented, audited and assigned an uncertainty.
- Overall dose delivery uncertainty is targeted at a few per cent, because the therapeutic window between tumour control and normal-tissue complication is narrow.
- The chain terminates one step short of the patient: the final step, from a calibrated machine to a dose deposited in a particular tumour in a particular person, is a **computation** performed by a treatment planning system against a CT-derived model, and there is no reference artefact for a human being.
- At the Instituto Oncológico Nacional in Panama City in 2000–2001, shielding-block data were entered into a treatment planning system in a way the system accepted and interpreted differently from what was intended; the resulting dose calculations were substantially high, the error ran for months across a cohort, patients died, and the physicists were prosecuted.
- Comparable calibration and planning accidents occurred at Épinal and elsewhere; the **IAEA accident investigation reports** are the audited public record of exactly this failure class.
- Late radiation injury is a duration rather than an event: necrosis, fistulae and non-healing ulceration present months to years after treatment ends, in tissue whose repair capacity was consumed at the time of exposure.
- The **CIPM Mutual Recognition Arrangement** and its key comparisons are the mechanism by which national measurement institutes accept each other's certificates — a peer-audited network with published degrees of equivalence rather than a hierarchy.
- The clinic-level artefact is the **annual calibration certificate**: a document with a date, a stated uncertainty, and a scope that describes the machine.
- Every calibrated instrument drifts between calibrations, and the interval is chosen so that the drift stays inside the stated uncertainty — which means the drift within an interval is unobserved by design, not by neglect.
- Accreditation bodies (ILAC signatories, and for dosimetry the AAPM-accredited ADCLs) certify the *competence to calibrate*, so the chain's integrity rests on periodic audit of process rather than on continuous measurement of output.

## 2. Time, frequency and the clocks

- The second is defined by the caesium-133 ground-state hyperfine transition at exactly 9,192,631,770 Hz, which makes frequency the most accurately realised quantity in the SI and makes every other unit that can be reduced to a frequency more accurate for it.
- The **HP 5061A caesium beam frequency standard**, built in Palo Alto, made portable atomic time practical; instruments of that family were flown around the world in the 1971 Hafele–Keating experiment that measured relativistic time dilation directly against ground clocks.
- Optical lattice clocks now operate at systematic uncertainties around **8 × 10⁻¹⁹** (JILA strontium, *Phys. Rev. Lett.* 133, 023401, 2024), roughly two orders of magnitude better than the caesium standard that defines the second.
- At that performance the gravitational redshift is resolvable across a **millimetre of height** (JILA, 2022, at the 10⁻¹⁹ level), which makes proper time a surveying instrument and elevation a frequency measurement.
- The CIPM has a stated roadmap of criteria for redefining the second on an optical transition, which would be the first time the SI abandoned a definition that still worked `[?]`.
- **UTC does not exist in real time**: it is computed retrospectively by BIPM from an ensemble of several hundred clocks in national laboratories worldwide and published monthly in Circular T, so every real-time clock is a *prediction* of UTC and its error is known only afterwards `[?]` (verify the current clock and laboratory counts).
- Time is disseminated by GNSS common-view, by two-way satellite time and frequency transfer, and by optical fibre links; each has a different uncertainty budget and a different failure mode.
- NIST Technical Note 2189 documents the dependence of US critical infrastructure sectors on positioning, navigation and timing services — telecommunications, electric power, financial transaction timestamping and data centre synchronisation all rest on the same substrate with limited independent redundancy.
- When GNSS timing is lost, a site enters **holdover** and runs on its local oscillator, drifting at a rate set by that oscillator's stability; nothing fails at the moment of loss, and the failure appears later when accumulated drift crosses a protocol threshold.
- **CGPM Resolution 4 (2022)** decides that the maximum permitted value of |UT1−UTC| will be increased in or before **2035**, and requests that the CIPM work with the ITU to propose the new value and a plan to implement it, with a proposal expected at the 28th CGPM in 2026.
- A **negative leap second** — deleting a second rather than inserting one — is now a live possibility because Earth's rotation has been running fast; no operating system, protocol or timescale distribution has ever executed one in production.
- Leap seconds have been inserted 27 times since 1972 `[?]`, always announced in advance by IERS Bulletin C, and the insertion is a scheduled discontinuity in a monotonic quantity that most software assumes is monotonic.
- GNSS satellite clocks are deliberately offset in frequency before launch to compensate for the combined special- and general-relativistic effects in orbit, so relativity is a manufacturing parameter rather than a correction.
- Financial timestamping regimes require clock synchronisation to UTC within stated tolerances (MiFID II specifies 100 µs for high-frequency trading venues `[?]`), which turns a metrological chain into a regulatory compliance obligation with an audit trail.

## 3. Radiation, dose and stochastic harm

- Radiation carcinogenesis is stochastic: there is no radiogenic cancer distinguishable from any other cancer, the biology is identical, and the causation is not present in the individual case at all — only in the population rate.
- Microdosimetry makes this concrete: at low dose the energy arrives not smoothly but as discrete particle tracks through cell nuclei, and for 30 keV X-rays roughly one primary electron track per nucleus corresponds to about **0.75 rad**.
- At that mean, Poisson statistics give **36.8% of nuclei zero tracks, 36.8% exactly one, and 26.4% two or more** — halving the dose does not halve the damage to a hit nucleus, it halves the number of nuclei hit, so there is no gentle dose, only a smaller number of tickets.
- Radioactive decay is irreducibly stochastic, which is the one quantum-mechanical fact that reaches human bodies with no interpretive dispute attached and is the load-bearing physics under both dose reconstruction and radionuclide therapy.
- Under **EEOICPA**, a former nuclear worker with cancer has their lifetime occupational dose **reconstructed** by NIOSH from surviving records, co-worker data and site models, then evaluated for a Probability of Causation.
- **NIOSH-IREP** is the Monte Carlo radioepidemiological program that returns that figure; the statutory rule recommends compensation where the POC is **≥50% at the upper 99th percentile credibility limit** — not the central estimate but the top of the uncertainty distribution, explicitly to give the claimant the benefit of every doubt.
- IREP's inputs are cancer type, year of diagnosis, years and type of exposure, reconstructed dose, birth year, smoking history for lung cancer, and ethnicity for skin cancer.
- Where records are too poor to reconstruct a dose at all, a class of workers may be added to the **Special Exposure Cohort** and compensated presumptively — the worse the documentation, the more likely the payment.
- The risk models underlying IREP derive from the **Life Span Study** of the Hiroshima and Nagasaki survivors, which means the calibration standard for human radiation risk worldwide is a bombed population followed for eight decades.
- The administering bodies are the NIOSH Division of Compensation Analysis and Support, the Department of Labor's Office of Workers' Compensation Programs, and the Advisory Board on Radiation and Worker Health.
- The **ICRP Reference Person** is a defined hypothetical adult with specified organ masses and physiology, sex-averaged across a Reference Male and Reference Female; ICRP Publication 110's voxel phantoms are its computational realisation, and every dose limit and dose coefficient in the world is calculated for it and for no one who was actually exposed.
- The difference between the Reference Person and any individual is not an error in the system; it is the definition of the quantity — effective dose is a protection quantity for a reference body, not a measurement of a patient.
- Internal contamination is measured by **whole-body counting** in a shielded chamber, of which Lawrence Livermore's In Vivo Measurement Facility is one; the subject sits still while detectors count the gamma emissions of whatever is inside them.
- **Low-background steel**: every batch of steel smelted after 1945 carries cobalt-60 and other activation products, because steelmaking blows atmospheric air through the melt, so the most sensitive detectors have historically been shielded with steel salvaged from ships that sank before the first test — notably the German High Seas Fleet scuttled at Scapa Flow in 1919. The supply is finite and the wrecks are being salvaged.
- **Radium-223 dichloride** is a calcium mimetic: injected intravenously it complexes with hydroxyapatite at sites of high bone turnover, half-life **11.435 days**, with over **95% of the decay energy as alpha particles at 5.979 MeV** and a range in tissue of two to ten cell diameters, given as six injections at four-week intervals.
- **Lutetium-177 PSMA-617** works by the same logic with a beta emitter and a molecular targeting ligand, and the supply chains for both — and for actinium-225 — are federal-laboratory dependent and chronically constrained.
- Once injected there is no antidote and no way to stop the schedule: the clock is set by nuclear physics and by nothing else, and it irradiates marrow adjacent to the targeted bone, producing anaemia, lymphocytopenia, thrombocytopenia and neutropenia.
- Under **10 CFR 35.75** a patient is released when the projected dose to the most exposed other individual is under **5 mSv**, with written instructions required if that dose is likely to exceed **1 mSv**; measured dose rates at one metre of a couple of mR/hr are typical, and NRC **Regulatory Guide 8.39** is the implementation guidance.
- The instrument that leaves with the patient is a printed instruction card: how many days to sleep apart, what distance to keep from children and pregnant women, how to use the toilet, and what to do if they are hospitalised or die within the interval.
- The **Naval Radiological Defense Laboratory** operated at Hunters Point, San Francisco, from 1946 to 1969 as the Navy's centre for decontaminating ships from Operation Crossroads; one of those ships was packed with radioactive waste and scuttled off the Farallon Islands in January 1951.
- Between 1946 and 1970 the NRDL and Treasure Island disposed of roughly **47,000 drums** of radioactive waste about thirty miles west of the Golden Gate — the first and largest US offshore radiological dump — in a location that is now a national marine sanctuary.
- The NRDL conducted human experiments including administration of radioactive material by mouth, and irradiated large numbers of animals, some raised on a ranch in Contra Costa County.
- A patient designated **CAL-1** was injected at a San Francisco hospital on **14 May 1945 with 3.55 µCi of plutonium** under a mistaken cancer diagnosis, accumulated on the order of **64 Sv** over the twenty years he subsequently lived, and had his excreta collected for 340 days without being told why — the resulting excretion data underlie plutonium biokinetic models still in use.
- The first director of Livermore's Biomedical Research Division spent the rest of his career arguing that there is no threshold dose below which radiation carries no risk — the linear no-threshold model that his own institution's regulatory framework adopted.

## 4. Dating, the bomb pulse, and the body as archive

- Atmospheric nuclear testing between 1955 and 1963 roughly **doubled** the concentration of ¹⁴C in the atmosphere; since the Partial Test Ban Treaty the excess has declined at about **4% a year** as it exchanges into the oceans and biosphere, and is expected to reach pre-bomb levels around 2030.
- Carbon is fixed into a cell's genomic DNA when the cell is formed and is not subsequently exchanged, so the **¹⁴C content of genomic DNA records the calendar year the cell was born**, readable by accelerator mass spectrometry to a resolution of a couple of years.
- That method established which human tissues renew and which do not, dating adipocytes, cardiomyocytes, T cells, neurons and arterial collagen, and settling questions — including whether adult human hearts generate new myocytes — that conventional anatomy had failed to settle for a century.
- **Tooth enamel** is laid down at a known age and never remodelled, so a molar gives year of birth for an unidentified body to roughly **±1.5 years**, with a published casework guide (ANZPAA, 2022) and a systematic review of forensic applications (*Forensic Science International*, 2025).
- The **Center for Accelerator Mass Spectrometry at Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory** runs bioAMS on milligram tissue samples and is one of a handful of facilities worldwide capable of the biological measurement; the biology side of the field is anchored at Karolinska.
- The technique has an expiry date: fossil-fuel dilution (the **Suess effect**) is now driving atmospheric ¹⁴C below pre-bomb levels, so resolution is collapsing, and organic material formed today radiocarbon-dates as though it were centuries old.
- Lead is a bone-seeker: over **90%** of an adult's total body lead burden sits in the skeleton, substituting for calcium in hydroxyapatite, with trabecular bone turning over in years and cortical bone in decades.
- Blood lead reflects recent exposure and is a poor index of burden; **bone lead measured in vivo by K-shell X-ray fluorescence of the tibia or patella** is the cumulative dose record, validated against cadaver bone, and there are very few such instruments in the world.
- Whenever the skeleton is mobilised — pregnancy, lactation, menopause, immobilisation, osteoporosis — stored lead returns to blood; isotopic tracer work attributes a substantial fraction of maternal blood lead in pregnancy and lactation to skeletal release, and lead crosses the placenta freely.
- The consequence is a delivery schedule: an exposure absorbed in childhood is re-released decades later at the moment the body begins remodelling to build a second skeleton.
- There is no treatment that removes lead from bone: chelation clears blood rather than skeleton, and by mobilising the mineral phase it can raise blood lead rather than lower it, so the K-XRF measurement is an accurate quantification of something unactionable.
- The **CDC blood lead reference value** is currently **3.5 µg/dL**, defined as the 97.5th percentile of the NHANES distribution in young children — a value that falls whenever the population improves, so the threshold moves with the thing it measures.
- The EPA **Lead and Copper Rule** and its Improvements use a **90th-percentile** sampling design across a defined tap pool, which produces a compliant system-level statistic that is consistent with individual taps far above the action level.
- **NIOSH ABLES** is the occupational blood lead surveillance programme, and its case definition threshold has been lowered repeatedly as the evidence for low-level harm accumulated.

## 5. Calibration against human subjects

- Pulse oximetry has **no physical reference standard**: there is no artefact, no synthetic phantom and no traceable SI chain that can tell a manufacturer what an SpO₂ of 82% is.
- The relationship between the red/infrared absorbance ratio and arterial oxygen saturation is not derivable from first principles at low saturations, because the optics of perfused living tissue are not modellable to the required accuracy.
- The only way to establish the curve is empirical: place an indwelling catheter in the radial artery, lower the inspired oxygen fraction until arterial saturation falls to about 70%, and draw arterial blood at each plateau for measurement on a multi-wavelength CO-oximeter, then fit the device to those points.
- FDA's 2013 premarket guidance asked for **≥10 healthy adults, ≥200 paired SpO₂/SaO₂ observations spanning 70–100% SaO₂**, and "at least 2 darkly pigmented subjects or 15% of the subject pool, whichever is larger", with acceptable accuracy **A_RMS < 3.0%** for transmittance sensors.
- **Occult hypoxaemia** — arterial saturation below 88% while the oximeter reads a reassuring 92–96% — occurred in **11.7% of Black patients (88/749) versus 3.6% of white patients (99/2,778)** in a Michigan cohort, and **17.0% versus 6.2%** in a multicentre cohort of 37,308 paired readings (*New England Journal of Medicine*, 2020).
- FDA's 2025 revision asks for **480 paired datapoints from ≥24 participants**, stratified by **Monk Skin Tone** with ≥25% in each of light, medium and dark cohorts and ≥6 darkly pigmented participants, with **Individual Typology Angle** measured colorimetrically at the sensor site, and defines non-disparate performance as **<1.5% bias variation** across pigmentation when SaO₂ exceeds 85%.
- The session is a stepped desaturation: thirty to forty minutes on a closed breathing circuit through a series of plateaus to about 70% saturation — the range in which people become confused and then unconscious — with arterial blood drawn at each, over one to two hours.
- Compensation is about **$200**, and the arterial puncture site needs roughly **two weeks** to heal, which sets the minimum interval before the same volunteer can be booked again.
- The **UCSF Hypoxia Research Laboratory** in San Francisco is the world's principal facility of its kind: it tests more than 60 oximeters a year, charges roughly **$40,000** per regulatory study, and has run an eight-month backlog driven partly by consumer fitness wearables; its historical volunteer pool was drawn from inside the university.
- The applicable standards are FDA 510(k) guidance and **ISO 80601-2-61**, with the **OpenOximetry** programme publishing reference data and methods; the physical instrument is a closed breathing circuit, a radial arterial line and a CO-oximeter.
- Measurement is a physical intervention, classically and without any quantum mechanics: a diagnostic radiograph deposits dose in order to produce the image, an arterial blood gas removes blood from a person in order to characterise the blood still in them, and a biopsy destroys the tissue it characterises.
- The general form is that where no artefact standard exists, the standard is a person — and the accuracy of the resulting curve is bounded by who was recruited.

## 6. Thermodynamics and the cost of information

- **Landauer's principle**: erasing one bit of information in a system at temperature T dissipates at least **k_B T ln 2**, which is **2.805 zJ (0.0175 eV) at 293.15 K** — a lower bound set by the second law, not by any engineering limitation.
- The bound applies to *erasure* and other logically irreversible operations, not to computation as such: logically reversible operations have no thermodynamic floor, which is the foundational result of reversible-computing research.
- The principle was measured directly with a single colloidal particle in a double-well optical trap (*Nature* 483, 187, 2012), and subsequently in nanomagnetic memory bits at around **4.2 zJ** per erasure (2016).
- **Maxwell's demon** is resolved by the same accounting: the demon must erase its own memory to operate in a cycle, and that erasure repays exactly the entropy the sorting extracted — the bookkeeping balances only when the observer's record is included in the system.
- **Szilard engines** — single-particle engines extracting k_B T ln 2 of work per bit of information — have been realised experimentally with single electrons and single colloidal particles, so the information-to-work conversion is a laboratory fact rather than a thought experiment.
- The scale check matters: biological neural signalling operates four to five orders of magnitude above the Landauer bound, so nothing in a brain is anywhere near thermodynamically limited and the heat of any biological "erasure" is far below the metabolic noise floor.
- The same holds for computing hardware: real CMOS switching energies sit orders of magnitude above k_B T ln 2, so the Landauer bound is not the binding constraint on data centre power and will not become one at any projected process node `[?]`.
- The measurement framework is the fluctuation-theorem family — non-equilibrium work relations connecting free-energy differences to distributions of measured work — which is what makes single-particle energy accounting quantitative rather than statistical-in-the-aggregate `[?]`.
- The durable, correct export of this area is the accounting rule rather than the energy: an observer who intervenes and keeps a record has costs that fall somewhere, and the cost appears at erasure rather than at observation.

## 7. Quantum limits on measurement and signalling

- Environmental **decoherence** is well-measured and quantitatively excellent: it explains why interference terms vanish from the reduced density matrix of a system coupled to an environment.
- It does not explain why a single definite outcome occurs: it converts a coherent superposition into an **improper mixture**, and the step from improper mixture to one thing having actually happened is exactly what remains unresolved — the standard review literature is explicit that decoherence does not solve the measurement problem.
- Joos–Zeh estimates give the scale: a **10 µm dust grain** superposed over a micrometre decoheres in of order **10⁻³⁶ s** in air at normal pressure, about **10⁻²³ s** in a good laboratory vacuum, and still about **10⁻⁶ s** in intergalactic space against nothing but cosmic background photons.
- A neuron is far larger, warmer and wetter than a dust grain, so there is no regime in which coherence survives long enough or at large enough scale to have any effect on a person; maintaining it would require a separate coherence-preserving mechanism, which does not exist.
- The **quantum Zeno effect** is real and measured (*Phys. Rev. A* 41, 2295, 1990), but the effect demonstrated is suppression of a **coherent Rabi oscillation** in a two-level system, not suppression of the exponential decay of an unstable state — a distinction that produced a published Comment and Reply at the time (*Phys. Rev. A* 43, 5165 and 5168) and has not gone away.
- There is also an **anti-Zeno regime**, in which frequent measurement *accelerates* decay rather than suppressing it, so "watching a thing stops it" is not a general fact about the world even inside the effect's own domain.
- The **no-communication theorem** is a theorem, not an engineering limitation: the reduced density matrix on one side of an entangled pair is unchanged by any operation performed on the other, so no local measurement alters any observable statistic anywhere else.
- The consequence is structural: entanglement can distribute correlation but cannot transmit a signal, and any account in which measuring one particle does something to a distant body is inconsistent with the formalism it claims to use.
- The single quantum fact that reaches bodies without any interpretive commitment is that **radioactive decay is irreducibly stochastic**, so an individual case is unattributable in principle and only the population rate exists — which is what makes probability of causation a legal construct rather than a physical measurement.

## 8. Records, media and format decay

- **OAIS (ISO 14721)** is the reference model for an archival information system, and **ISO 16363** the standard against which a trustworthy digital repository is audited and certified; TRAC was its predecessor checklist.
- **Fixity** is the practice of storing a checksum and re-computing it on a schedule: it detects corruption and does not repair it, and where no fixity regime exists corruption is undetectable rather than absent.
- **LOCKSS** implements preservation by many independent copies that poll each other and repair from the majority, so the integrity guarantee is a distributed voting protocol rather than a property of any medium.
- Disk corruption is measured: a study of **1.53 million drives over 41 months** recorded more than **400,000 checksum mismatches**, with **0.86% of nearline drives (3,088 of 358,000)** and **0.065% of enterprise drives (767 of 1.17 million)** developing at least one (FAST '08).
- **Latent sector errors** are the characteristic failure: a sector is intact until it is read, and archival sectors are read rarely, so the error is discovered at restore time — that is, at the moment the copy is needed.
- Magnetic tape's binding constraint is not the medium but the drive: LTO drives read two generations back and write one generation back, so a perfectly readable cartridge becomes unreadable when a drive generation is retired, on the manufacturer's schedule rather than on a decay curve.
- Quoted LTO archival life is **15–30 years** under controlled temperature and humidity; older tape formulations suffer binder hydrolysis ("sticky shed"), which is chemically reversible by baking and is a one-time recovery rather than a repair.
- **Format obsolescence** is the failure in which the bits survive and the reader does not; the two remedies are migration (rewrite the object into a current format, accepting loss at each step) and emulation (preserve the original environment), and both are recurring costs rather than one-time acts.
- The **Rosetta Project** (Long Now Foundation, San Francisco) addresses this by removing the reader entirely: parallel texts in over a thousand languages micro-etched onto a nickel disk, readable by optical microscope, with the first pages scaling down visibly to instruct the reader how to read the rest.
- The **Internet Archive**, a San Francisco non-profit, holds what is in many cases the only surviving copy of a web resource, which makes a large part of the public record's persistence contingent on a single private organisation's operating budget `[?]`.
- Preservation is an operating expense, not a storage decision: the failure mode of a digital archive is a funding lapse, and the physical media typically outlast the institution's ability to keep migrating them.

## 9. Error, corruption and silent failure

- **ECC memory** in its common SECDED form corrects any single-bit error in a word and detects any double-bit error; beyond that the code is silent and returns a wrong answer that carries no indication of being wrong.
- The structural property of any error-correcting code is that it **conceals the underlying error rate**: the system reads as perfect right up to the point where errors exceed the code's capacity, and then fails across the board rather than gradually.
- Correction therefore removes the observability of degradation, which is why fleet operators instrument corrected-error counts and not just uncorrected ones — the raw rate is the only leading indicator.
- **Mercurial cores** are individual CPUs in large fleets that compute wrong answers intermittently, deterministically for particular inputs, and undetectably: Google's 2021 report puts the incidence at "a few mercurial cores per several thousand machines" and withholds exact corrupt-execution-error rates.
- Meta's 2021 disclosure describes the same phenomenon at a scale of hundreds of thousands of machines, with a documented case of a single core computing certain power operations incorrectly for particular operands.
- These faults are core-, temperature-, frequency- and age-dependent, appear after deployment rather than at manufacture, and defeat both burn-in and periodic self-test because they are input-specific.
- Detection requires either redundant execution on independent hardware or application-level checksums, and both cost throughput — so the amount of silent corruption a fleet tolerates is a budget decision with a stated price.
- The **Patriot battery at Dhahran, 25 February 1991**: a 24-bit truncation of the constant 1/10 in the system's timing accumulated **0.34 seconds** of clock drift over a hundred hours of continuous operation, the tracking gate looked in the wrong place, and **28 people died** — a failure entirely inside the arithmetic, with a body count and no component defect.
- The general shape is that a wrong answer that is plausible is more dangerous than a crash, because every downstream consumer is designed to accept a plausible answer.

## 10. Cryptography and the long horizon

- **SHA-1** fell to a practical collision in 2017 and to a **chosen-prefix collision** in 2020, and remains deployed in legacy certificate chains, version control object identifiers and document signing formats.
- Collision resistance is a property of the function rather than of any document: once a colliding pair exists, a signature computed over one member is a valid signature over the other, which makes every past signature in that algorithm retroactively weaker.
- **Harvest-now-decrypt-later** is the observation that ciphertext captured today is decryptable whenever the capability arrives, so the confidentiality lifetime of anything transmitted now is set by the adversary's storage budget and patience, not by current cryptanalysis.
- NIST published the first post-quantum standards in August 2024: **FIPS 203 (ML-KEM)**, **FIPS 204 (ML-DSA)** and **FIPS 205 (SLH-DSA)**.
- **NIST IR 8547** sets the transition schedule: RSA and elliptic-curve algorithms at the 112-bit security level are **deprecated after 2030** and **disallowed after 2035**, meaning they will no longer be approved in FIPS and NIST guidelines.
- The genomic asymmetry is the sharpest case: a genome cannot be rotated the way a key can, is already deposited in biobanks and consumer databases, discloses information about relatives who never consented, and will be the same plaintext for the subject's entire life and beyond it.
- **Dual_EC_DRBG** was a deterministic random bit generator standardised in NIST SP 800-90A whose construction permitted a trapdoor available to whoever chose its curve points; it was removed from the standard in the 2014 revision, and the episode is the reference case for how a standardisation process is itself part of the threat model.
- The **Debian OpenSSL defect (2006–2008)** removed a source of entropy from the seeding path, reducing the effective key space to the process ID — roughly **32,768** possibilities — so keys generated over about two years were enumerable, and were valid, well-formed and indistinguishable from good keys.
- Random number generator failure is silent by construction: the output of a broken generator passes every downstream check, because nothing downstream can distinguish a low-entropy stream from a high-entropy one without knowing the seed.
- Allocation concealment in randomised controlled trials depends on an unpredictable sequence; a predictable generator breaks blinding and determines which arm a patient is assigned to, which makes an RNG defect a clinical exposure rather than an information-security one.

## 11. Numerical models and their known divergence

- **IEEE 754** binary floating point cannot represent 0.1 exactly, so decimal constants acquire a representation error at entry and every subsequent operation propagates it; the resulting drift is deterministic, reproducible and invisible in the output.
- **Catastrophic cancellation** destroys significant digits when nearly equal quantities are subtracted, and the result is not flagged: the answer has fewer correct digits than its representation implies, with no indication of how many.
- Floating-point addition is not associative, so summation order changes the sum, and a parallel reduction is not bit-reproducible across different thread counts or machine configurations — reproducibility must be engineered rather than assumed.
- Deterministic atmospheric predictability is capped at roughly **two weeks** by finite-error growth in a chaotic system, which is a property of the atmosphere rather than of any model, so improving the model does not extend the limit.
- **Ensemble forecasting** responds by running perturbed initial conditions and reporting the spread, so the honest product is a distribution while the operational consumer generally wants a single number, and the reduction from one to the other is a documented but rarely visible judgement.
- The **CMIP6 "hot model" problem**: a subset of participating models have equilibrium climate sensitivity above the assessed range, so the raw multi-model mean runs warm and cannot be used directly.
- IPCC AR6 assessed ECS as **likely 2.5–4 °C with a best estimate of 3 °C**, derived from multiple lines of evidence, and screened or weighted models against it rather than averaging the ensemble — which means the published projection is a curated object and the curation is itself a documented expert judgement.
- **Structural uncertainty** — uncertainty in the form of the model rather than in its parameters — is not captured by an ensemble spread, so the reported uncertainty interval is systematically narrower than the real one and is known to be.
- A model tuned to reproduce a historical period cannot be validated on that period, so calibration and validation compete for the same data, and the split between them is a methodological choice with no external referee.
- In radiotherapy planning the model is a model **of a person**: there is no downstream measurement to compare it against, so a systematic error in the model is indistinguishable at every point in the process from correct output.
- Every link in a traceability chain can certify correctly while the composed system is wrong, because the chain certifies transfers of a standard and the last step is not a transfer — the audit's integrity is exactly what guarantees no one upstream can see past the end of it.
