# MECH-BIO — ecology, evolution, epidemiology, the biosphere

*Domain survey for the generative playbook. Candidates assessed against A (a body in it) · B (duration, not event) · C (an engine) · D (a turn that is real, audited and good) · E (an administering institution) · F (setting-a matrix). Premise-seed level only: what the situation is, never how anything is produced.*

**Read this first — the constraint, and how it was applied.**

§0 is firm that the horror is never a population. This domain is the one where that fails most easily, because its natural sentence is *a disease is spreading through people*. Every candidate below has therefore been written so that the frightening thing is a **process or an apparatus**: an action level, a quarantine calendar, a seed vault at −18 °C, a hysteresis loop, an inspection station, a captive-breeding studbook. Where material touches communities that institutions have already harmed — the tribal shellfish-harvesting cohorts in §2, the children given cadaveric growth hormone in §4 — the villain named is the standard, the programme and the interval, never the exposed. Two candidates were **rejected outright on this ground alone** (see Rejects R1 and R2), and one was rewritten twice before it would hold.

Second constraint applied: **the slate has already spent parts of this domain.** Story 18 runs on xenotransplantation and endogenous retroviruses; story 20 runs on stool banking, FMT donor screening, colistin/AMR and phage-borne toxin genes. Those are not available and are listed in the rejects with that reason. "The screening programme that returns a correct negative" is likewise used three times already (stories 8, 18, 20) and should not be the turn for anything new here.

# 1. THE STRONG CANDIDATES

## C1 — The barren that will not come back, and the animals that wait in it

**What it actually is.** Between 2013 and 2016 the sunflower sea star *Pycnopodia helianthoides* was killed across its range by sea star wasting disease — roughly **six billion animals, about 95% of the global population**, functionally extinct through most of California. The causative agent was only identified in 2025: *Vibrio pectenicida* strain FHCF-3 ([Nature Ecology & Evolution, 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41559-025-02797-2)). With its main predator gone and a marine heatwave running concurrently, purple urchins increased on the order of **10,000%** and **95–96% of the bull kelp canopy along ~350 miles of the north coast** was lost ([Rogers-Bennett & Catton, *Sci Rep* 2019](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-019-51114-y); [Mongabay, 2025](https://news.mongabay.com/2025/09/post-blob-californias-kelp-crisis-isnt-going-away/)). A decade later it has not come back. Barrens sit at roughly **120 urchins/m²**; kelp recovery needs about **2/m²**.

The hysteresis is not metaphorical. Starved purple urchins enter **metabolic depression rather than dying** — the literature's own word for them is *zombies* ([Rogers-Bennett et al., "The zombification and reanimation of purple urchins", *J Exp Mar Biol Ecol* 2021](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0022098121001362)) — and can hold that state for years, with lifespans of 20–40 years or more, reanimating the moment algae reappear. So the barren defends itself: any kelp that recruits is eaten by animals that have been waiting, empty, in place, since before the recruitment. Removing the pressure does not restore the system. Southern California's best-resourced clearance effort took **six million urchins removed over a decade, 80 acres, and over $4 million** — one acre costing three years of volunteer diving and 150,000 urchins.

**What it does to a body.** Urchin gonad atrophies to nothing; the animal persists as a grazing test with no reproductive tissue and near-zero metabolism, indefinitely. Red abalone starved out of a fishery closed since 2018. And the *Pycnopodia* death itself is the image: a twenty-armed animal that goes limp, twists, drops arms that crawl away independently, and dissolves — the arms outliving the animal by days.

**The engine.** A two-state system with a threshold, in which the losing state is maintained by animals that have stopped living without dying. Grazing pressure is decoupled from grazer condition. It would run identically if every agency vanished.

**The turn.** The captive-breeding-for-disease-resistance programme for the sunflower star: California Academy of Sciences, the Sunflower Star Laboratory at Moss Landing, The Nature Conservancy and the Shellfish Health Lab at Bodega Marine Laboratory have raised juveniles from spawn, run caged ocean "soaks" in Monterey Bay in 2025, and the Ocean Protection Council approved **over $500,000 in September 2025** for reintroduction ([California OPC](https://opc.ca.gov/2026/03/bringing-back-the-stars-californias-effort-to-rescue-the-sunflower-sea-star/)). It is genuinely good, genuinely audited, and genuinely working — an eDNA monitoring tool now exists to track it. That is the thing to make the mechanism: the deliberate breeding of a *resistant* apex predator, released on purpose, at density, with a studbook.

**The institution.** CDFW (with its Bodega Shellfish Health Lab), the California Ocean Protection Council, NOAA Greater Farallones and Monterey Bay National Marine Sanctuaries, the Sunflower Star Laboratory, the Noyo Center for Marine Science, Reef Check California's volunteer divers.

**setting-a matrix.** Structural, not scenery: Bodega Marine Laboratory, Moss Landing, the Farallones sanctuary, the Sonoma–Mendocino coast, the closed north-coast abalone fishery, and a volunteer diver corps that counts urchins on a schedule.

**Premise seed.** *A state programme breeds and releases a disease-resistant apex predator into a coastline that has been an urchin barren for a generation, and the release works exactly as designed on animals that had stopped being alive without having died.*

## C2 — Below the action level

**What it actually is.** Domoic acid is a neurotoxin produced by the diatom *Pseudo-nitzschia*, structurally a glutamate analogue and up to **100× more potent than glutamate** at kainate receptors. It concentrates in the hippocampus — CA1, CA3, CA4 and the dentate gyrus. The 1987 Prince Edward Island outbreak produced **over 100 cases, 19 hospitalisations, 3–4 deaths, and permanent anterograde amnesia in roughly a quarter of those affected** — the reason the syndrome is called amnesic shellfish poisoning. It arrived on this coast in 1991 in Monterey Bay.

California's answer is a monitoring apparatus that works: a **regulatory action level of 20 ppm in shellfish meat**, an **annual sport-mussel quarantine from 1 May to 31 October**, sentinel-mussel and phytoplankton networks, and fishery-wide crab closures. Since domoic-acid monitoring began in 1991, **no human illness from domoic acid in California-harvested shellfish has been reported** ([CDPH, *Natural Marine Toxins*](https://www.cdph.ca.gov/Programs/CEH/DRSEM/CDPH%20Document%20Library/EMB/Shellfish/Natural_Marine_Toxins_2018.pdf)). By contrast, the same programme's PSP record — 582 illnesses and 39 deaths since 1903 — is what the calendar was built out of.

The thing under it: the action level protects against **acute** poisoning. The CoASTAL cohort study of tribal razor-clam harvesters found **memory deficits associated with repeated exposure below the regulatory threshold**, prompting a proposed tolerable daily intake of 0.003 mg/kg/day ([Krasner et al., 2025, *Marine Drugs*, hosted by The Marine Mammal Center](https://www.marinemammalcenter.org/storage/app/media/Misc/PDF/Research%20Publications/2025/Krasner_et_al_2025_The%20Toxic%20Effects%20of%20Environmental%20DA%20Exposure%20on%20Humans%20and%20Marine%20Wildlife_MD.pdf)). And the sentinel species is doing it in public: California sea lions with chronic exposure develop **hippocampal atrophy with ventricular enlargement, spatial-memory failure, behavioural inflexibility and spontaneous progressive epilepsy**, and the toxin bioaccumulates in amniotic fluid and milk, so a cohort is exposed before birth.

**What it does to a body.** It takes the organ that makes a place navigable and a day retrievable, and it takes it in increments, from people who are eating correctly, from a resource that has been tested and passed. There is no illness to point at, no threshold crossed, no positive result. The person is simply a little less able to find their way back each year, and the standard says the food was safe, and the standard is correct about what it measures.

**The engine.** A bloom-driven excitotoxin operating on the hippocampus of every vertebrate that eats filter feeders, at a dose the regulatory instrument is not built to see, on a seasonal clock. Mechanically describable, running now, independent of anyone.

**The turn.** The CDPH Marine Biotoxin Monitoring Program itself: a genuine, audited, unambiguous public-health victory — decades of quarantine orders, volunteer sentinel sites and crab-season delays with a clean human record. Make **the quarantine calendar** the mechanism. The 1 May–31 October window is a real boundary that has saved real lives, and it is drawn in the wrong dimension for what the story is about.

**The institution.** CDPH Environmental Management Branch (Marine Biotoxin Monitoring Program), CDFW, the Fish and Game Commission's crab-season authority, NOAA/NCCOS, California Sea Grant, The Marine Mammal Center in Sausalito — which diagnosed the first marine-mammal case in 1998 and alerts public-health officials.

**setting-a matrix.** Structural. The Marine Mammal Center in the Marin Headlands; the Half Moon Bay and Bodega Bay crab fleets and the delayed openers; the county environmental-health officers who post the quarantine; Tomales Bay and Drakes Estero shellfish leases; Monterey Bay, where the toxin was first found on this coast.

**Premise seed.** *A monitoring programme with a spotless forty-year record of preventing poisoning goes on issuing correct clearances, while the thing it is not measuring removes, one season at a time, the faculty that would let anyone notice a pattern in their own life.*

**Handling note.** The horror is the action level and the calendar. The harvesters are competent, correct and doing the right thing; the tribal cohort material must be written as *what the standard was never designed to protect*, with the apparatus as the villain, per §0.

## C3 — The thermal barrier, and what it was always accidentally doing

**What it actually is.** There are on the order of millions of fungal species and only a few hundred that cause human disease. The best explanation for that ratio is **mammalian endothermy**: our body temperature sits above the growth ceiling of the overwhelming majority of environmental fungi, and that thermal exclusion zone — not immunity, not medicine — is the main reason mammals do not live the way amphibians and bats now do. Casadevall's global-warming emergence hypothesis argues that *Candida auris*, which appeared as three phylogenetically distinct clades on three continents essentially simultaneously in 2011–12 and grows readily at 37 °C and 42 °C, is the first pathogen plausibly produced by the barrier thinning ([Casadevall, Kontoyiannis & Robert, *mBio* 2021](https://journals.asm.org/doi/10.1128/mbio.00360-21)). The hypothesis is contested and should be held as contested — a 2024 review argues climate is not yet supercharging fungal pathogens ([PubMed 38341269](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38341269/)) — but the *barrier* itself is not contested at all.

What the barrier's absence looks like in animals that do not have it is documented at population scale. Chytrid (*Batrachochytrium dendrobatidis*) has caused declines in **501 amphibian species and the presumed extinction of 90** — "the greatest documented loss of biodiversity attributable to a disease" ([Scheele et al., *Science* 2019](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.aav0379)). White-nose syndrome, caused by a fungus that grows on hibernating bats precisely because torpor drops their body temperature into its range, has spread to **more than 40 US states and eight Canadian provinces** since 2006. And human fungal disease is now estimated at **~6.5 million invasive infections and ~3.75 million deaths a year**, up from prior estimates of 1.5–1.7 million ([Denning, *Lancet Infect Dis* 2024](https://fungalinfectiontrust.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Denning-Incidence-and-mortality-of-severe-fungal-diseases-Lancet-Infect-Dis-2024.pdf)).

**What it does to a body.** The register here is not an infection scene, it is a **temperature**. §3.15: *the room is kept at a stated temperature for a stated reason, and the reason is the whole story.* The body horror is a thermometer and a growth-ceiling table, and the arithmetic between them narrowing. Downstream: a hibernating animal woken repeatedly by an irritant on its face until it burns the fat it needed for March; a person whose only protection was that they run hot.

**The engine.** A physical exclusion — a two-degree margin between an organism's ceiling and a mammal's core — that is not a defence anyone built, is not maintained by anyone, and is closing. This is the domain's purest instance of §0's *the safety was always accidental and has run out*.

**The turn.** Two, both real and both audited. (i) **Endothermy itself** — the most successful anti-infective in the vertebrate record, never designed, never audited, and expiring. (ii) The **white-nose interventions that work**: a bacterial probiotic that raised bat survival from **8.4% to 46.2%** in Wisconsin trials, an oral raccoonpox-vectored vaccine with no mortality or clinical disease observed at Idaho's Minnetonka Cave as of March 2024, UV-C sterilisation and passive mine-cooling ([USFWS](https://www.fws.gov/story/preventing-and-treating-white-nose-syndrome)). Note what the cooling intervention is: the response to a thermal-barrier failure is **to make the room colder**. (iii) And the cleanest one available anywhere in this file: **reintroduction of chytrid-resistant frogs**. Over 15 years, 24 translocations to 12 sites in Yosemite using frogs from populations that had evolved resistance; 9 of 12 reintroduced populations reproduced, with new adult recruitment in ~79% of post-translocation survey years ([Knapp et al., *Nat Commun* 2024](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-53608-4)). A species that had lost **over 90% of its range** is coming back because someone moved the survivors of the pathogen into the pathogen.

**The institution.** USFWS (national WNS response, ~150 partner organisations), USGS National Wildlife Health Center, NPS, CDFW, WHO (fungal priority pathogens list, 2022), CDC's antifungal-resistance surveillance.

**setting-a matrix.** Moderate but real: the Sierra frog work runs through UC Santa Barbara, UC Berkeley and the UC Natural Reserve System; CDFW's wildlife health lab; the California Academy of Sciences herpetology collection; and the setting-a's own hospital estate for the *auris* end — a nosocomial fungus is a story about a building's surfaces and its cleaning contract.

**Premise seed.** *A degrees-and-thresholds table maintained by no one has been the only thing standing between mammals and the largest kingdom of life, the margin is now inside the measurement error, and the working intervention is to make a room colder than the animals in it need it to be.*

## C4 — The interval, industrially

**What it actually is.** From the 1960s to 1985, national programmes in the US, UK, France and elsewhere treated children with pituitary dwarfism using growth hormone extracted from cadaver pituitaries collected at autopsy and pooled. It worked: the children grew. **Approximately 7,700 US patients** received it through the National Hormone and Pituitary Program. Some batches carried prions. **254 chGH-associated iatrogenic CJD cases worldwide as of January 2024**, 36 of them among US NHPP recipients; the longest documented latency in a US case is **48.3 years** ([CDC *EID*, June 2025](https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/eid/article/31/6/24-1519_article)); genetically determined incubation of up to 40 years is documented in the UK cohort ([*Brain* 2015](https://academic.oup.com/brain/article/138/11/3386/330626)).

Then it got worse in a way nobody had budgeted for. In 2024, eight recipients of cadaveric hGH were reported with early-onset Alzheimer's disease — five with dementia between **ages 38 and 55** — and archived batches were shown to contain measurable amyloid-β with seeding activity transmissible to mice ([Banerjee et al., *Nature Medicine* 2024](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-023-02729-2)). Not a prion disease. A second, different seeded proteinopathy riding the same vector, surfacing forty years later. (The authors state explicitly, and this must be preserved: there is no suggestion Aβ transmits in ordinary life.)

Underneath it is a population-scale silent-carriage figure. Britain's appendix surveys found abnormal prion protein at roughly **1 in 2,000** across the post-BSE cohort ([Gill et al., *BMJ* 2013](https://ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3805509); confirmed in [*Acta Neuropathologica* 2020](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00401-020-02153-7)) — a prevalence that has driven surgical-instrument policy and blood-donor deferral for two decades on the basis of an unknown number of people who will never be told anything.

**What it does to a body.** A child of eight is given a real medicine for a real deficiency, by a national programme, with parental consent, and it works — they reach an adult height they would not have reached. Four decades later, at forty-six, they cannot hold a sentence. There is no test that would have found it, no dose that was wrong, no one who was careless, and the physician who administered it is dead.

**The engine.** A pooled biological product distributing a self-templating protein through a treated cohort with a latency measured in decades. Population-scale, mechanically describable, and — crucially — **it continued to operate for thirty years after the last dose was given**, which is precisely §0's *already done, at scale, and not reversible*.

**The turn.** The growth hormone. A therapy that was good, that was correct, that was the right thing to give, administered by people doing their jobs well, with informed consent from parents who wanted it. §3.14 exists for exactly this: *the consent was given before the use existed*; *the lookback letter*; *it was done to you years ago and is only now becoming legible, and the person who did it has retired.* The secondary turn is the **lookback apparatus itself** — recipient notification, the UK CJD Incidents Panel, the surgical-instrument quarantine regime — a genuinely good, genuinely careful public-health machine whose only product for most of the people it contacts is a letter that changes nothing.

**The institution.** NIH / the National Hormone and Pituitary Program and its successor surveillance; the UK Health Security Agency and the CJD Incidents Panel; the National Prion Clinic; FDA's blood-donor deferral policy; hospital sterile-services departments running single-use instrument policy for a risk expressed as 1 in 2,000.

**setting-a matrix.** Adaptable rather than native: UCSF is where prions were characterised and where the Institute for Neurodegenerative Diseases sits — the single strongest institutional tie in this file to the mechanism itself. Also available: California's tissue and eye banks under CDPH licence, and hospital sterile processing.

**Premise seed.** *A national programme that made short children taller, correctly and kindly and with everyone's consent, is still delivering a second consignment forty years after it closed, and the only apparatus that exists to meet it is a letter.*

**Handling note.** The horror is the pooling, the interval and the notification apparatus. Nothing here may make dementia, short stature or the recipients frightening; the frightening thing is a programme that cannot be recalled because its product was already absorbed.

## C5 — Cancer as a species

**What it actually is.** In at least three lineages, cancer has stopped being a disease of an individual and become an **organism in its own right** — a clonal cell line transmitted between hosts as an infectious agent, carrying its own genome, older than any of the animals it lives in.

- **CTVT**, the canine transmissible venereal tumour: a single cell lineage that left the body of one dog roughly **11,000 years ago** and has been passing between dogs, as a cell, on every inhabited continent, ever since. It is the oldest continuously propagating cell line known.
- **DFT1 and DFT2** in Tasmanian devils: DFT1 first seen in 1996, DFT2 in 2014 — two independent transmissible cancers in one host species in twenty years, both spread by biting ([Transmissible Cancer Group, Cambridge](https://www.tcg.vet.cam.ac.uk/about/DFTD)). The species is now endangered.
- **Bivalve transmissible neoplasia**: contagious leukaemias in clams, mussels and cockles that travel **through open seawater** — cancer cells released by an infected animal and taken up by another. Multiple independent lineages, and at least one that has crossed *between species*. Pacific-coast prevalence figures: ~10% in South Skagit Bay soft-shell clams in 2022, **rising past 75% in Port Susan**, 5–10% in native basket cockles ([Salish Current, 2026](https://salish-current.org/2026/02/19/contagious-cancer-poses-possible-threat-to-salish-sea-clams/); [Metzger et al., *Nature* 2016](http://www.nature.com/nature/journal/v534/n7609/abs/nature18599.html)).

**What it does to a body.** The tumour is not yours. It has a different karyotype, a different genome, and a different birthday, and it is wearing you. A devil's face is replaced by an animal that has been alive since 1996 and is only using this jaw. A clam's haemolymph fills with cells that will outlive it in the water column. This satisfies §2.21 exactly: *a body human at every scale except organisation*, and *something that keeps what it consumed on the outside of itself.*

**The engine.** A cell lineage with a transmission route and no host of its own — immortal, unbounded by the death of any individual, and a genuine biological entity whose census is a population count. Water is the medium in the bivalve case, which means the vector is the environment.

**The turn.** The **insurance population**: the Save the Tasmanian Devil Program's captive metapopulation, studbook-managed against genetic drift, plus wild devils now showing evolved resistance and reduced tumour virulence ([Epstein et al., *Nat Commun* 2016](https://www.nature.com/articles/ncomms12684)). A textbook, audited, working conservation intervention — you take healthy animals out of the population, keep them under certificate, and put them back. The second turn is the **shellfish health certification apparatus**: seed transfers between growing areas are licensed on the basis of laboratory disease-free certificates, and it is a functioning biosecurity system.

**The institution.** For devils: the Save the Tasmanian Devil Program and the zoo studbook network. For bivalves: CDFW's Shellfish Health Lab at Bodega Marine Laboratory, CDPH's shellfish sanitation programme, the Pacific Northwest Research Institute, the Puget Sound Restoration Fund, and the tribal shellfish programmes.

**setting-a matrix.** Strong and specific. The soft-shell clam *Mya arenaria* — the species in which BTN was first identified — **entered the Pacific coast in San Francisco Bay in 1874**, accidentally, in shipments of Atlantic oysters that began in 1869, and by the 1880s was the dominant commercial clam in SF Bay markets ([The Exotics Guide](https://www.exoticsguide.org/mya_arenaria)). The Bay is the port of entry. Add Tomales Bay's growers, Bodega's health lab, and the certificate that moves seed between them.

**Premise seed.** *A licensing system that certifies shellfish seed as disease-free is doing exactly what it was designed to do, and the thing it is moving between growing areas under certificate is not a disease but an animal, older than the industry, that has been travelling in the water since before anyone thought to look.*

## C6 — The prion in the ground

**What it actually is.** Chronic wasting disease is a transmissible spongiform encephalopathy of deer, elk and moose. It has now been reported in **37 US states** plus Canada, Norway, Finland, Sweden and South Korea ([CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/chronic-wasting/data-research/index.html)), and it reached **California in 2024**, confirmed in deer in Inyo and Madera counties. Two features make it different from every other wildlife disease in this file: infectious prions are shed in saliva, urine and faeces by animals that look healthy, and they **persist in soil and on plants**, so the pasture stays infectious after every animal on it is dead. Culling does not clear the ground.

The species barrier is where the register lives. There is no confirmed human case. Macaque studies disagree with each other — one found no transmission to cynomolgus macaques ([*J Virol* 2018](https://journals.asm.org/doi/10.1128/jvi.00550-18)); other work has been read the other way. CDC's standing advice is that hunters should have animals tested before eating them, which is an institution saying, in public, *we do not know, please assume*. And BSE is the precedent everyone is holding: a barrier that was confidently asserted and then was not there.

**What it does to a body.** In cervids: months of progressive weight loss, ataxia, drooling, loss of fear, standing in the open. In people: nothing, so far, which is the point — the entire harm currently exists as an **interval that has not elapsed yet**, being managed by a testing programme whose result changes what you did last autumn.

**The engine.** A self-templating protein with an environmental reservoir and no organism to kill. It does not need a host to persist, it does not decay on the timescales anyone plans on, and it would go on doing this if every wildlife agency closed tomorrow.

**The turn.** The **hunter-submitted surveillance programme** — a genuinely good, genuinely voluntary, genuinely effective public-participation system in which thousands of people bring in the heads of animals they shot so that the state can map a disease. It works; it is the reason anyone knows where CWD is. CDFW publishes the positive detections as an open dataset ([CA Open Data ds3183](https://data.ca.gov/dataset/chronic-wasting-disease-positive-detections-2024-2025-cdfw-ds3183)). Make the surveillance the mechanism: the harvest is the sample, the hunter is the instrument, and the freezer full of meat waiting on a lab result is the room the story happens in.

**The institution.** CDFW's Wildlife Health Laboratory, USDA APHIS, USGS National Wildlife Health Center, state carcass-transport regulations and taxidermy rules, and the carcass-disposal contractors.

**setting-a matrix.** Weaker than most here, and honestly so: CDFW is a Sacramento institution, the detections are Inyo and Madera. What is available regionally is the **rendering and carcass-disposal chain**, the deer that live inside the setting-a's open-space districts, and the hunter check-station apparatus — plus, at a stretch, the same soil-persistence logic applied to an urban open-space preserve with a mandated public trail.

**Premise seed.** *A voluntary testing programme that thousands of people participate in correctly, every autumn, produces a map of ground that will still be infectious in fifty years, and the only thing the result changes is what a family already ate.*

## C7 — Committed

**What it actually is.** The two chemistry changes now running in the California Current arrive on the same upwelled water and neither is an event.

*Oxygen.* The ocean has lost about **5.0 Pmol O₂, 1.8% of pre-industrial inventory, since 1770**. The finding that matters for this register: even if emissions stopped instantly, the ocean is **committed to a total loss of 20.3 Pmol — 7.4% — by 2650**, i.e. **less than a quarter of the deoxygenation caused by historical emissions has happened yet**, and it continues for centuries after emissions cease ([Oschlies, *Nat Commun* 2021](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-021-22584-4)). Eighty per cent of it is below 2,000 m.

*What the end state looks like where it already exists.* On the Namibian shelf, organic carbon accumulates faster than it oxidises, sulphate reduction runs in the sediment, and hydrogen sulphide comes out of the seabed — by diffusion, by bubbling, and by methane dislodging chunks of sediment into floating islands, leaving craters. Water-column total H₂S exceeds **100 µM**; the oxidised sulphur turns the sea milky turquoise in patches **visible from orbit** ([Frontiers in Marine Science, 2018](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/marine-science/articles/10.3389/fmars.2018.00341/full); [NASA Earth Observatory](https://earthobservatory.nasa.gov/images/13155/hydrogen-sulfide-eruptions-along-the-coast-of-namibia)). Giant sulphur bacteria, *Thiomargarita namibiensis*, at **47 g/m² wet weight**, sit on top of it consuming the sulphide and are the only reason more of it does not reach the surface.

*Carbonate.* On this coast, upwelled water is already corrosive. **53% of onshore pteropods sampled in the California Current showed shell dissolution** ([Bednaršek et al., 2014](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4024287/)). In 2007–08 it killed larval oysters at Pacific Northwest hatcheries and nearly ended the industry.

**What it does to a body.** H₂S at the relevant concentrations paralyses cytochrome oxidase — cellular respiration stops in a body surrounded by oxygen — and above a threshold it also destroys the sense of smell, so the warning stops arriving before the exposure does. §2.20's *atmosphere that is a by-product: a stated tolerance in minutes, a colour, a depth* is not an invention; it is a shelf that exists. And the calcifying half: a shell dissolving on a living animal from the outside in.

**The engine.** Sediment chemistry and gas exchange operating over a shelf, on a schedule set by upwelling, with a bacterial mat as the only thing between it and the surface — plus an oxygen debt that is already incurred and pays out over six centuries.

**The turn.** The **Burke-o-Lator monitoring network**. When Pacific Northwest hatcheries collapsed, NOAA and Oregon State put real-time carbonate-chemistry instruments on the intake pipes so growers could time their water. It saved a multi-hundred-million-dollar industry, it is audited, it is unambiguously good, and it is one of the cleanest climate-adaptation success stories on the coast ([AAAS *How We Respond*](https://howwerespond.aaas.org/a-crippled-oregon-shellfish-hatchery-spawns-better-ocean-monitoring-systems.html)). **Hog Island Oyster Co. in Tomales Bay runs one.** Make the instrument the mechanism: a screen on a pipe that tells you, correctly, when it is safe to open the valve.

**The institution.** NOAA PMEL and the IOOS regional associations, the California Ocean Protection Council and the West Coast Ocean Acidification and Hypoxia Science Panel, CDPH's shellfish programme, the Regional Water Quality Control Boards.

**setting-a matrix.** Structural: Tomales Bay and Drakes Estero growers; Bodega Marine Laboratory; the Farallones and Cordell Bank sanctuaries where the upwelling arrives; the Golden Gate as the pump that puts corrosive water into the Bay on a tidal clock.

**Premise seed.** *An instrument on an intake pipe tells a shellfish grower, correctly and in real time, which hours of which days the water can be let in, and the answer has been getting shorter every year for a decade on a curve nobody in the room can bend.*

## C8 — Spillover is a hunger signal

**What it actually is.** A 25-year study of Hendra virus in subtropical Australia (1996–2020) reconstructed the whole causal chain and it is not about the virus. Habitat loss removed the native winter-flowering trees flying foxes depend on. After 2003, roost numbers **tripled** and the animals became permanently resident in agricultural landscapes. **Forty spillover events occurred after 2002; there had been none from 1996 to 2002.** Spillover clustered in winters following climate-driven food shortage, when nutritionally stressed bats shed more virus near horses. And the protective factor: where a winter nectar pulse in remnant native forest drew aggregations of **100,000+ bats away from farmland, spillover probability fell from 0.014 to effectively zero** ([Eby et al., *Nature* 2022](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-022-05506-2)).

**What it does to a body.** Hendra kills roughly three-quarters of the people it infects and most of the horses. But the body that matters for the premise is the **reservoir's**: a starving animal, in a suburban fig tree, whose hunger is the variable that moves the risk. The engine is legible as a nutritional state.

**The engine.** Land-use change → phenological failure → nutritional stress in a reservoir → altered shedding and altered roosting → contact. Every link measured, none of it requiring anyone's intent, all of it continuing if every health department closed.

**The turn.** **Ecological restoration as a pandemic-prevention intervention.** Planting winter-flowering trees is the audited, effective, unambiguously good thing — and it works by *taking the animals away from us*, which is to say the successful public-health intervention is a landscape that people are asked to make and then not use. That is a turn with a shape: the prevention measure is a permanent withdrawal from land.

**The institution.** Wildlife health and agriculture departments; in the US analogue: USDA APHIS Veterinary Services, CDC's One Health office, state vector-control districts.

**setting-a matrix.** Present but requires work: the setting-a's mosquito and vector control districts (Alameda County, San Mateo County, Marin/Sonoma) are **special districts with their own tax revenue, their own boards, and statutory entry powers** — and §5 of the slate notes the corpus has never once used a special district. Add the open-space districts (Midpen, EBRPD) whose land is the restoration, and the California Department of Food and Agriculture's landscape-scale planting authority.

**Premise seed.** *A special district with its own tax base buys land and plants it so that the animals will go there instead of here, the intervention is measured and works, and the covenant that makes it work forbids anybody from ever setting foot on it.*

## C9 — The bank, the bottleneck, and what comes out of the freezer

**What it actually is.** Ex-situ conservation is now a functioning industrial apparatus with a genuine, audited success record.

- **Svalbard Global Seed Vault**: **over 1.4 million seed samples** deposited, from ~70 deposit events and dozens of national genebanks, held below freezing in a mountain ([Crop Trust, 2025](https://www.croptrust.org/news-events/news/article/svalbard-global-seed-vault-crosses-major-milestone-14-million-seed-samples-secured)). It has been drawn on once in earnest — ICARDA withdrew and later re-deposited its collection when Aleppo became unusable.
- **The Frozen Zoo** (San Diego Zoo Wildlife Alliance): live cell cultures banked since the 1970s.
- **The black-footed ferret**: the entire living species descends from **seven individuals**. In 1988 tissue was banked from a female called Willa whose cells hold **three times the genetic diversity of the current population**. Willa has since been cloned three times — Elizabeth Ann, Noreen, Antonia — and in 2024 **Antonia bred with a wild-type male and produced two healthy kits**, the first offspring of a cloned US endangered species ([USFWS, Nov 2024](https://www.fws.gov/press-release/2024-11/advancements-black-footed-ferret-conservation-continue-new-offspring-cloned)).

**What it does to a body.** It puts a specified genetic content into a living animal that did not consent to being a copy and cannot be told. §2.21 has the shape already: *stock that degrades a little each generation with no baseline left to measure against*, and this is its inverse — a baseline that exists, in a freezer, as a number, against which every living animal can be scored and found short. The body is an animal born in 2024 that is, as a fact on a form, an animal from 1988.

**The engine.** A managed metapopulation running on a studbook, with a cold reservoir of superior alleles that can be issued into it, indefinitely, on a schedule set by drift. Population-scale, mechanically describable, and utterly independent of any individual's intent.

**The turn.** The ferret programme is the turn: unambiguously good, thoroughly audited, and the definitive rebuttal to everyone who said cloning was a distraction. Made the mechanism, it becomes *the animal issued from the bank to correct a deficiency in the living*, and then the question of what the criterion is, and who scores.

**The institution.** USFWS, the AZA studbook system and Species Survival Plans, San Diego Zoo Wildlife Alliance, ViaGen, the Crop Trust and NordGen at Svalbard, USDA-ARS's National Laboratory for Genetic Resources Preservation and the National Clonal Germplasm Repository at Davis, and California Plant Rescue.

**setting-a matrix.** Strong and under-used. **Revive & Restore is headquartered in Sausalito** and runs the USFWS biobanking partnership and the ferret and Przewalski's horse cloning work from there — a small nonprofit on the north shore of the Bay holding the genetic reserve of American endangered species. Add the UC Botanical Garden at Berkeley, the USDA-ARS repository at Davis, and the setting-a's own conservation-genomics capacity (California Academy of Sciences, UCSF, the gene-editing institutions at Berkeley and Brisbane).

**Premise seed.** *A small office by the water holds the only copies of a species that are better than the species, and the recovery plan requires them to be issued into living animals on a schedule, against a scoring rubric that says how much of what is currently alive is surplus.*

## C10 — The colony with no edges

**What it actually is.** Argentine ants (*Linepithema humile*) in their native range live in ordinary, mutually hostile colonies. In California they do not. A **single supercolony extends from San Diego to Ukiah — the length of the state** — in which ants from either end recognise each other as nestmates and do not fight ([UC Irvine / Tsutsui & Shea](https://www.chem.uci.edu/files/Argentine_ants_release.html)). The recognition system is chemical: methyl-branched cuticular hydrocarbons read on contact. The introduced population passed through a genetic bottleneck that **flattened the diversity of those recognition cues**, and the loss of the ability to tell each other apart is precisely what made the colony continental — no territorial boundaries, no intraspecific war, so all the energy goes into expansion. Related unicolonial populations in Europe and Japan have been argued to belong to the same globally distributed entity ([Sunamura et al., 2009](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/230000446_ORIGINAL_ARTICLE_The_global_expansion_of_a_single_ant_supercolony)).

**What it does to a body.** The bodies here are the displaced: Argentine ants remove native ant faunas wholesale and, through them, the horned lizards and other specialists that ate them. And the register's real image is the ants themselves — hundreds of millions of individuals with no self and no other, in which the individual body is not the unit of anything.

**The engine.** A recognition system degraded to the point of universal acceptance, producing a single organism the size of a state. §2.1 already reaches for this — *a self that survives dispersal and scales with it* — and here it is, real, and the mechanism is **the loss of the faculty that would have distinguished one from another**, which is §0's *invisible from inside* in an insect.

**The turn.** The **San Francisco Estuary Invasive Spartina Project**: invasive hybrid cordgrass reduced from **805 acres to under 38 acres across a 70,000-acre project area — about 95% — since treatment began in 2005**, with **450,000+ native plants** put back and endangered Ridgway's rail habitat restored ([Cal-IPC](https://www.cal-ipc.org/project/invasive-spartina-eradication/)). One of the most successful large-scale invasive-plant eradications anywhere, run out of the Coastal Conservancy. It is genuinely good, genuinely audited, and it works by **identifying, at scale, which individuals are the wrong kind and removing them** — which is exactly the operation the ant has stopped being able to perform.

**The institution.** California State Coastal Conservancy, USFWS, CDFW, Santa Clara Valley Water District, county agricultural commissioners, CDFA's Pest Exclusion Branch — which inspects **nearly 20 million vehicles a year** at the border protection stations ([CDFA](https://www.cdfa.ca.gov/plant/PE/ExteriorExclusion/borders_faq.html)).

**setting-a matrix.** Very strong. The Bay is the world's most heavily invaded estuary; the Spartina project is a Bay institution; and the CDFA inspection apparatus is a real, physical, staffed border around California that most residents have driven through without noticing.

**Premise seed.** *An eradication programme that has spent twenty years correctly telling one kind of individual from another, at 95% success across seventy thousand acres, meets a population that lost the ability to make that distinction and became one animal the length of the state because of it.*

## C11 — Restoring to a baseline that never existed

**What it actually is.** San Francisco Bay has lost the great majority of its historic tidal marsh, and what replaced the native community is a novel assemblage: the Bay is routinely described as the most invaded estuary in the world, with a nonindigenous fauna that arrived in oyster shipments, ballast water and hull fouling from 1869 onward — the soft-shell clam in 1874, and a great deal since. The **South Bay Salt Pond Restoration Project** is the largest tidal-wetland restoration on the West Coast: **15,100 acres acquired in 2003 for $100 million**, a **50-year adaptive-management programme**, and **3,770 of a targeted 7,500 acres restored to date** ([South Bay Salt Ponds](https://www.southbayrestoration.org/page/our-progress)).

Sitting under it: **New Almaden**, one of the largest mercury mines in the northern hemisphere, worked from 1845, which supplied the quicksilver for the Gold Rush and left the Guadalupe watershed the setting-a's primary mercury hotspot — around **90% of the mercury load there is mining-derived**. Opening ponds to the tide erodes sloughs and can remobilise that legacy mercury into a system that methylates it. The programme's answer is monitoring and gates: Pond A8's structure has eight movable gates, closed December through May, and tern-egg mercury actually **fell 60% from 2011 to 2013** with no attributable increase in slough fish ([Estuary News](https://archive.estuarynews.org/estuary-news-freeingfishandlockingupmercury/)). The apparatus is competent and the monitoring is honest, which is what makes it usable here.

And over all of it, **shifting baselines** (Pauly, 1995): each generation of scientists takes the state of the system at the start of its own career as the reference condition, so the target itself walks. What the restoration is restoring *to* was itself already degraded, already invaded, and was chosen by people whose memory of the Bay started in the 1970s.

**What it does to a body.** Methylmercury in a food web is a body horror at the scale of a bird's egg and a subsistence angler's dinner. The clinical picture is unambiguous and the exposure route runs through the thing everyone agrees is good.

**The engine.** A restoration programme that, by doing the correct thing — letting water back in — moves a nineteenth-century industrial inventory out of the sediment and into living tissue, on a fifty-year clock, governed by an adaptive-management framework that will outlive everyone who wrote it.

**The turn.** The restoration itself. Fifteen thousand acres, four agencies, half a century, real birds, real marsh, real flood protection, unambiguously good. §1 step 3 wants exactly this: *take a real thing that works and make it the mechanism.* Here the good thing is the water, and the water is the vector.

**The institution.** CDFW, USFWS (Don Edwards San Francisco Bay National Wildlife Refuge), the State Coastal Conservancy, the Santa Clara Valley Water District, the SF Bay Regional Water Quality Control Board and its mercury TMDL, BCDC, and the San Francisco Bay Restoration Authority — which is funded by **Measure AA, a parcel tax the region voted for itself**.

**setting-a matrix.** Total. This candidate is the setting-a.

**Premise seed.** *The region taxes itself to open fifteen thousand acres of salt pond back to the tide, the marsh comes back exactly as promised, and what the water carries out of the ground is an inventory laid down in 1845 by the mine that made the Gold Rush possible.*

# 2. THE REJECTS

Rejected freely, and several of these are strong science.

**R1. "Non-communicable disease is transmissible via the microbiome."** The finding is real and startling — ~50% strain sharing mother-to-infant in year one, 12% between cohabiting adults' gut microbiomes, 32% oral, strains persisting six months, and the authors explicitly raise transmissibility for microbiome-associated *non-infectious* disease ([Valles-Colomer et al., *Nature* 2023](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-022-05620-1)). **Rejected on the §0 constraint.** Every premise it generates makes an illness or a body type the contagion, and the only apparatus it suggests is one that separates people on the grounds of what lives in them. There is no version of it where the villain is the apparatus rather than the ill. Also **spent**: story 20 already runs on stool banking and FMT donor screening.

**R2. "Toxoplasma changes human personality/behaviour."** The rodent work is solid and the parasite-produced-dopamine mechanism is now published ([*Nat Commun* 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-66139-3)). The human behavioural literature is not: the Dunedin population-representative birth cohort found essentially null associations across brain and behaviour measures ([*PLOS One* 2016](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0148435)). **Fails D** (the claim is in doubt, so nothing about it is "not in doubt") and **fails the §0 constraint** (it locates the horror in the infected person's character). *Partially salvaged:* the **land-to-sea vector** version survives as a background condition rather than a candidate — a strain of *T. gondii* whose only definitive host is a domestic cat, whose oocysts survive coastal runoff, killing southern sea otters with fatal steatitis ([Frontiers in Marine Science, 2023](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/marine-science/articles/10.3389/fmars.2023.1116899/full)). Its virtue is the apparatus — CDFW's Marine Wildlife Veterinary Care and Research Center in Santa Cruz, storm-drain jurisdiction, and litter disposal as a wildlife-mortality pathway — but on its own it does not clear C or A for a human body.

**R3. Endogenous retroviruses, and horizontal gene transfer via mobile elements.** Scientifically ideal for this register — ~8% of the human genome is retroviral, syncytin (HERV-W) is a captured envelope gene doing placental work, plasmids and integrons move resistance between unrelated bacteria. **Rejected as spent**: story 18 is built on it, and the slate's own review flags the errors it produced. Nothing new should stand on it.

**R4. Antimicrobial resistance as a trajectory.** The figures are the strongest in the domain — 1.14M deaths directly attributable in 2021, forecast **1.91M/yr by 2050 and 39 million cumulative deaths 2025–2050** ([GRAM, *Lancet* 2024](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lancet/article/PIIS0140-6736(24)01867-1/fulltext)). **Rejected twice over**: **spent** (story 20), and on its own it **fails A and C** — it is a rate, not an engine. It remains available as arithmetic inside another premise, never as the premise.

**R5. Insect and arthropod decline.** Krefeld's **>75% loss of flying insect biomass over 27 years** ([*PLOS One* 2017](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0185809)), ~9%/decade terrestrial in meta-analysis, and the western monarch overwintering count falling from millions in the 1980s to **1,901 in 2020**, with the three lowest counts in the programme's 28-year history all falling in 2020, 2024 (9,119) and 2025 (12,260) ([Xerces](https://www.xerces.org/press/western-monarch-numbers-remain-at-historic-low)). **Fails A and C.** It is a magnificent, alarming trend and nothing more: no body, no engine, and — fatally — the counting is done by volunteers on Thanksgiving weekend, which makes the *only* thing in the room a clipboard. Available as backdrop; not a premise.

**R6. Mycorrhizal networks / the "wood-wide web."** **Fails D outright.** A 2023 review found the popular claims rest on positive citation bias and overinterpretation ([Karst, Jones & Hoeksema, *Nat Ecol Evol* 2023](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41559-023-01986-1)). A turn must be *good, audited and not in doubt*; this is the opposite. Also fails A.

**R7. Negligible senescence and geroscience.** Naked mole rats defying Gompertz, *Hydra*, the 392-year Greenland shark, *Turritopsis*, and the Buck Institute in Novato as the regional tie. **Fails B and C**: it is an aspiration and a comparative-biology literature, not a process operating on bodies at population scale. There is nothing running.

**R8. De-extinction as spectacle.** Colossal's dire wolves and thylacine programme. **Fails D and E**: no audited success, contested claims, and a governance apparatus that does not yet exist. The genuinely audited thing in this space is the ferret work, which is why it sits inside C9 instead.

**R9. Gene drives.** Real, governed, and consequential — but there has still been **no gene-drive field release anywhere**. Target Malaria's Burkina Faso releases in 2019 and August 2025 were non-drive lines, and in August 2025 the national authorities **suspended all its research and ordered the modified lines destroyed**, citing transparency, divided expert assessment and insufficient national biosafety capacity ([NC State GES Center, 2025](https://ges.research.ncsu.edu/2025/09/blog-governing-emerging-technologies-a-lesson-from-burkina-faso/)). **Fails B and C for now** — the engine has never been switched on, so there is no duration and nothing operating on bodies. Excellent as *institutional* material (the biosafety committee, the sovereignty question, the insectary destruction order); not as an engine.

**R10. Dormancy and revival from long dormancy.** The **46,000-year-old nematode** revived from Siberian permafrost ([*PLOS Genetics* 2023](https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/996694)), 30,000-year permafrost viruses, the Beal seed experiment running since 1879. **Fails A and C as a standalone**: it is a single marvellous fact with no population-scale process and no body. *Folded in*: the mechanically identical, ecologically enormous version — zombie urchins in metabolic depression for decades — is inside C1, where it has a body, an engine and a coastline.

**R11. Trophic cascades as such.** Sea otters reducing burrowing-crab-driven marsh-edge erosion in Elkhorn Slough is one of the best conservation results of the decade ([Hughes et al., *Nature* 2024](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06959-9)), and otter recovery from near-extirpation is a genuine audited success. **Fails A and B on its own** — it is the *good news*, structurally. Keep it as a **turn** to be spent on something else (it is the obvious turn for any premise about predator reintroduction), not as a background condition.

**R12. Rinderpest and eradication biology.** Second disease ever eradicated (declared 2011); virus-containing material now sequestered under an FAO–WOAH approved-holding-facility regime with periodic destruction rounds ([CDC *EID* 2022](https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/eid/article/28/9/22-0297_article)). **Fails A and C** for the same reason — the engine has been switched off, deliberately and successfully. Retained instead as the single best **turn-object** in the whole domain: an eradication that worked completely, and a small number of freezers where the thing still exists under licence, being counted.

**R13. Sudden oak death.** *Phytophthora ramorum*, in California since the mid-1990s, millions of tanoak and oak dead across 15 coastal counties, spread by bay-laurel foliar infection and rain splash, regulated through nursery quarantine ([California Oak Mortality Task Force](https://www.suddenoakdeath.org/about-sudden-oak-death/)). Superb setting-a matter — it was found in **Marin County first**, and the apparatus (CDFA nursery inspection, the SOD Blitz volunteer survey) is exactly the right shape. **Fails A**: it does nothing to a human body, and every attempt to give it one requires a second impossibility. Held as **setting and apparatus**, and as a strong second element to combine with C10 or C11.

# 3. CLOSING NOTE

**What this domain uniquely supplies: hysteresis.** Every other bank in the playbook has to work to justify §0's *already done, at scale, and not reversible*. Ecology supplies it as a standard result. A regime shift is a system with two states and a threshold, where removing the pressure does not restore the original condition, and the mathematics of that has been standard since Scheffer. That is not a metaphor for the register — it *is* the register, with a literature and error bars. C1 is the purest instance: the pressure is gone, the predator is being bred back, and the barren stays, because it is held in place by animals that stopped living without dying. C7 is the same shape at planetary scale with a number on it — less than a quarter of the deoxygenation historical emissions have already caused has happened yet, and it will go on arriving for six hundred years whatever anyone does now. Nothing in the law, finance or engineering banks can offer a *committed* quantity like that.

**What it supplies second: the accidental safety.** §0's best sentence — *the safety is gone for good and was never deliberate* — has exactly one literal instance in nature, and it is C3. Mammalian body temperature is the most effective anti-infective in the vertebrate record. Nobody built it, nobody maintains it, nobody audits it, it has been the only thing between us and the largest kingdom of life, and the margin is two degrees.

**What it supplies third, and most usefully: the interval.** §3.14 is a family the slate is under-using, and this domain has the only documented case anywhere of a therapeutic intervention delivering a second, different pathology **forty years after the programme closed** (C4). It comes with a real institutional response — recipient notification, incidents panels, instrument quarantine — whose entire product is a letter that changes nothing for the person who receives it.

**Where these sit in the playbook.**

- **§2.20 (substance and residue)** is where C7 belongs almost unaltered: a sulphidic shelf is *an atmosphere that is a by-product: a stated tolerance in minutes, a colour, a depth, produced continuously by something running normally and with no plan to stop*, and it is real and photographable from orbit. C11's mercury belongs there too — *a contaminant found in the sample and then in the lines that were cooling the sample*.
- **§2.21 (a change the body is awake for)** takes C5 whole: a transmissible cancer is *a body human at every scale except organisation*, and it is an animal older than the host wearing a face. C1's urchins are the family's missing member — *a degradation the subject is content inside*, with no distress and no terminus.
- **§3.14 (the interval)** is where C4 and C6 sit, and both would strengthen a family that currently has no entry running at national-programme scale.
- **§3.15 (scale in units the body understands)** is where this domain earns its keep: 120 urchins per square metre against a target of 2; 47 grams per square metre of sulphur bacteria as the only barrier; 20 parts per million; six billion sea stars; one colony from San Diego to Ukiah; 1.4 million seed samples at −18 °C; 20 million vehicles a year through the inspection stations.
- **§3.12 (disgust produced by correct procedure)** is where the turns land. Six of the eleven candidates put a *successful conservation or public-health programme* in the mechanism position — the captive-breeding studbook, the quarantine calendar, the hunter surveillance scheme, the cloned ferret, the eradication project, the restored marsh — and in every case the programme is competent, honest, audited and doing precisely what it was built to do.
- **§5 (setting-a)** gains three things the slate has never used: the **special district** (vector control and water districts, with their own tax bases and boards), the **marine laboratory** (Bodega, Moss Landing, Tiburon), and the **inspection station** — a real, staffed, physical border around the state that twenty million vehicles a year drive through.

**The one warning.** Everything above is real, and §1 step 3 is explicit that a premise built entirely from real mechanisms is journalism. None of these candidates is a premise. Each is a **background condition or an engine** into which exactly one impossibility still has to be spent — and the cheapest, worst way to spend it here would be to make the organism do something more. The better place, in every case above, is the **instrument**: a rubric, an action level, a studbook, a certificate or a monitoring feed that tracks something no instrument could track. The bodies are already provided.
