# MECH — ASTROPHYSICS, COSMOLOGY, DEEP TIME

*Survey of the domain for material that can serve as a background condition or an engine. Assessed against A (a body in it), B (duration rather than event), C (an engine), D (a turn available), E (a real institution), F (setting-a matrix). Everything below is real unless flagged; the impossibility is not spent here, it is left for the premise.*

**The domain's structural problem, stated once.** Astronomy is the classic source of the failure §0 names — the enormous thing that names no body and no cost. A supernova is not dark. A heat death is not dark. Scale in exponents is the opposite of §3.15, which asks for units the body understands. Almost everything in this field fails criterion A, and most of what is written about it is spectacle with an awed narrator, which is §3.7 composure inverted into the wrong register.

**The inversion that rescues it.** Three shapes convert this domain from spectacle into apparatus, and everything kept below is one of them:

1. **The computable arrival.** A cause that has already finished and is in transit. It is not an event because nothing is pending — it is *done*, and only the arrival is scheduled. Latency generates a date, a date generates a disclosure policy, a disclosure policy generates an office, and the office generates the story. This is the only domain where §0's "already done, at scale, and not reversible" is literally true of an external fact rather than of an institution's history.
2. **The regulated deep-time period.** Real agencies are legally obliged to compute harm to people who do not exist yet — to name them, give them a diet, a water intake and a postcode, and count them. Nothing else in the playbook's banks has a bureaucracy with a million-year mandate.
3. **The unconsented baseline.** Cosmic ray background is a dose every person carries, at a known rate, which no one agreed to, which is subtracted from every measurement, and which the professional bodies have ruled must not be converted into a body count even though their own model says it can be.

## 1.1 The roster is the dose — aircrew and the galactic cosmic ray flux

**What it is.** Above about 8 km the atmosphere stops shielding you. A full-time long-haul crew member flying 800–900 block hours a year absorbs **3–5 mSv/yr**, and **6 mSv/yr or more** on polar routes at high cruise altitude — roughly two to three times the global average natural background of 2.4 mSv/yr, and higher than the average measured dose of most monitored nuclear workers. The rate is about **4–6 µSv per hour aloft**, and it scales with altitude and with geomagnetic latitude, so the dose is a computable function of the route and the flight level. The FAA's **CARI-7 / CARI-7A** code (Civil Aerospace Medical Institute, Oklahoma City) will calculate it for any city pair, free, on request.

The epidemiology is real and not in dispute in outline: elevated melanoma and non-melanoma skin cancer in pilots and cabin crew, elevated breast cancer in female cabin crew, and pilots roughly three times as likely as non-pilots to develop nuclear cataract.

**The jurisdictional split, which is the whole thing.** Under **EU Directive 2013/59/Euratom**, aircrew likely to receive more than 1 mSv/yr are *occupationally exposed persons*: the operator must assess the dose, keep the record, and organise rosters to reduce it, and a crew member who declares a pregnancy must be scheduled so that the dose to the fetus for the remainder of the pregnancy stays under **1 mSv**. In the United States the FAA publishes guidance and the calculator and requires nothing: no mandatory assessment, no dose record, no mandatory reassignment on declared pregnancy. The same body, on the same aircraft, at the same flight level, is either a monitored radiation worker or nobody, depending on the operating certificate.

**What it does to a body.** It deposits ionising energy in the tissue of a person who is at work, at a rate she cannot feel, for the whole of her career, and the deposition is proportional to how good her bid was. There is no acute injury and no symptom; the injury is a lifetime integral and a probability that lands, if it lands, twenty to forty years later, in a body that has retired.

**The engine.** Seniority bidding. Crew scheduling is a genuinely fair, genuinely audited, contractually negotiated instrument that allocates the desirable flying — the long-haul, high-latitude, high-pay routes — by seniority. It is therefore also, without anyone designing it that way, the dose-allocation instrument, and it allocates the highest dose to the people who have earned the right to compete for it. The engine runs on wanting: crews *bid* for the polar tracks. It would go on existing if every regulator vanished, because it is a labour agreement.

**The turn.** The bid line. The thing that is good, audited, effective and not in doubt is the seniority system — the union's great achievement, the protection against favouritism, the reason a captain's roster cannot be punished. Make that the mechanism: the fairest instrument in the industry is the one that sorts a workforce by cumulative absorbed dose, and the people at the top of it are there because they won.

Secondary turn available: the pregnancy provision. In Europe it is a protection that works; the moment it is invoked it also converts a private fact into a scheduling record held by the employer, and the record is the disclosure.

**Institutions.** FAA Civil Aerospace Medical Institute (CARI-7); EASA and the national competent authorities under 2013/59/Euratom; commercial dosimetry services that sell per-flight dose calculation to airlines; **ICAO's space weather advisory system**, operational since November 2019, in which four designated global centres (the US via NOAA SWPC, the European **PECASUS** consortium, ACFJ, and CRC) issue MOD and SEV advisories to aviation for **RADIATION**, HF COM and GNSS; NOAA's **S-scale**, whose text says from S2 upward that "passengers and crew in high-flying aircraft at high latitudes may be exposed to radiation risk," with S1 at about 50 occurrences per solar cycle, S3 at about 10, S4 at about 3, and S5 at fewer than one.

**setting-a matrix.** SFO's transpacific and polar-track flying to Asia is exactly the high-latitude, high-altitude, long-duration flying that maximises the integral; the crew bases and the union locals are here; and the airline's scheduling system — the actual mechanism — is software written and run in this region.

**Premise it generates.** A crew scheduler at an SFO base runs the monthly bid award, and the seniority list she is applying is, read down, an exposure ranking with the most senior name at the top.

## 1.2 The interval that pays at fifty percent — probability of causation

**What it is.** The Linear No-Threshold model holds that stochastic risk is proportional to dose with no threshold, which means every dose — including the unavoidable cosmic ray component of natural background, roughly 0.33–0.4 mSv/yr at sea level within a US average total of about 6.2 mSv/yr per NCRP Report 160 — carries a nonzero probability of a fatal cancer. The model is the basis of every radiation protection regulation in force. It is also arithmetically capable of converting any population's collective dose in person-sieverts into a number of expected deaths, and the professional bodies have ruled that this must not be done: ICRP's position is that collective dose is a tool for optimising protection and is not to be used to calculate cancer deaths in a population, particularly from small individual doses over long periods. The number exists, is computable from the institution's own model, and the institution has issued a rule against computing it.

**Where it acquires a bureaucracy.** Under the **Energy Employees Occupational Illness Compensation Program Act**, NIOSH performs **dose reconstruction** for a former nuclear worker with cancer — frequently a dead one, frequently from incomplete or missing monitoring records — and the Department of Labor runs the result through **NIOSH-IREP** to produce a **Probability of Causation**. At **50% or above** ("at least as likely as not"), the claim pays **$150,000 plus medical benefits**. Below it, nothing. The Radiation Exposure Compensation Act does the same work for downwinders and uranium miners by a different route: a statutory list of diseases, a statutory list of counties, and a date range.

**What it does to a body.** It puts a number on a specific cancer in a specific person and uses it to decide whether that cancer counts. The body has already done the work — the exposure was in 1961, the leukaemia in 1989, the claim in 2004, the reconstruction in 2011, the determination after the claimant's death — and the interval between the two ends is the mechanism, not a delay in it. This is §3.14 in its purest available form: the result arrives correctly, on time, addressed to somebody no longer in a position to receive it.

**The engine.** Retrospective dose reconstruction at population scale. A program that takes incomplete records of what a body absorbed decades ago, models what is missing with defensible assumptions, and returns a percentage. It runs on files. It would continue to exist if the compensation program were abolished, because the same reconstruction methodology is what any epidemiological cohort study requires.

**The turn.** The compensation program is the good thing. It was won by sick workers and their advocates after decades; it is a genuine remedy, generously drawn, deliberately claimant-favourable (uncertainty is resolved at the upper credibility limit in the claimant's favour). Make *that* the mechanism: the instrument that finally recognised these people is the instrument that reduces each of them to a percentage and turns half of them away at 49%, and the more carefully it is administered the more precisely it does so. Nobody is lying and nothing is being denied in bad faith. The subsidiary turn: background — including the sky's fixed share — is what gets subtracted, so the universe's contribution to your cancer is the part that is definitionally nobody's fault.

**Institutions.** NIOSH Division of Compensation Analysis and Support; DOL Office of Workers' Compensation Programs; the Advisory Board on Radiation and Worker Health; ICRP and NCRP for the model; the covered-facility and Special Exposure Cohort designation process, which decides by class whether a whole workforce may skip the arithmetic.

**setting-a matrix.** **Lawrence Livermore** and the **Lawrence Berkeley Laboratory** are covered facilities under EEOICPA, with claimant populations in Livermore, Tracy and the East Bay; the Hunters Point Naval Shipyard radiological legacy and its falsified soil-sampling scandal sit inside San Francisco; the same dosimetry and health-physics community staffs both sides of the desk.

**Premise it generates.** A dose reconstructor at a contractor's office in Livermore fills the gap in a dead machinist's 1963 film-badge record with the model's default assumption, and the default is the reason the number comes out at 48.9%.

## 1.3 The clock inside every body, and the year it stops working

**What it is.** Atmospheric nuclear testing between 1955 and 1963 roughly **doubled** the concentration of ¹⁴C in the atmosphere. Every organism that has eaten since then carries the year in its tissue: cells built in 1968 have 1968's ratio, cells built in 1994 have 1994's. **Bomb-pulse dating** reads a cell population's birth date directly from its carbon. It has been used to date human neurons, adipocytes, cardiomyocytes, T cells (spleen populations turn out older than blood, lung and intestinal ones), collagen in aneurysm walls, and — routinely, forensically — **tooth enamel**, which is laid down once in childhood and never remodelled, so the enamel gives a year of birth for an unidentified body to within a year or two.

**The window is closing, and there are two closures.** Atmospheric ¹⁴C fell **below its pre-1955 value for the first time in 2021**: new organic material now carries the signature of a 1955 sample. The second closure is the **Suess effect** — fossil carbon is ¹⁴C-dead, so continued emissions dilute the atmosphere further, and on high-emission projections new material produced around **2050 will have the same radiocarbon signature as material from about AD 1050**. Fresh tissue will date to the Middle Ages. Forensic dating of recent remains stops working, and so does art and ivory authentication; the mitigations on offer are δ¹³C and ¹³⁷Cs, which are weaker.

**What it does to a body.** They take the tooth. The method requires enamel, so identification of an unknown decedent proceeds by extracting a tooth, burning it, and reading the year off the carbon — the body is the only remaining witness (§3.13) and it is consumed to be read. And the label itself was written into every person alive in the window by the tests, without anyone's consent, as a global act of contamination that is now the most useful biological clock ever available.

**The engine.** A planetary isotopic label, applied once to an entire human cohort, legible per cell, decaying out of usefulness on a known schedule. It runs whether or not anyone is measuring, and it is exhausting itself.

**The turn.** The Partial Test Ban Treaty. The good, audited, unambiguously correct thing — the treaty that stopped the poisoning of the atmosphere in 1963 — is precisely what makes the clock run down; and the second good thing, decarbonisation, is what finishes it. The identification of the unidentified dead is a capability that exists only because of an atrocity and expires because the atrocity was stopped. Nobody can want either half.

**Institutions.** **Lawrence Livermore's Center for Accelerator Mass Spectrometry (CAMS)** — the lab that does this; the Karolinska group for the cell-biology applications; the IntCal calibration consortium; medical examiners' and coroners' offices that commission the assay; INTERPOL DVI protocols.

**setting-a matrix.** Structural, not scenery. CAMS is in Livermore. The samples are couriered there. The county whose unidentified remains are being read is a setting-a county, and the lab that reads them is forty miles away and was built for weapons work.

**Premise it generates.** A coroner's office in a setting-a county has a shelf of unidentified remains and a hard closing date, because after it the teeth stop being able to say when their owners were born.

## 1.4 The person defined by regulation — the reasonably maximally exposed individual

**What it is.** **40 CFR 197.20–197.21** requires the Department of Energy to demonstrate that a repository at Yucca Mountain will not give too much dose to a person specified in the regulation. That person is defined in law. She **lives in the accessible environment directly above the highest concentration of radionuclides in the plume**. She **drinks 2 litres a day of water from wells drilled at that location**. Her diet and her way of life are set to the **mean values of a survey of the residents of the Town of Amargosa Valley, Nevada**. Her permitted annual dose is **15 mrem (0.15 mSv) for the first 10,000 years** and **100 mrem (1 mSv) thereafter, throughout the period of geologic stability** — which the rule sets at **one million years** after disposal.

She does not exist. She never will. She is re-instantiated at every timestep of the performance assessment, for a million years, and the regulation is met or not met according to what happens to her.

**What it does to a body.** It gives a body a legal existence with no interiority: a location, a water intake in litres, a diet taken from a survey of real neighbours who *do* exist and were asked what they eat, and a permitted dose. Everything a person is, that is not exposure pathway, has been struck as not load-bearing. This is §2.1 written by a rulemaking committee and published in the Federal Register.

**The engine.** Performance assessment. A probabilistic model, run over a million simulated years, that transports radionuclides through rock and groundwater into a defined mouth. It is not a metaphor and not a policy; it is a computation with outputs in millirem per year, and it is the licence.

**The turn.** The RMEI was invented *to be protective*. Conservatism is the whole point: you place the hypothetical person at the worst location, give her the highest plausible intake, and if she is safe everyone is. It is one of the better ideas in environmental regulation and it works. Make that the mechanism: the way you protect a million years of unborn people is to invent one and specify her, and once she is specified she is the only one the system can see — the real residents of Amargosa Valley were surveyed to supply her diet and have no other standing in the document.

**Institutions.** EPA (40 CFR 197); NRC (10 CFR 63); DOE Office of Legacy Management, whose statutory role is custody in perpetuity. Cousins with the same shape: Finland's **Posiva** and Sweden's **SKB**, whose safety cases must model the **next glacial cycle** — permafrost depth, ice-sheet load, glacial meltwater chemistry at 430 m — over 100,000 years, which is where Milankovitch forcing acquires a bureaucracy and a licence condition.

**setting-a matrix.** Berkeley Lab and Livermore did the Yucca Mountain hydrology, geochemistry and thermal-hydrology modelling; the coupled-process codes and the peer review are regional work product.

**Premise it generates.** A modeller finds that the licence turns on one parameter of a person who does not exist, and that the parameter was taken from a real household in Amargosa Valley who answered a questionnaire in 1997.

## 1.5 The expected number of future intruders

**What it is.** The **Waste Isolation Pilot Plant** must demonstrate compliance with **40 CFR 191** for **10,000 years**, and the dominant release scenario is a future human drilling into the repository by accident. So the DOE computes how often that will happen. The **Compliance Recertification Application** derives a future drilling rate from the previous **100 years of actual drilling in the Delaware Basin** — New Mexico Oil Conservation Division and Texas records, about **99% oil and gas** — and carries it forward: **46.8 boreholes/km² per 10,000 years** in the original application, **59.8** at CRA-2009, **67.3** at CRA-2014. Intrusion times are drawn from a **Poisson process** in three regimes: **0–100 years**, active institutional control, no intrusions; **100–700 years**, passive controls reduce the rate by two orders of magnitude; **700–10,000 years**, full rate. The repository is discretised into 144 blocks. The result is a distribution: **the most likely number of intrusions into a waste panel is 5, with probability 0.1715**; the probability of **zero** intrusions is **0.0041**; the modelled maximum is 14, at probability 0.0011.

A federal agency has calculated, as a licence condition, that it will almost certainly be drilled into by people who do not know what is down there, and has published the number.

**And it has designed the warning.** Sandia's marker studies (SAND92-1382 and its companions) convened panels of anthropologists, linguists, astronomers, archaeologists and materials scientists, who concluded that **no language can be relied on for 10,000 years** and that the message must therefore be carried by the shape of the land. The design concepts are on the record: **Landscape of Thorns**, **Spike Field**, **Spikes Bursting Through Grid**, **Menacing Earthworks** (lightning-shaped berms radiating from an open centre, designed to dwarf the visitor and to deny any sense of place), **Black Hole** (a slab of black basalt that absorbs desert heat and reads as uninhabitable), **Rubble Landscape**, **Forbidding Blocks**. The message they drafted begins:

> *This place is not a place of honor. No highly esteemed deed is commemorated here. Nothing valued is here.*

**What it does to a body.** It puts a drill bit through a waste panel and brings contaminated material up the borehole to a crew that came for potash or gas, and the model says roughly five crews. The dose to each is calculated. The bodies are anonymous, future and enumerated, which is exactly the register §3.5 works in: the number is the horror.

**The engine.** Extrapolated economic behaviour. The rate is not a guess about the future; it is the past hundred years of drilling in that basin, projected forward and treated as a natural process — because over that timescale, human resource extraction *is* a natural process, with a rate constant, like erosion.

**The turn.** The marker system is the most sincere, most carefully reasoned communication any government has ever attempted: an honest attempt to warn strangers, with no benefit to the warner, across a gap longer than writing has existed. It works by being repellent. The people it is meant to protect will experience it as a hostile landscape built to frighten them, and the report says so, and that is the correct design.

**Institutions.** DOE Carlsbad Field Office and WIPP; EPA (40 CFR 191, recertification every five years); Sandia National Laboratories; the Human Interference Task Force lineage; the Nuclear Energy Agency's *Preservation of Records, Knowledge and Memory* programme.

**setting-a matrix.** Weaker than the others: Berkeley and Livermore geoscience feeds the same regulatory apparatus, and the University of California ran Los Alamos and Livermore when the marker work was commissioned. Take it as a §5 hook rather than a claim.

**Premise it generates.** A recertification analyst updates the drilling rate for the five-year cycle, the number goes up because the basin got busier, and the report she is writing is a forecast of how many people will be killed by her employer's competence.

## 1.6 The alert with a computed arrival

**What it is.** The shape the whole domain exists to supply. **Neutrinos from a core-collapse supernova outrun the light**: from **SN 1987A**, underground detectors caught a burst of about 13 seconds roughly **three hours** before any telescope saw the star brighten, because the neutrinos leave the collapsing core immediately and the shock takes hours to break out of the envelope. The **SuperNova Early Warning System (SNEWS)** is a standing global network of neutrino experiments that exists to turn that head start into an alert to the astronomical community — the lead time is "minutes, hours or days, depending on the nature of the stellar envelope and the amount of obscuration." Meanwhile the light-travel latency makes the whole sky a set of finished events with pending arrivals: Betelgeuse is roughly 500–550 light years away, so if it has gone, it went in the fifteenth century and nothing anyone does now is upstream of it.

The receiving apparatus is now industrial. The **Vera C. Rubin Observatory** began the Legacy Survey of Space and Time on **30 June 2026**, taking a new image every **40 seconds** with a camera built at **SLAC**, and issuing up to **seven to ten million alerts a night** into a broker ecosystem that no human being reads. On the interpretive side there is a real disclosure bureaucracy: the IAA's **Declaration of Principles Concerning Activities Following the Detection of Extraterrestrial Intelligence**, the **Rio 2.0** significance scale, and a live 2026 revision — a protocol whose core requirement is *independent verification before announcement*. It has been exercised once in earnest: **BLC1**, the Breakthrough Listen candidate from the direction of Proxima Centauri, which took about a year of work to establish as terrestrial interference.

**What it does to a body.** Nothing, on its own — and that is the honest assessment. This candidate is a **shape and a machine**, not a harm. It is kept because it is the carrier wave: it converts any impossibility placed inside it into pure latency with a date, and a date is what generates schedules, disclosure policy, triage and an office. Spend the impossibility here (a signal already in transit whose arrival is computable and whose effect on tissue is known) and criteria A, C and D are supplied by the impossibility while B, E and F are supplied by real machinery.

**The engine.** Detection at a rate that exceeds attention, coupled to a verification protocol that guarantees delay. Ten million alerts a night, brokered by software; a candidate that takes a year to kill; a neutrino trigger whose whole value is measured in hours. The apparatus cannot be made faster without being made wrong, and it would go on running if every institution that funds it dissolved, because the photons keep arriving.

**The turn.** The verification requirement. It is unambiguously good: it exists so that no one panics a population over an artefact, and it was written by careful people after decades of false alarms, and BLC1 proves it works. Make that the mechanism — the protocol that protects the public from a false announcement is the reason the people in the path find out last, and every hour it costs is an hour it was designed to cost.

**Institutions.** SNEWS; the IAU Transient Name Server and CBAT; NASA/GSFC and **SLAC** for Fermi GBM gamma-ray burst alerts; Rubin Observatory and its alert brokers; the IAA SETI Permanent Committee; NOAA SWPC and the ICAO advisory centres for the parts of the sky that require an operational response, and **DSCOVR at L1**, which buys the terrestrial grid **15 to 60 minutes**.

**setting-a matrix.** Unusually strong. The **SETI Institute** (Mountain View) and the **Allen Telescope Array** (Hat Creek, CA); **Breakthrough Listen** and the Berkeley SETI Research Center at UC Berkeley, which owns BLC1; **SLAC** (Menlo Park), which built the LSST camera and runs Fermi-LAT operations; **Lick Observatory** on Mount Hamilton, whose **KAIT** robotic telescope ran one of the longest-lived automated supernova searches in the world; **Berkeley Space Sciences Laboratory** in the hills above campus, operating heliophysics missions. The whole apparatus of *finding out first* is in this region.

**Premise it generates.** A post-detection verification committee meets in Mountain View on a fixed weekly cadence because the protocol requires independent confirmation, and the arrival date is inside the meeting schedule.

## 1.7 The correction that works, and the cause that cannot be proved

**What it is.** Secondary neutrons from cosmic ray air showers flip bits in semiconductors. The effect is real enough to have a **JEDEC standard** (**JESD89**, measurement and reporting of alpha-particle and terrestrial cosmic-ray-induced soft errors), an accelerated-test industry (neutron beam time bought at spallation sources), altitude-dependent derating in avionics single-event-effect certification, and an entire mitigation stack — ECC, chipkill, scrubbing, triple-modular redundancy — that exists to make the errors disappear before anyone sees them.

It is also, in the field, close to unprovable in the individual case. A careful field study of two large clusters found **4.5 million corrected errors and 71 uncorrected errors across 2×10¹² MB-hours** on ECC-protected server DIMMs, and **over 25 million errors in 1.35×10⁸ MB-hours** on unprotected mobile-class memory — and detected **no correlation whatsoever** with neutron monitor counts. When NASA's Engineering and Safety Center was asked by NHTSA to investigate Toyota's unintended acceleration, single-event upset was one of the mechanisms examined; the report could not demonstrate it and could not exclude it.

**What it does to a body.** Through the machine, and only through the machine: a throttle controller, an infusion pump, a ventilator, a linear accelerator, a signalling relay. There are dead people in the Toyota file. The mechanism's signature is that the body is unambiguous and the cause is not.

**The engine.** A flux you can budget for and never attribute. It produces a *rate* that is real at population scale and a *cause* that is unfalsifiable at the individual scale — which is precisely §0's "no agent": ask who did this and the true answer is nobody, and here the nobody is a particle from outside the solar system that left no trace of itself.

**The turn.** Error-correcting memory. It works. It silently repairs the corruption and logs the correction, which means the well-engineered system is the one that guarantees nobody ever learns the rate, and the failures that reach a body are by construction the ones from the parts of the stack that were not worth protecting. The audit trail records "corrected" and therefore records nothing.

**Institutions.** JEDEC's reliability committee; RTCA/EUROCAE avionics SEE guidance; IEC 62396 for atmospheric radiation effects in avionics electronics; NHTSA and the NASA Engineering and Safety Center; the neutron-beam test facilities that sell the qualification.

**setting-a matrix.** The semiconductor design houses and the hyperscale data-centre operators whose reliability budgets contain this line item are headquartered here; the decision about which products get ECC and which do not is a product-management decision made in Santa Clara County.

**Premise it generates.** A reliability engineer discovers that the incident rate her company reports is a function of which of its products log corrections, and that the safest device in the catalogue is the one nobody has ever been able to blame.

# 2. THE REJECTS

Ruthlessly, with the criterion each fails. Several supply an image or a figure and nothing more; the domain is mostly this.

- **The Sun's main-sequence evolution and the habitability window** — luminosity rising roughly 1% per 110 Myr, ~70% of present output at 4.5 Ga (the faint young Sun), CO₂ starvation ending C₃ photosynthesis on the order of 10⁸–10⁹ years out, moist greenhouse in ~1–2 Gyr. **Fails A, C, E.** No body, no engine on bodies, no institution. It is a background condition and can be the *reason* an institution exists in a far-future frame, but it cannot be the mechanism.

- **Milankovitch cycles** — 100 kyr eccentricity, 41 kyr obliquity, ~21–23 kyr precession, next glacial inception plausibly delayed tens of thousands of years by anthropogenic CO₂. **Fails A and E standing alone.** Kept only as a component of 1.4: the one place these cycles acquire a body and a bureaucracy is a repository safety case that must model the next ice age as a licence condition.

- **Proton decay, heat death, and the far-future timeline** — Super-Kamiokande's limits push the proton lifetime past ~10³⁴ years; the Adams–Laughlin eras run to 10¹⁰⁰. **Fails A, C, E.** This is scale in exponents, which is the opposite of §3.15. A number that cannot be converted into a quantity a body feels is not enormous in this playbook's sense; §0's "enormous" counts people, not orders of magnitude.

- **False vacuum decay** — the metastable-electroweak-vacuum result that follows from the measured Higgs and top masses. **Fails A and B decisively.** It arrives at the speed of light, so there is no latency, no interval, no warning, no schedule and no office; and nothing happens to a body because there is no body afterward. *One artifact survives and is worth keeping in §4:* the **LHC Safety Assessment Group review** (CERN, 2008; arXiv:0806.3414), a formal published document in which an institution certifies that its instrument will not destroy the universe, with reasoning, references and a sign-off.

- **The cosmological horizon and the expansion cutting off the observable universe** — accelerated expansion means the overwhelming majority of currently observable galaxies are already causally unreachable, and on ~10¹¹-year timescales the extragalactic sky empties. **Fails A, C, E.** It is the domain's truest "no exit, or an exit that cannot be used" and it happens to nobody. Worth one line in §2 and nothing more. (setting-a note: **DESI** is run out of **Berkeley Lab**, and the Supernova Cosmology Project that found the acceleration was LBNL work — the region measured the thing that closes the door.)

- **The anthropic principle, the Fermi paradox, the Doomsday argument, the Great Filter.** **Fails §2's own test.** These are propositions — claims about how the world works, with no subject, no mechanism and no cost — and a thing asserted about everybody is a thing happening to nobody. They read as finished when they are barely started.

- **A Carrington-class geomagnetic storm as the story.** **Fails B**, and fails A except at one remove. Grid collapse is an event with an infrastructural aftermath, which is disaster fiction, and the playbook does that better through other routes. What survives is absorbed into 1.1: the **DSCOVR** 15-to-60-minute warning latency, the **G/S/R scales** with their per-cycle frequencies, and the aviation radiation advisory.

- **Gamma-ray bursts as an extinction mechanism** — the Ordovician hypothesis; **GRB 221009A** measurably disturbed the ionosphere and induced ground currents on 9 October 2022, from 2.4 billion light years away. **Fails A, C, E** as an engine. The 221009A detail is a superb single beat — an object that finished before the Earth had animals, arriving as a line on a magnetometer trace — and belongs in §4 or as an image, not as a mechanism.

- **Muon and neutrino flux through living tissue** — about one muon per cm² per minute at sea level, so tens to hundreds through a person every second, one or two through a hand; about 10¹⁴ neutrinos through each of us per second, with perhaps one interaction in a body over a lifetime. **Fails A in the sense that matters**: these are body figures with no cost attached, and the cosmic component of background dose is only ~0.33–0.4 mSv/yr. Keep them as **§3.15 units**, deployed against something that *does* cost — never as the harm itself.

- **Cosmic rays and the genome as a standalone engine.** **Fails A on the arithmetic.** Endogenous oxidative metabolism inflicts on the order of 10⁴–10⁵ DNA lesions per cell per day; background radiation's contribution is a rounding error against it. The honest version of this fact is an inversion — the sky is doing far less to you than your own respiration — which is interesting and is not horror. The usable half is the *regulatory* treatment of it, which is 1.2.

- **Near-Earth objects as an impact story.** **Fails B.** The impact is an event. *What survives and is genuinely strong is the office*, folded into the closing note below and into §4: **JPL Sentry**, which automatically scans the catalogue and publishes without human intervention; the **Torino** and **Palermo** scales; the **IAWN**/**SMPAG** notification thresholds; the risk-list dynamic in which a shrinking uncertainty region makes the probability **rise** before it collapses (2024 YR4 climbed past 3% for 22 December 2032 before observations eliminated Earth entirely and left a residual lunar-impact chance); **NASA Ames**' Asteroid Threat Assessment Project and its **PAIR** model, which converts an object into **expected casualties**; **LLNL**'s nuclear-deflection simulation work; and the biennial interagency planetary-defence tabletop exercises that rehearse deciding whom to evacuate. This is a real bureaucracy with a real body count in its outputs, and it is a **conditional** keep: it becomes a candidate the moment the premise makes the arrival certain rather than probabilistic, at which point it converts into 1.6.

- **SETI as a subject.** **Fails A and C** on its own. Retained only as the disclosure apparatus inside 1.6.

- **Leap seconds and the abolition of civil time's tie to the Earth's rotation** — CGPM Resolution 4 (2022) to stop leap seconds by 2035, the prospect of a first-ever *negative* leap second as the Earth's rotation speeds up, and the fact that the abolition was driven substantially by the operational cost to large data-centre operators (Google's leap smear was invented in Mountain View). **Fails A**, and it is a strong regional-institutional object with no body in it. Park it in §5 as a setting-a artifact; it is not a mechanism.

# 3. CLOSING NOTE

**What this domain uniquely supplies.** Three things the playbook cannot get from medicine, finance, land, labour or belief:

**1. Latency with a computable arrival — the only honest way to have a cause with no agent and still have a deadline.** Everywhere else in the banks, the reason nobody stops it is bureaucratic: the funding is annual, the standard predates the thing, the presentation is atypical. Here the reason is a physical constant. The cause is finished, the arrival is arithmetic, and nothing anybody does is upstream of it — which means an institution can be *entirely competent, entirely honest and entirely futile* without a single person behaving badly, and the story is then purely about what a competent office does with a date. That is §0's "no agent" and "already done, at scale, and not reversible" supplied by physics rather than asserted by the author, and it is the cleanest such supply available anywhere in the material.

**2. A real bureaucracy with a mandate in deep time, and a legally defined body inside it.** Nothing else in the banks has agencies that are statutorily required to compute harm to people who do not exist. The RMEI has a water intake in litres and a diet taken from a survey of her neighbours. WIPP's performance assessment says five. These are not thought experiments; they are licence conditions, recertified on a five-year cycle, with named offices and public dockets. This is §2.5 and §2.10 material with citations, and it is the domain's single most valuable contribution, because it gives the playbook a genuine *institution of the far future* rather than a gothic one.

**3. An unconsented baseline that everybody carries.** Background dose — the sky's fixed share of it — is the only harm in the whole corpus that is already inside every reader, at a known rate, on the record, subtracted from every measurement as noise, and ruled by the responsible professional body to be uncountable in bodies even though its own model counts it. That is §3.1 retroactive re-reading of the audience, available without a single invented fact.

**And the domain's discipline, stated as a warning.** Everything here fails unless it is converted into a unit the body understands. mSv per hour aloft. Boreholes per square kilometre per ten thousand years. Litres per day. One muon per square centimetre per minute. Three hours of neutrino lead time. Fifteen to sixty minutes from L1. Fifty percent. The moment the writing reaches for light-years or 10³⁴, the register has left and taken the horror with it.

**Where it sits in the playbook's structure.** Not a new section. The material is a *substrate* for §3 and a set of specifics for §5, and forcing it into its own bank would produce exactly the astronomical-spectacle failure this survey has spent most of its length rejecting.

- **§3 is the primary home, and it needs one new family.** Propose **§3.16 — The arrival is computable**, sitting between §3.14 (the interval) and §3.15 (scale). §3.14 is the dread of a latency that has *already elapsed for somebody*; the new family is the dread of a latency that is *still running and has a publishable end date*, which is a different shape and generates different apparatus — a disclosure policy, a verification protocol, a warning threshold, a triage list, and an office whose whole job is the gap. Lines it would carry: the alert that has to be independently confirmed before it can be announced, and the confirmation is the delay; the head start measured in hours against a cause that finished before the language was written; the risk table that is never empty and is revised nightly.

- **§3.4 (duration with no exit)** takes the regulated deep-time period: a compliance demonstration that runs to one million years, recertified every five.

- **§3.14 (the interval)** takes probability of causation: the interval formalised as a payment threshold, and the determination that arrives after the claimant's death.

- **§3.15 (scale in units the body understands)** takes the astronomical unit conversions listed above, and should be explicitly extended with the note that exponents are *not* this — that 10³⁴ is the anti-pattern the family exists to prevent.

- **§2** takes a small number of lines, no more than five or six, mostly in **2.5 (maintenance and duration)** and **2.10 (institutions)**: the person defined by regulation with a stated water intake; the expected number of future intruders, published; the clock written into a cohort by an atrocity and expiring because the atrocity stopped; the fairest instrument in the industry sorting a workforce by absorbed dose.

- **§4** takes artifacts, and they are unusually good: a Sentry risk-table row with its Palermo value; a CARI-7 dose printout for a specific city pair; a dose-reconstruction report with a percentage on the last page; a Sandia marker elevation drawing of the Landscape of Thorns with a scale bar; the LSAG review; an ICAO SEV radiation advisory in its fixed message format.

- **§5** takes the regional entries, and they are structural rather than scenic: **LLNL CAMS** in Livermore (the only place the teeth can be read); **LLNL** and **LBNL** as EEOICPA covered facilities and as Yucca Mountain modelling shops; **NASA Ames** at Moffett Field (ATAP/PAIR, the casualty model); **SLAC** at Menlo Park (the LSST camera, Fermi-LAT); **Lick Observatory** and KAIT on Mount Hamilton; the **SETI Institute** at Mountain View, the **Allen Telescope Array** at Hat Creek, and **Berkeley SETI**/Breakthrough Listen; **Berkeley Space Sciences Laboratory**; **LBNL** and DESI; SFO's polar and transpacific crew bases and the scheduling software that builds their lines.
