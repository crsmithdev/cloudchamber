## 0. THE FILTER I APPLIED

This domain fails criterion **A** more often than any other. Almost everything in it is a fact about landscape — a rate, a hazard map, a trend line — and the discipline the assessment imposes is that a rate is not a body. The second most common failure is **B**: geology's headline products are events (a quake, an eruption, a flood), and §0 forbids the event shape outright. What survives is the small set of processes that are *cumulative, irreversible, and enter tissue* — and I have been strict about that. Nine candidates held. Fourteen did not.

The domain's compensating strength is **D**. This is the richest available seam for the turn, because environmental regulation is one of the few fields where things demonstrably *worked*, were *audited*, and are *not in doubt*: the ozone layer, the PFOS phase-out, the Superfund cap, the wetland restoration, the whistleblower statute, the sound-insulation grant. Every one of those is a genuine good with a mechanism inside it.

## G1. The rise from beneath — emergent groundwater loading a remedy from the wrong side

**What it actually is.** Sea-level rise raises the coastal water table before it overtops anything. Denser seawater pushes inland beneath the ground, lifting the freshwater lens above it; shallow groundwater emerges at grade, floods basements and crawlspaces, infiltrates sewers, and — the load-bearing part — rises *into* contamination that was capped against rainfall coming down. SFEI's Shallow Groundwater Response to Sea Level Rise project (2020–22, Alameda / Marin / San Francisco / San Mateo, Contra Costa following) mapped the "highest annual" shallow water table across the Bay side of five counties and modelled it forward; the response reaches **up to three miles from the shoreline**, and groundwater "can emerge aboveground as an urban flood hazard even before coastal floodwaters overtop the shoreline."

Kristina Hill (UC Berkeley) and Daniella Hirschfeld (Utah State) counted **over 5,200 state- and federally-managed contaminated sites in the setting-a** exposed to this — more than ten times prior estimates — against **326 Superfund sites and an estimated ~115,000 total sites nationally**. The exposed communities named are **West Oakland, the Richmond waterfront, and Bayview–Hunters Point** (Hill & Hirschfeld; see also *Earth's Future* 10.1029/2023EF003825, 2023).

**What it does to a body.** Three routes, all documented. (i) A rising water table intersects a residual solvent plume and drives vapour intrusion upward through a slab — TCE at the short-exposure endpoint that governs the Middlefield-Ellis-Whisman site is a developmental cardiac endpoint with a window measured in weeks, so the exposure that matters is to a first-trimester pregnancy in a ground-floor unit. (ii) Water standing in a yard or crawlspace over a closed municipal dump carries lead, arsenic and PAHs to the surface where children play and where it dries into dust. (iii) Groundwater at or near grade defeats sub-slab depressurisation entirely: a fan cannot depressurise a saturated sub-slab, so the engineering control fails silently while continuing to run and to log its runtime.

**The engine.** Hydraulic head. It is not a decision, it has no operator, it operates continuously on every parcel simultaneously, and it would continue precisely unchanged if every regulator in the state were dissolved tomorrow. It is also monotone on the relevant timescale: multi-century sea-level commitment from emissions already made is the textbook case of a debt already owed and not yet delivered.

**The turn.** *The cap.* Containment-in-place is the modal Superfund remedy and it worked: a clay or geomembrane cap, a Land Use Covenant recorded against the deed, a vapour barrier, a sub-slab fan with an O&M agreement, a five-year review. The remedy's whole logic is that the contamination is *kept where it is* and the pathway is *from above*. It succeeded at both. The water table arriving from underneath is loading the one face the design never had to consider, and every element of the remedy — the low-permeability cap that now traps water and vapour beneath a house, the covenant that fixes the land use so the population above it is stable and known — converts from protection to delivery without anything failing. A second turn is available in the map itself: SFEI's groundwater layer is an unambiguous public good, and it is also what makes 5,200 parcels individually identifiable to an underwriter.

**The institution and its instrument.** San Francisco Bay Regional Water Quality Control Board, Land Disposal Program (solid-waste discharge requirements for closed landfills; it has begun requiring operators to consider rising groundwater). DTSC's draft guidance directing site managers to evaluate rising groundwater at remediated sites. The recorded **Land Use Covenant** and the **five-year review** under CERCLA §121(c) are the artifacts. EnviroStor and GeoTracker are the public registers.

**Premise it would generate.** A five-year review signed off as protective at a closed Bay-margin landfill, on a site where every monitoring well reads clean because the contamination is no longer in the water — it is in the four hundred houses above it.

## G2. Inelastic compaction — ground and storage that do not come back, and the arsenic the squeezing releases

**What it actually is.** Aquifer-system compaction has two regimes. Above the preconsolidation stress, drawdown compresses the elastic skeleton and the ground rebounds when heads recover. Below it, the fine-grained clay and silt interbeds compact **inelastically**: the clay platelets rearrange, pore space is destroyed, and it is permanent. The land does not come back, and — the part usually left out — **the storage does not come back either**. The aquifer permanently loses the capacity to hold water, so every subsequent drought is drawn against a smaller reservoir. A 2026 PNAS analysis of the Sacramento Valley describes an abrupt transition to irreversible damage once the system crosses that threshold `[?]` — I could not retrieve the paper's figures directly (publisher 403), so treat the citation as real and the numbers as unverified.

The body link is **Ryan Smith et al., "Overpumping leads to California groundwater arsenic threat," *Nature Communications* 9:2089 (2018)**: in the Tulare basin, the water squeezed out of compacting clays carries arsenic with it into the pumped aquifer, and subsidence rate is a predictor of arsenic concentration. The land sinking and the arsenic in the tap are the same physical event.

**What it does to a body.** Chronic inorganic arsenic in drinking water: hyperkeratosis of the palms and soles, skin, bladder and lung cancer, peripheral neuropathy, and — the one that fits §0 — measurable deficits in children exposed *in utero* and in early childhood. The exposed population is not urban ratepayers; it is domestic-well households and small water systems that cannot afford treatment, drilling deeper into exactly the compacting interval that releases it. Deeper drilling is the rational response to a falling table and is also the exposure.

**The engine.** Effective stress. A load applied by absence — the water that is no longer there is what is doing the crushing. Population-scale, mechanically exact, entirely indifferent, and *ratcheted*: you cannot un-crush a clay.

**The turn.** *Managed recharge, and the pump tax that paid for it.* Santa Clara Valley is the standing proof that the problem is solvable: downtown San Jose fell about 13 feet between 1910 and 1969, and the fall was **stopped** — by imported water, managed recharge, and a groundwater production charge levied on the pumpers. That is a genuine, audited, complete success, and the district still measures it. The turn is that the success is what makes the *next* basin's failure legible and legally actionable: SGMA (2014, Water Code §10720 et seq.) codified "significant and unreasonable land subsidence" as one of six statutory undesirable results precisely because the Santa Clara case proved it avoidable, so every basin now has a named agency, a plan, a 2040 deadline, and a documented standard it will be found to have breached. The measurement is the liability.

**The institution and its instrument.** Groundwater Sustainability Agencies under SGMA; the extensometer and the InSAR time series; the annual subsidence report; DWR's authority to place a basin on probation. Santa Clara Valley Water District's groundwater production charge.

**Premise it would generate.** A basin's subsidence monitoring network, working perfectly, producing the record that proves the ground and the water in it were spent — filed annually by the agency created to prevent it, on behalf of the households whose wells are the instrument.

**Boundary note.** §5.3 already owns *San Jose's 13 feet*, *the Delta's peat*, and *SGMA as a deadline with a named failure*. What is new here is (a) irreversibility as a physical mechanism rather than a fact, (b) the loss of *storage* as distinct from the loss of *elevation*, and (c) the arsenic pathway that puts a body in it.

## G3. Body burden as archive — PFAS, and clearance by transfer

**What it actually is.** Per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances are effectively non-degrading in the environment and are eliminated from the human body over years, not hours. NHANES 1999–March 2020 finds them in essentially the whole US population; 2017–18 geometric means: **PFOS 4.25 ng/mL, PFOA 1.42, PFHxS 1.08, PFNA 0.411** (ATSDR clinical guidance). They cross the placenta into cord blood and are excreted in breast milk.

The setting-a cohort is exact. The **Women Firefighter Biomonitoring Collaborative** (UC Berkeley, UCSF, Silent Spring Institute) compared **86 women firefighters of the San Francisco Fire Department against 84 women office workers in downtown San Francisco**; of twelve PFAS tested, seven were detected in most participants, and **PFHxS, PFUnDA and PFNA were significantly elevated** in the firefighters (*Environmental Science & Technology*, 26 February 2020). San Francisco is about **15% women** on the fire line against roughly 5% nationally, so the cohort exists at all because the department is unusual. The study was initiated because the women themselves reported what they believed was an elevated rate of breast cancer.

**What it does to a body.** It is in the serum, the liver, the placenta and the milk. The health outcomes are contested in size and not in kind. The mechanism that makes it a Fog Belt engine is the elimination pathway: an adult woman's serum PFAS falls with parity and with months of lactation. **The fastest route by which an adult body clears these compounds is transferring them to a child.** That is not metaphor and it is not disputed; it is why maternal serum concentrations decline across successive pregnancies.

**The engine.** A partition coefficient. Not a policy, not an industry — a molecule that binds serum albumin, is reabsorbed in the proximal tubule, and moves down a concentration gradient into the two tissues a body makes for someone else. It operates on every gestation and every feed, at population scale, and no institution is required.

**The turn.** *The phase-out worked, and biomonitoring is how we know.* PFOS serum concentrations in the US population have fallen steeply and continuously since the 3M phase-out beginning in 2000 — one of the clearest demonstrations in toxicology that regulating a chemical out of commerce shows up in human blood within a decade. **Biomonitoring California** (SB 1379, Chapter 599, Statutes of 2006) is the statutory programme that measures chemicals in Californians and **returns individual results to participants**; its firefighter study (*JOEM* 2015) is part of the same lineage. And in 2025 the **San Francisco Fire Department became the largest US department to convert its whole fleet of turnout gear to non-PFAS**, following the city's move to ban the chemistry in the gear. Everything about this is a success. The turn is that the success makes the body a *dated record*: the falling population curve means a serum result is a timestamp, the cohort exists because the department did the right thing by hiring women, and the individual result-return letter is the instrument that tells a person a number about themselves and about whoever they fed.

**The institution and its instrument.** Biomonitoring California (CDPH / OEHHA / DTSC), and its **individual result-return letter**. CDC/NHANES as the national reference. The SFFD's gear procurement record.

**Premise it would generate.** A biomonitoring cohort in which every result is correct, every letter is accurate, every participant consented, and the panel is legible as a birth-order ledger — the first child carries the department's whole first decade.

**Boundary note.** `setting-a.md` §3 already has the BAAQMD Hot Spots letter — *a letter that arrives at a house and states a number about the people in it*. The distinction to hold: Hot Spots states a **modelled risk from a facility**; a biomonitoring result-return states **a measured quantity inside the addressee's own body**, with no facility, no perimeter and no defendant. Different instrument, different register.

## G4. The dated cell — the bomb pulse, and the window closing

**What it actually is.** Atmospheric nuclear testing roughly **doubled atmospheric ¹⁴C** by the 1963–65 peak; the Partial Test Ban Treaty stopped the input, and the excess has decayed away quasi-exponentially by exchange with the oceans and biosphere ever since. Because every carbon atom a body incorporates comes from the atmosphere via food, the atmospheric curve is written into tissue at the moment that tissue is made. Jonas Frisén's group used this to settle questions anatomy had failed to settle for a century — that adult humans do generate new **cardiomyocytes** (and to constrain neurogenesis) — by measuring the ¹⁴C content of sorted cell nuclei and reading the date off the calibration curve. **Dental enamel**, which is laid down at a known age and never remodels, gives forensic **year of birth to roughly ±1.5 years**; there is a published casework guide for it (ANZPAA, 2022) and a systematic review of its forensic use (*Forensic Science International*, 2025). Much of the biological work runs at the **Center for Accelerator Mass Spectrometry at Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory**.

**What it does to a body.** Nothing, at these doses — and that is the point. The mechanism does not injure; it *inscribes*. Every cell a person has built since about 1955 carries a date, and the date is legible to anyone with 10 mg of tissue and an accelerator.

**The engine.** Isotopic exchange. Global, uniform, unavoidable, complete: there is no unexposed control population born after 1955 anywhere on Earth. The engine ran without an operator from the moment the tests stopped.

**The turn.** *This is the exemplar of the shape.* The largest uncontrolled human experiment ever conducted on the atmosphere became **the most precise instrument cell biology has for asking when a cell was born** — and it did so entirely by accident, with no one's intent and no one's permission. Two further folds are real and available. First: the window is closing. Atmospheric ¹⁴C has now fallen back through pre-bomb levels and is being driven *below* them by fossil-fuel dilution (the Suess effect), so bomb-pulse dating is losing resolution and will soon be useless — and material formed today will radiocarbon-date as though it were centuries old. Second: **the laboratory that runs the biological measurement is a nuclear weapons laboratory in Livermore.** The institution that put the marker in you is the institution that reads it out.

**The institution and its instrument.** LLNL CAMS. The accelerator mass spectrometer, and the published calibration curve.

**Premise it would generate.** A Livermore AMS run on ordinary tissue from an ordinary decedent, correctly calibrated and correctly reported, returning a population of cells whose date of formation is not compatible with the person they were taken from.

## G5. A load with no clearance — microplastic in tissue

**What it actually is.** Nihart, Campen et al., **"Bioaccumulation of microplastics in decedent human brains," *Nature Medicine* (published online 3 February 2025)**, using pyrolysis gas chromatography–mass spectrometry on autopsy tissue: **brain median 4,917 µg/g** (IQR 4,026–5,608) in 2024 decedents, against **3,345 µg/g** in 2016 decedents — a rise of roughly **50% in eight years** (P = 0.01). Liver median 433 µg/g, kidney 404 µg/g. **Polyethylene is ~75%** of the brain load. Particles are overwhelmingly **nanoscale shard-like fragments under 200 nm**. Brains from dementia decedents carried **26,076 µg/g** — direction of causation unestablished, and I flag that honestly.

The setting-a matrix is unusually strong. SFEI and 5 Gyres' **San Francisco Bay Microplastics Project** (2017–2021, ~$1M, Moore Foundation / Patagonia / Ocean Protection Council) found Bay water and sediment among the most microplastic-contaminated measured anywhere, with **an estimated 7 trillion particles a year delivered by urban stormwater** — hundreds of times more than by wastewater — of which **nearly half are tyre wear particles from roads**.

**What it does to a body.** It is in the parenchyma. The blood–brain barrier, which half a billion years of evolution built to exclude foreign matter, is transparent to a material first manufactured in 1907. There is no identified clearance pathway for the sub-200 nm fraction, which is what makes this an accumulation rather than an exposure.

**The engine.** Comminution and inhalation. Tyres abrade against road, stormwater carries the fragments to the Bay, and the finest fraction is respirable. It runs on every vehicle-mile driven and requires no one's decision. Monotone: the 2016 → 2024 slope is the engine's rate.

**The turn.** Weaker than the others and I will not oversell it. Two real ones exist. (i) **The medical examiner's tissue archive.** Formalin-fixed autopsy tissue retained for good forensic and public-health reasons is the only reason the 2016 baseline exists — the trend is measurable solely because a coroner's office kept things it had no intention of using this way. (ii) **The stormwater programme.** Municipal Regional Permit trash-capture requirements, full-capture devices, and green infrastructure are audited, quantified and genuinely working on the visible fraction, and they are sized for what a screen catches; the fraction that reaches tissue is the fraction that passes every device the permit specifies as compliant. A programme in full compliance is a programme that has removed the evidence and not the load.

**The institution and its instrument.** SF Bay RWQCB Municipal Regional Stormwater Permit (trash load reduction schedule). The Office of the Chief Medical Examiner and its tissue retention policy. SFEI as the regional monitor.

**Premise it would generate.** A stormwater programme certifying full trash-capture compliance across a county, and a medical examiner's archive in the same county in which the load in the tissue rises on a straight line through the compliance date.

## G6. Restoration as mobilisation — the mercury that the good work methylates

**What it actually is.** New Almaden (1845–1976) was the largest mercury mine in North America and its output is in Bay sediment permanently. Inorganic mercury sitting in sediment is comparatively inert. **Methylmercury** — the form that biomagnifies and crosses the placenta — is produced by sulfate-reducing bacteria under anoxic conditions, which is exactly the condition created by re-wetting sediment. Tidal marsh restoration therefore *manufactures* the bioavailable species out of the legacy stock. This is not a fringe claim: it is in the environmental documents of the **South Bay Salt Pond Restoration Project** (the largest tidal wetland restoration on the US West Coast), USGS measured **waterbird egg mercury concentrations in response to restoration** in South Bay (OFR 2014-1189), and there is a peer-reviewed literature on **slough evolution and legacy mercury remobilisation induced by wetland restoration in South San Francisco Bay**.

The regulatory frame is the **San Francisco Bay Mercury TMDL**: adopted by the Water Board 2004 and 2006, effective 7 November 2007, EPA-approved 12 February 2008; **fish tissue target 0.2 ppm**; allocations of 11 kg/yr municipal wastewater (final 2028), 1.0 kg/yr industrial, 80 kg/yr urban runoff. And the TMDL's own implementation identifies the tidal marsh and salt pond projects as *the most promising avenue for reducing methylmercury* — the same projects that produce it in the short and medium term.

**What it does to a body.** Methylmercury crosses the placenta and concentrates in the fetal brain, where the effect is on neurodevelopment and is not recoverable. The exposed population is not the general public — it is subsistence and cultural anglers on the piers and their children, the people for whom the OEHHA advisory is written in several languages and stated in **meals per month**. They are the population least able to substitute the protein.

**The engine.** Sulfate reduction. Bacterial, anaerobic, thermodynamically driven, indifferent, and switched on by adding water to sediment. It scales with restored acreage.

**The turn.** *The restoration itself.* This is the cleanest turn in the domain. Tidal marsh restoration is the Bay's flagship environmental good — funded by a voter-approved parcel tax, celebrated, ecologically sound, essential for shoreline resilience against the sea-level rise in G1, and beyond dispute correct. The birds come back. The fish come back. And the mercury that has been lying inert since the nineteenth century is converted into the form that goes into the birds, the fish, and the children, *by the act of restoring*. Nobody is wrong, nothing failed, and the same regulator both sets the fish tissue target and requires the marsh.

**The institution and its instrument.** SF Bay RWQCB (the Mercury TMDL and its NPDES implementation, reissued every five years since 2007). California State Coastal Conservancy / USFWS / CDFW (South Bay Salt Pond Restoration Project). OEHHA (the **fish consumption advisory posted at the pier, in meals per month**). USGS (the waterbird egg monitoring programme).

**Premise it would generate.** A restoration project meeting every ecological performance target it was funded to meet, monitored honestly, with the egg-mercury series and the children's cord blood rising on the same curve as the acreage.

**Boundary note.** §5.3 already holds *contamination that outlives the industry, and a remedy that is a sign at the pier stating how many meals a month a body may take.* That line owns **the advisory**. What is new is **the creation of the exposure by an act of remediation** — the causal arrow from the good deed to the dose.

## G7. The verified record — Hunters Point, and the retest as the release

**What it actually is.** The Hunters Point Naval Shipyard was the Navy's radiological decontamination centre; ships from Operation Crossroads were sandblasted there, and radium-226 deck markers, caesium-137 and strontium-90 are in the fill. Remediation contractor **Tetra Tech EC** falsified soil sampling: supervisors instructed workers to substitute clean soil for samples from potentially contaminated locations. Two supervisors, **Stephen Rolfe and Justin Hubbard, each served eight months in federal prison in 2018**. The company paid **$40 million** to the United States in 2025 for Superfund Act violations, and on **25 August 2026 US District Judge James Donato ordered a further $57 million** in the False Claims Act case, of which **nearly $12 million goes to seven former Tetra Tech workers** who reported it. The Navy has been retesting since the falsification surfaced in 2013; **radioactive material has been found as recently as spring 2026**, and completion is estimated at perhaps another decade. Lennar took the first parcels from 2004; people live and work on the site now.

**What it does to a body.** Radium-226 and its daughters, caesium-137, strontium-90 in surface and near-surface soil in a residential and artist-studio neighbourhood — the pathway is soil ingestion and inhalation of resuspended dust, and strontium-90 substitutes for calcium in bone. The specific cruelty is that the correction is itself the exposure: **retesting means excavation, and excavation means resuspension**, over the same blocks, for a decade, on a schedule published in advance.

**The engine.** Not the contamination — the **verification chain**. Every downstream institution consumed the falsified data as truth and acted correctly on it: the Navy's Finding of Suitability to Transfer, the conveyance, the escrow, the title insurance, the entitlement, the building permit, the occupancy. None of them did anything wrong. The engine is a data-integrity failure propagating through a stack of institutions each of which is designed to trust the layer beneath it, and it runs at population scale because that is how title works.

**The turn.** *The False Claims Act, and the seven people who used it.* The qui tam provision is a real, audited, functioning instrument of accountability; seven workers reported what they saw, DOJ prosecuted, two men went to prison, a court ordered $97 million across two settlements. It worked. And what it produced, for the people actually living on the parcel, is: a decade of excavation, a permanently unmarketable title, a neighbourhood that now knows it was lied to and cannot leave, and a compensation flow that reaches the whistleblowers and the federal Treasury and not one resident. The vindication is the harm's delivery mechanism.

**The institution and its instrument.** US Navy BRAC Program Management Office; US EPA Region 9 (NPL listing); CDPH Radiologic Health Branch; DTSC. The artifacts are the **Finding of Suitability to Transfer** and the **re-survey work plan with its parcel-by-parcel schedule**.

**Premise it would generate.** An honest re-survey, conducted to the letter, that is the largest single release of material the neighbourhood has ever had — and the document that authorises it is a court's remedy.

**Handling note.** This is live litigation about a real, majority-Black neighbourhood with an active environmental justice campaign. §0 governs absolutely: the villain is the apparatus, never the residents, and the story cannot be about the community's credulity. The safest construction puts the reader inside the verification chain — a title officer, a five-year reviewer, a data validator — rather than beside the residents.

## G8. The sealed envelope — nocturnal noise, the easement that buys it, and heat as the second load

**What it actually is.** Transportation noise at night is a measured cardiovascular hazard operating below the threshold of waking. Saucy et al. analysed **24,886 cardiovascular deaths around Zürich Airport, 2000–2015**, and found significant associations between aircraft noise **in the two hours preceding death** and mortality from all cardiovascular causes, strongest for ischaemic heart disease, myocardial infarction, heart failure and arrhythmia; the population-attributable fraction was about **3% of night-time cardiovascular deaths**. Susceptibility was higher in **women**, in areas with **low background noise**, and in **buildings constructed before 1970**. The mechanism is established experimentally: a single night of aircraft noise produces endothelial dysfunction in healthy humans, partially rescued by vitamin C, implicating reactive oxygen species; animal work shows blood pressure elevation and vascular oxidative stress from exposure during the sleep phase only (*European Heart Journal* 42(8):844, 2021).

**What it does to a body.** Sympathetic and HPA-axis activation during sleep, repeated nightly for decades, producing endothelial dysfunction, hypertension and, at the end of the distribution, a death at three in the morning that is attributed to the heart. The exposure does not wake the person. There is no symptom, no complaint, and nothing for the person to notice.

**The engine.** Acoustic energy on a flight schedule. It runs on a published timetable, over a fixed contour, on everyone under it, and it would continue exactly as it is if every noise office in the country closed.

**The turn.** *The residential sound insulation programme.* Under **14 CFR Part 150**, funded through AIP and passenger facility charges and administered at SFO through the **Aircraft Noise Abatement Office** with the **SFO Airport/Community Roundtable** (established 1981), tens of thousands of homes in South San Francisco, San Bruno, Millbrae, Daly City and Pacifica have been acoustically insulated — new windows, sealed envelopes, quantified interior noise-level reduction, verified on completion. It is a real, audited, effective good that measurably improves people's lives.

It also does two other things. First, sealing an envelope for acoustic performance removes its infiltration ventilation, which raises indoor CO₂, PM and radon, and — in a housing stock with almost no mechanical cooling — raises and holds the indoor night-time minimum during heat events, which is the parameter that kills. That is the second load: heat's mortality signal is nocturnal, not diurnal, and coastal California's building stock has neither cooling nor a code requirement for it. **Vecellio et al.** (PSU HEAT Project, *J Appl Physiol* 2022; *ERL* 2023) further found the empirical critical wet-bulb limit for **young, healthy** subjects to be around **30.6–31 °C** in humid conditions, well under the canonical 35 °C — the published survivability threshold was wrong in the optimistic direction, and it is the number that sits in planning documents.

Second, and this is the sharper edge: participation in a sound insulation programme routinely requires the homeowner to **grant an avigation easement over the property**, recorded against the deed `[?]` — standard practice at Part 150 programmes; verify the specific SFO instrument before use. The remedy is exchanged for the permanent extinguishment of the right to object to the thing it remediates.

**The institution and its instrument.** FAA 14 CFR Part 150 (Noise Exposure Maps and Noise Compatibility Programs); AC 150/5000-9B, *Guidelines for Sound Insulation of Residences Exposed to Aircraft Operations*; the **CNEL 65 dB contour** as the eligibility boundary; the SFO Roundtable's quarterly noise reports; the recorded avigation easement.

**Premise it would generate.** The quietest houses under the approach path — sealed, verified, easement-encumbered, and warm — and the people inside them dying at 3 a.m. of something no monitor in the programme is calibrated to record.

## G9. Dense gas at grade — the climate remedy stored under people

**What it actually is.** Geologic carbon sequestration injects supercritical CO₂ into deep saline formations under an EPA **Class VI** Underground Injection Control permit, which carries a default **50-year post-injection site care and monitoring period**. EPA issued its first-ever Class VI permits for carbon sequestration in California. The transport leg is the hazard. On **22 February 2020 a Denbury CO₂ pipeline ruptured at Satartia, Mississippi**, releasing for roughly four hours from 7:06 p.m.: **over 200 people evacuated, at least 45 hospitalised**, with respiratory distress, disorientation, loss of consciousness, convulsive shaking and cardiac complications. CO₂ is colourless, denser than air, and pooled in the valley for hours rather than dispersing. **Cars stopped running**, because combustion engines need atmospheric oxygen — so people could not drive out and responders could not drive in. Denbury failed to notify authorities for over 40 minutes and paid a **$2.8 million** PHMSA penalty, the second largest in that agency's history. Survivors three years later reported headaches, concentration difficulties, muscle tremors and worsened asthma.

California's response is **SB 905 (Caballero, 2022)**, which created CARB's Carbon Capture, Removal, Utilization and Storage Program and imposed a **moratorium on CO₂ pipeline transport until PHMSA updates its federal regulations**. The federal rule that would lift the moratorium has been withdrawn `[?]`, which leaves California with a ban whose expiry condition may never occur.

**The duration form** — and this is the version that satisfies §0 rather than the Satartia event. Class VI's whole regulatory apparatus exists because injected CO₂ that migrates upward into an underground source of drinking water **acidifies it and mobilises metals** — arsenic and lead among them — from the aquifer matrix. The failure mode that the fifty-year monitoring period is designed to catch is not an explosion. It is a slow, silent, chemical change in the water above the plume, in a body of rock nobody can see, monitored by an obligation that outlives the corporate entity that signed it.

**What it does to a body.** Two registers, both physical. Acute: CO₂ at 7–10% causes unconsciousness within a minute; it is not an inert asphyxiant but an active agent driving hyperventilation, acidosis and convulsion, and it lies in low ground where children and the elderly are. Chronic: metals mobilised out of an aquifer into a municipal or domestic supply, on a timescale of decades, arriving as an epidemiological signal in a population that was told the storage was permanent.

**The engine.** Density and solubility. CO₂ is heavier than air and it forms carbonic acid in water. Both properties are the reason it is a good storage medium and the reason it is a hazard; they are the same properties.

**The turn.** *Carbon sequestration.* It is the audited, permitted, monitored, incentivised, unambiguously necessary climate remedy — and the thing being permanently stored under a community is a substance that suffocates at grade and dissolves rock. The permit that authorises it is also the document that concedes it: Class VI's fifty-year post-injection care period is a regulator writing down, in advance, that this is expected to need watching for longer than the operator will exist. And SB 905's moratorium is an even better artifact — a protection that switches itself off automatically upon the issuance of a federal rule, whoever issues it and whatever it says.

**The institution and its instrument.** US EPA UIC Class VI (the permit, the area of review, the post-injection site care and site closure plan, the financial assurance instrument). PHMSA (49 CFR Part 195). CARB under SB 905. The **State Fire Marshal**, proposed under AB 2623 (2024) as the state standard-setter.

**setting-a matrix.** The refineries at Martinez and Rodeo and the cement and biomass sources are the region's capture candidates; the storage is elsewhere and the transport crosses the Delta and the Central Valley. The regional shape is the familiar one: the setting-a's decarbonisation is discharged as a physical hazard laid across somebody else's ground, exactly as its water is.

**Premise it would generate.** A fifty-year post-injection monitoring obligation, correctly financially assured, being discharged by the fourth successor entity to hold it, over an aquifer whose metals series has been rising within permit limits for thirty years.

# 2. THE REJECTS

Ordered roughly by how close they came.

**Radon and NORM.** *(Closest reject.)* Passes A decisively — radon is the second leading cause of lung cancer in the US after smoking, EPA action level 4 pCi/L, and the exposure is chronic, domestic, invisible and lifelong. Passes B, C and E. It fails **F and duplication**: most of the setting-a is EPA Radon Zone 3 (lowest predicted potential), CGS's detailed radon potential mapping is concentrated elsewhere in the state, and — decisively — `setting-a.md` §3 already owns the **sub-slab depressurisation fan that must run forever** through the MEW TCE vapour-intrusion entry. Radon would be the same room with a different gas. Held in reserve; if used, it must be for the thing TCE cannot do, which is that radon has **no source, no responsible party and no defendant** — it comes out of the ground because the ground is granite.

**ShakeAlert and earthquake early warning.** The figures are excellent and the horror is real: alert times range from a few seconds to tens of seconds, the 50–80 second warnings are available **only to users far from the epicentre**, and the USGS states plainly that "if the earthquake begins directly below you… [you have] little or no warning." For a Hayward rupture, the two million people directly over the trace are inside the late-alert zone *by construction* — the system's coverage is inversely proportional to need. But it fails **B** (the payload is an event, which §0 forbids) and **C** (there is no engine; a notification system is not a process operating on bodies). **Salvage:** it is a first-rate §4 artifact — the alert itself, with its ~0 second delivery — and a first-rate §3.14 *interval*. §5.3 already owns the fault, the recurrence arithmetic and HayWired.

**Atmospheric rivers and the megaflood scenario.** ARkStorm and ARkStorm 2.0 (Huang & Swain, *Science Advances* 2022) are event-shaped and fail **B**. They also duplicate §5.3's *disaster with published arithmetic and no date* almost exactly. **Salvage:** the turn is genuinely strong and orphaned — **Forecast-Informed Reservoir Operations at Lake Mendocino** (Sonoma Water with Scripps CW3E) is an audited, independently confirmed success in which trusting a forecast let a reservoir hold more water without raising flood risk. That is a working good looking for a load; give it to a different engine.

**Volcanic and caldera monitoring.** Long Valley's colour-coded alert-level scheme and the California Volcano Observatory's notice apparatus are lovely bureaucracy, and the 1982 Long Valley hazard-notice episode is a real case study in a warning that damaged a town economically without an eruption. Fails **B** (event), **C** (no engine on bodies) and **F** (not setting-a). **Salvage:** the alert level as an artifact — a colour that changes a town's insurance without anything happening.

**Induced seismicity.** The setting-a case is beautiful and I wanted it to work: Santa Rosa's treated municipal wastewater is piped roughly 40 miles to **The Geysers** geothermal field and injected, and the injection demonstrably raises the seismicity rate — a city's sewage producing earthquakes in another county, monitored by LBNL and the field operator. It fails **A**: the events are small, and the harm is cracked plaster and annoyance. **Salvage:** superb texture, and a real §2.8 complicity image (your shower causes their earthquakes).

**Geomagnetic field, excursions and GIC.** Fails **A** directly — geomagnetically induced currents damage transformers, not people. The only route to a body is via grid loss, and §5.3 already owns that with PSPS and the Medical Baseline allowance, better and more specifically. NERC TPL-007 is a genuine institution with a genuine instrument (the benchmark GMD event and the transformer thermal assessment), but it is guarding equipment.

**Deep carbon, methane reservoirs and clathrate stability.** Fails **A**, **C** and **E**. It also fails on the evidence: the current synthesis (Ruppel & Kessler, *Reviews of Geophysics* 2017) is that catastrophic hydrate release is not supported — dissociating hydrate methane is largely oxidised in the sediment and water column before reaching the atmosphere. Using it would require inventing the mechanism, which spends the one impossibility on something that produces no scene. **Rejected outright.** The deep-carbon territory is better served by G9, where the carbon is going *down* on purpose.

**Megadrought and paleoclimate records.** Williams et al. (*Science* 2020; *Nature Climate Change* 2022) establish that 2000–2021 was the driest 22-year period in southwestern North America in at least 1,200 years, with a large anthropogenic contribution. Fails **A** — it is a fact about landscape. **Salvage:** the tree-ring chronology as an artifact — a record that retroactively reveals the twentieth-century baseline on which every water right in the state was allocated to have been anomalously wet.

**Glacier and snowpack loss.** Fails **A**. The April 2026 survey found **no measurable snow at the Phillips Station course after a record hot, dry March** — a striking image and still a storage-accounting question, not a body. Its only route to a body is through drinking water, where G2 does the job better and with an actual toxicant.

**The ozone layer.** Correctly identified as a turn-shaped object, and I am rejecting it as a *candidate* while keeping it as the reference shape. The Montreal Protocol worked: the 2025 Antarctic ozone hole was small and short-lived and the WMO reports recovery on track to 1980 levels by mid-century. But there is no engine and no body — the harm was delivered decades ago and is not recoverable, which makes it a fact rather than a process. **Use it as the exemplar of D**, and note the one genuinely uncomfortable fold if it is ever needed: the recovery has itself shifted Southern Hemisphere atmospheric circulation, so undoing the damage changes the weather too.

**The Anthropocene stratigraphic marker.** The Anthropocene Working Group's Crawford Lake GSSP proposal — with the 1952 plutonium fallout spike as the primary marker in varved lake sediment — was **rejected by the Subcommission on Quaternary Stratigraphy in early 2024** and the vote upheld. Fails **A** and **C**. **Salvage:** an outstanding artifact. A sediment core in which the boundary between two epochs is a bomb, formally proposed, formally voted on, and formally refused — with the refusal being a matter of stratigraphic procedure rather than of fact. That is §4 and §2.16 (classification as the operative act) material, not an engine.

**Liquefaction and made ground.** §5.3 owns it outright — *made ground remembers what it was made of* is already the line, with the Marina and the Cypress Structure under it. Nothing to add that is not duplication.

**Slow-moving landslides and creep.** Portuguese Bend is the national exemplar (movement reaching several inches to a foot per week in 2024, NASA InSAR mapping, a municipal buyout programme, and a community being pulled apart at walking pace) but it is Southern California. The setting-a's creep is Hayward's ~5 mm/yr, which §5.3 owns with California Memorial Stadium's separation joint — and that entry is already the best possible statement of the shape. Fails **F** by duplication.

**Infrasound.** Fails **A** on the evidence. Successive systematic reviews of wind-turbine infrasound find no supported health effect at real-world exposure levels; the reproducible finding is that *expectation* produces symptoms. That is a genuinely interesting phenomenon, but it belongs to the psychology bank, not this one, and building on it would require an impossibility spent on a mechanism the reader cannot picture.

**Light pollution.** Real and non-trivial — IARC classifies shift work involving circadian disruption as **Group 2A probably carcinogenic**, melatonin suppression is a documented mechanism, and ALAN–breast cancer meta-analyses find a small positive dose–response. It fails §0's explicit floor: *if the harm is small enough to be an epidemiological finding, it is not this register.* **Salvage:** as a second load on G8's sealed-and-lit envelope, or as texture.

**Saltwater intrusion.** In the Delta this is a water-supply and agricultural problem; the body is several removes downstream, so it fails **A** as a standalone. **Salvage:** the **emergency drought salinity barrier at West False River** (installed 2015, again June–July 2021, removed October 2022) is an excellent artifact — an emergency rock dam thrown across a navigable channel to keep the ocean off the export pumps, built and removed on the Department of Water Resources' schedule. And the intrusion mechanism is a supporting driver for G1.

**Concrete and steel degradation.** Chloride-induced rebar corrosion and alkali-silica reaction are genuine, cumulative, irreversible and institutionally administered (Caltrans and the National Bridge Inventory condition ratings on a 24-month inspection cycle; ASCE's 2025 California report card). It fails **A/B**: the body arrives only at collapse, which is an event. **Salvage:** §5.3 already does this job better — *settlement readings filed on schedule, and owners who can no longer sell* at 301 Mission — and that is exactly the right form for a declining condition rating.

**Urban heat island and wet-bulb limits, standalone.** Folded into G8 rather than rejected. Standalone they fail **B** (heat waves are events) and, in the setting-a specifically, **F**: the fog belt is the region's free air conditioning and the regional heat signal is weak relative to inland California. As a *second load on a sealed envelope with no cooling*, they work.

# 4. CLOSING NOTE — WHAT THIS DOMAIN UNIQUELY SUPPLIES, AND WHERE IT SITS

**What it supplies that no other bank does: the already-done.** §0 asks for *already done, at scale, and not reversible*, and most domains have to argue for that property. Here it is a physical fact with a number attached. Inelastic compaction cannot be undone. A ¹⁴C atom in a neuron is dated and cannot be re-dated. Multi-century sea-level commitment is owed and merely undelivered. Nanoplastic under 200 nm has no clearance pathway. PFAS leaves an adult body chiefly by entering a child's. These are not trends or risks; they are ledger entries that have already been posted, and the story's present tense is the interval before settlement. That is the exact temporal shape §0 asks for and this domain is the only one that supplies it as measured quantity rather than as assertion.

**Second: it supplies the turn in bulk.** Environmental regulation is the field's great success story, and each success is structurally a Fog Belt turn already: the cap that worked, the phase-out that shows in the blood, the marsh that came back, the qui tam that jailed two men, the insulation that made the bedroom quiet, the sequestration that takes the carbon out of the air. All real. All audited. All not in doubt. In every case the mechanism of the good is the mechanism of the harm, and no one has to be lying. G6 is the purest instance — the restoration methylates the mercury — and G4 is the purest statement of the shape, because the most precise instrument cell biology has for dating a human cell is nuclear fallout, and the laboratory that runs it is a weapons laboratory in Livermore.

**Third: it supplies the body at the right scale.** §0 demands enormity and forbids the epidemiological finding. This domain hands over 5,200 sites, an entire national population's serum, every cell formed on Earth since 1955, 7 trillion particles a year, and a fifty-year monitoring obligation. Nothing here is a fraction.

**Where it sits in the playbook's structure.**

- **§5.3 gains a companion, not an extension.** The nine candidates do not fit inside *Water, fire, ground* — that section is about land as an asset. What they want is a sibling domain under §5, provisionally **"What the ground puts into people"** or **"Body burden and the substrate"**, holding G1–G7 and G9. It would be the section that carries the geology bank's contribution to §2.14 (the body as material) and §2.20 (substance, residue, and what the room smells like), and it is the natural home for the region's biomonitoring, its fill, its fallout and its remediation.
- **§4 gains four artifacts** with no current home: the **individual biomonitoring result-return letter**, the **five-year review that finds a remedy protective**, the **re-survey work plan with its parcel schedule**, and the **recorded avigation easement**. Add, from the rejects, the **~0-second ShakeAlert notification**, the **rejected Anthropocene golden spike**, and the **colour-coded volcano alert level**.
- **§3 gains one mechanism that is not yet stated.** The playbook has *care that is capture* (3.8) and *correctness as the wound* (3.3), but not the specific form these candidates keep producing: **remediation as delivery** — the harm arriving *because* the thing was fixed, on the schedule of the fix, funded by the fix, and documented in the fix's own compliance record. G1, G6, G7, G8 and G9 are five independent instances of it. That is enough recurrence to be a family rather than a coincidence, and it is the single most useful thing this domain has to give.
