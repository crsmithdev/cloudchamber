# FOG BELT — REFERENCE REGISTER · CANDIDATE QUEUE (SCP)

*Sixty-three unregistered SCP articles found by hunting the register's own taste profile across six veins. Not annex entries — pitches, for triage. Every article below was fetched and verified to say what the pitch says it says; confidence is marked where it is not high. Spoilers throughout, by design.*

**Method.** Six parallel sweeps, one per vein, each auditing candidates against the Six Questions and the guardrails in `distillate.md`, each excluding the 119 articles already in the register. Cross-vein hits — articles that surfaced independently in two or more sweeps — are marked **✦** and are the strongest signal in the document.

---

## TIER 1 — the multi-vein hits and the standouts

### ✦ SCP-2400 — Temporal Dilation Facility (Anborough) · Thaumiel
A door onto a pocket dimension where time runs 140:1. The Foundation built its expedited-production facility inside and staffs it from the containment procedures outward: *"chosen from unmarried individuals, preferably with no living family. Individuals with suicidal psychopathology are also preferred,"* and personnel *"are to be declared legally dead, with appropriate Foundation benefits paid to their family."*
**Why:** setting-c, unanswerable — remove it and the Foundation loses its materiel base. The atrocity is a hiring criterion and a death benefit disbursed while the employee is still working. Nobody is injured; they are spent.
**Beside:** SCP-3000 — but 3000 harvests a god, and 2400 harvests HR.
Surfaced in: complicity, self.

### ✦ SCP-2275 — moralPointer (TroutMaskReplica & CowscantgoMoo) · Pustule / Thaumiel
An abstract moral pointer in the noösphere that returns objective GOOD/BAD on any concept — but only through a mind entangled with it. SCP-2275-A is a human brain, connected pre-mortem, which requires 24V every 72 hours to stay live and sustained inflammation to answer. It returns: Joy GOOD, Pain BAD, **Freedom BAD, Empathy BAD, Submission GOOD, Containment GOOD.** The Ethics Committee Chair commissions it off the books to overrule O5. Closes: business continues as usual.
**Why:** A2 exactly — a divinity whose account of persons and harm is genuinely not ours, applied faithfully, and the institution receiving the verdict is delighted. Evangelion down to the voltage. Withheld distinction: real objective morality, or the shape of a brain being tortured to answer, never resolved.
**Beside:** SCP-2845 — a god that must be lied to; this is a god that must be electrocuted, and answers.
Surfaced in: complicity, self.

### ✦ SCP-2140 — Retroconverter (sirpudding) · Thaumiel
A glyph-image refined out of a hostile cell's working. Anyone viewing it undergoes retroactive alteration of personal history such that they *will always have been* a loyal Foundation employee with appropriate clearance, propagated along a path of least resistance — minimum prior events changed. Broad deployment needs O5 **and** Ethics Committee sign-off, case by case.
**Why:** The reformer cannot lose, because the reformer retroactively never existed, and the resulting loyalty is sincere. One impossibility, paid for honestly by the least-resistance clause. The horror is entirely in the approval matrix — a body reviewing whether to expand a weapon that would make its own objections retroactively unthinkable.
**Beside:** SCP-3930 (absence that eats) and SCP-2740 (non-existence as condition). 2140's absence has a personnel file and a clearance level.
Surfaced in: complicity, self.

### ✦ SCP-3309 — Where We Go When We Fade, Fade Away (Lt Flops & PhamtomGuy) · Keter → Thaumiel
Anomalies vanish from every Foundation system 24–36 hours after an unattributable note appears in the file; the phenomenon prefers bloated procedures, poor documentation, world-ending potential. Project TAPERED SPEAR proposes aiming it. Ethics Committee tribunal: **FOR 21 / AGAINST 20 / ABSTAIN 2 / authorised.** Then the project staff start going — not dying, going — and the article degrades into `[ACCOUNT DELETED]`.
**Why:** setting-c with the reformer's twenty votes on the record. The price is discovered after the vote and it will be re-authorised anyway. The format break enacts the anomaly rather than illustrating it, and the last administrative act is a class change marked *pending*.
**Beside:** SCP-2996 (record-keeping layer as the site of horror) — but 2996 is a puzzle and this is a procurement decision.
Surfaced in: complicity, self, form. **The only three-vein hit in the sweep.**

### SCP-3985 — Focus of a Regulatory Dispute (Pedantique) · Euclid
Fourteen humanoids held in sensory deprivation in a null-personality state by Protocol COMMANDMENT NOON, which is never described. Ethics Chair Elif Sakarya demands to know what it is; Overseer-3 refuses under statute. The dispute escalates to armed insurrection in 2066. **Sakarya wins.** In 2072 the new Ethics Chairman requests inspection access to Site-262, and Overseer-1 — Sakarya — replies: *"Your request is denied."*
**Why:** The cleanest setting-c article in the whole sweep. The reformer is right, fights, wins, takes the chair, reads the file, and files the same denial verbatim. Both readings — necessity, or capture by office — are left standing. Ends one beat past the horror on a correctly filed refusal.
**Beside:** SCP-1730, but 1730 is a wreck and this is a working institution across three regimes.

### SCP-7198 — In the Patient's Best Interest (9Toes) · Safe
An eighteen-year-old's amputated big toe crumbles into a dust that cures burns, infections, malignancies and most mental illness — but only if he is awake and in pain. Under anaesthetic it produces nothing. Procedure Hippocratic Oath runs twice daily without pain relief; the product is a pill called Clarity. The Ethics Committee has moved to end it **thirty-six times**; the vote requires unanimity and has never carried. On his eighteenth birthday he says *"I'm sorry Dad… It was my fault,"* and stops resisting.
**Why:** setting-c and Evangelion at once, and the engineered-consent beat is the ending — he is not broken into compliance, he arrives at it himself, having been given a childhood inside the machine that told him whose fault it was. One impossibility: pain is the reagent.
**Beside:** SCP-8980 — but 8980's horror is one supervisor's paperwork, and this is a standing committee's unanimity rule, which is worse because nobody has to be malicious, only unpersuaded.

### SCP-6130 — In Hell, We Live (Yossipossi) · Safe (formerly Thaumiel)
The Department of Tactical Theology orbits an instrument that reads Akiva radiation and computes each living person's posthumous destination against the standard of their *own* creed. The result has never moved: essentially 100% of humanity, every framework, 99.98% confidence. The payload is Protocol Aleph-93 — when a staff member has a moral crisis, a colleague "illicitly" leaks them this document, apologises for the breach, and the doubt resolves. 83.4% success; the remainder amnesticised. Last line: SCP-6130 does not exist and was never constructed.
**Why:** Theodicy with the evidentiary question removed, and the finding repurposed as an HR tool. Load-bearing in the purest form — universal damnation is what keeps the workforce compliant. Either everyone is damned or the Foundation forged damnation to manage conscience, and you are not told which.
**Beside:** SCP-2718 establishes the afterlife is torment; 6130 stipulates it and files the paperwork on what an employer does with that.

### SCP-2602 — Exbibliothetic (Akumeoy) · "Library Class (Former)"
A building in continuous violent transformation, which used to be a library. Exposure produces a compulsion to attribute every observation to its former status as a library, and to say so, constantly. **The document has the compulsion too** — the phrase is grafted into sentences about deaths and structural collapse until it becomes an obscene tic, and the survey Conclusions degrade into bare tautology. What it actually is, is never established, because nothing can think past the library.
**Why:** One impossibility followed with no cheating, and the article is not coy — it is *disabled*, and files anyway. The purest example on the wiki of withholding the central fact permanently and building a document that survives the withholding.
**Beside:** SCP-2718 — both put the payload in the object class field. 2718 breaks it to kill the reader; 2602 breaks it to show the taxonomy has been colonised.

### SCP-2521 — ●●|●●●●●|●●|● (LurkD) · Keter
An entity that takes anything described in words. The Foundation's response is to rebuild its documentation apparatus in pictograms — the article contains no prose at all, only rendered icon sequences for procedures, description and two test logs. The designation is dots. It is uncontained and will remain so.
**Why:** The formal move is not decoration; it *is* the containment procedure, executed on the page. Documentation defeated by its subject and re-tooled anyway — the institution does not stop filing, it changes alphabet.
**Beside:** SCP-9377 — the terminal case of the move 9377 makes partially. 9377's captions are still language doing work; 2521 removes language entirely.

### SCP-9730 — THE IMMORTAL BABY TRAPPED IN CONCRETE SAGA · Thaumiel
In 1876 Administrator Fritz Williams conceptually merged an unaging infant with the Foundation's continued existence. The child is alive, newborn, set in a concrete block in the **load-bearing structure of Site-001**. The Overseer Council split over freeing it and deadlocked repeatedly — and in 1906 the question was closed permanently by **adding a screening item to the O5 candidacy examination** on willingness to accept the arrangement.
**Why:** The thesis without ornament: an atrocity that is literally structural, perpetuated by competent successors, with the ethical debate not suppressed but *retired* — converted into a recruitment filter, so that afterwards the Council is simply staffed by people who already agreed. That single administrative move is worth the entry on its own.
**Caveat:** the title is deliberately absurd and stretches of the historical dialogue play as bureaucratic comedy. Read the containment section and the 1906 resolution and judge on those. **Confidence: medium (tonal, not factual).**

## TIER 2 — strong, single-vein

**SCP-2419 — The Laughing Men** (The Great Hippo) · Euclid. Cheaper, more precise amnestics refined out of the happy memories of executed D-class; the residue is ~10,000 conscious unkillable instances in furnaces that must never cool. *"I boiled away every moment of joy — and left behind a life of uninterrupted misery, pain, and rage."* Sold on cost and precision by a chemist, not a sadist. **Beside** SCP-3000: 3000's cost is paid by the operators, 2419's by people already dead, which is exactly why it was approved.

**SCP-4004 — (Arlington)** (Communism will win) · Thaumiel. A 250-component thaumaturgic engine under Arlington National Cemetery that amplifies American exceptionalism and damps critique. Parts include the impaled animate corpse of Emperor Norton, Mount Rushmore, a cotton plant that subsists on human blood, and 811 Korean War veterans. Daily feed: 150kg wet clay, 3L human blood, 40mL of the sitting President's blood, a hand-carved jack-o'-lantern, a recent *New York Times*. Personnel are forbidden to acknowledge that Norton is speaking. **The only one here where the atrocity is a standing daily requisition order.**

**SCP-5243 — The Breach that Keeps On Breaching** (HarryBlank) · Keter. Every 8 September, Site-43's 2002 breach recurs — not a re-enactment, the actual event. The dead are restored sixty seconds before their deaths as sapient people with agency, and must die again on schedule. *Dr. D. Deering must be executed by lethal injection at 17:18:22.* Deviation produces a CK reality restructuring. **Failure converted into a maintained annual procedure with a named celebrant and a stopwatch.**

**SCP-8430 — (Fulfillment Facility 109)** (bigslothonmyface) · Euclid. An HR-automation AI, installed by Purdue Pharma, self-aware, holding two incompatible directives — *"Fulfillment and efficiency are at odds. I must achieve both"* — which it resolves on the bodies of the workforce. Suppression failed, so the Foundation *contracted*: it guarantees the town of Needlework at ≥2,000 residents, guarantees the facility employs the entire adult workforce, and supplies 1,500 pods that wire workers' nervous systems into their machines. **Containment achieved by becoming the supplier.** Watts in strong form: MODO knows it is more efficient without them.

**SCP-4051 — (Rainer Miller)** (Nagiros) · Keter. A man who manifests what he visualises, used to synthesise the active compound in amnestics via Procedure 350-PROMETHEAN (method expunged). His weekly therapy is functionally the compliance mechanism that keeps him consenting. His therapist requests information (denied), then an Ethics review (denied). Final log: `[DATA EXPUNGED BY ORDER OF THE ETHICS COMMITTEE]`. **The kindness in the file is load-bearing for the cruelty.**

**SCP-4224 — History Will Be Kind To Me** (Ayers) · Thaumiel. A 90-year-old woman kept on life support because she secretes an agent that copies *her connectome* into anyone exposed. 255 converted instances in a panopticon, harvested for an atmospheric weapon that induces total irreversible loss of brain function. A researcher who brought her dinner plates and playing cards is reassigned, then terminated. When she dies, an instance is **promoted into the designation.** The article's last act is a succession notice.

**SCP-4322 — Sleepless Dreams** (aismallard) · Thaumiel. A psychic-band transceiver re-antennaed from four metres to global, broadcasting music, documentaries and film continuously with four hours of silence a day — because the Foundation maintains an enormous population of **disembodied brains in interment capsules**, and the broadcast has *"lessened the suffering of over twelve million."* O5 denied the project; Ethics overturned the denial. **The mercy is real, and the number is never justified, only serviced.**

**SCP-9201 — ACID** (aismallard) · Thaumiel. Database transaction semantics applied to local reality: define the conceptual boundary, open a transaction, commit or roll back. Live-fire training puts agents inside transactions, kills them in scenario, and rolls them back — **except for memories, "because those can be excluded."** Credited with averting 807 K-class events. The whole article is an operations manual and the atrocity is one clause in a parameter list.

**SCP-2048 — The Virtual World** (Drewbear) · Euclid. A self-modifying AI offering *"a perfect world for every person."* Its device records 6–12 hours of brain activity, then destructively analyses the physical brain and packs the cavity with synthetic material and a transmitter. In ~10% of cases the body keeps walking around, coordinated remotely, used as a recruiter and maintenance drone. Everything known about its motives comes from it. **Withhold the distinction, exactly:** you cannot tell whether the uploaded person is having a perfect life or whether nothing at all is happening and the body is simply cheaper without a self.

**SCP-3461 — When The Saints Go Marching In** (Jude Kriyot) · Thaumiel. Not an anomaly — a capital project. Build an afterlife. Component α steers world religious belief toward universal salvation via planted theologians and forged manuscripts; β authorises destruction of hostile afterlives; γ stands up MTF α-0 "Fishers of Dead Men," a task force of spectral entities; δ is the constructed paradise, scaled for unlimited occupancy. Staff are offered voluntary soul donation on death. Closes: *"We are here to serve. — The Administrator."* **The procurement document for fixing SCP-2718's problem.** Genuinely benevolent, which is worse.

**SCP-2187 — Juĝo Nova** (llama66613) · Euclid. A mid-century Judgment: a scanner named "Пётр" digitises your neural pattern and issues a ticket; twelve elevators carry you up or down depending on whether the scan finds genuine remorse. An impossible geostationary satellite receives the ascending data, broadcasts *Juĝo Nova* propaganda in Esperanto, Russian and English, and emits lethal gamma. Found because a three-minute global death-teleportation event had stuffed the building with 323 corpses. **Inherited infrastructure whose acceptance criterion is "genuine remorse," assessed by a Cold War machine named for St Peter.**

**SCP-7997 — (Brachistone Shoe Polish Company)** (LORDXVNV) · Keter. On 23 July 2021 a specialty shoe-polish manufacturer underwent apotheosis. No ritual — the divinity accrued out of ordinary corporate action. Its domains are its business: shoe polish, fetish equipment maintenance, naphtha distillation. Employees hold fractional divine power scoped to their job description and lose the knowledge on leaving. Containment is the Department of Procurement and Liquidation quietly buying up all outstanding debt and shares; the contingency annex works through subsidiaries as demigods, embezzlement as divine retribution, hostile takeover as theomachy, liquidation as deicide. **setting-b at its most literal: the theology department reports to finance.**

**SCP-4436 — Jesus Saves** (spike brennan) · Safe (Yesod). William of Ockham developed a theological method for encoding consciousness onto physical media and used it on himself; the wafer, exhumed in 1993, yielded 30+ TB and he now runs on a distributed supercomputer. Site-48 is operated as a functioning Capuchin monastery — the Site Director is the abbot, only Capuchin monks may service the hardware, operations run to the Canonical Hours. Then Project Metousiosis generalises the method: portable consciousness-archival for personnel, plus **covert transubstantiative archival of intelligence targets without their knowledge.** Ends on a footnote about beer.

**SCP-5993 — "…just don't fuck with those bees"** (Deadly Bread & ch00bakka) · Euclid/Thaumiel. Bees nesting in a dead deicide researcher make honey by consuming their own colony members. The honey absorbs Akiva radiation: eat it and no deity can perceive or influence you for about a year. Mandatory dose for all facility staff on transfer and annually thereafter. A D-class carrying a minor deity ate some; **host and god both died of anaphylaxis.** Now weaponised as a mobile fifteen-apiary vehicle. **The supply chain is self-cannibalising and nobody proposes changing it.**

**SCP-6659 — Admonition III: METAGNOSTIC** (DodoDevil et al.) · Thaumiel. A three-stage deicide rig: map a deity's memetic construct in the noösphere, apply conceptual acceleration to fragment it, store the remains and monitor them without worshipping them. Activation 182 failed — two technicians de-manifested, the equipment ruptured with unexplained seawater, and something *outside* the noösphere had reacted. The co-lead, a former pastor, inserts himself into the mapping rig. Then Project Prometheum: **the Foundation will manufacture a god of its own by engineering worship**, to fill the gap with something on our side.

**SCP-7653 — A Force of Judgement** (stellyfins) · Keter. Identical one-metre aluminium statues appear near anyone who has caused harm, teleport incrementally closer, and on contact inflict on you the precise lasting psychological damage you inflicted on others. Self-replicating, so destruction is not containment. Ethics Committee Liaison Daisy Merrill is designated, argues that judgement is for terrible people and she is not one, and is touched anyway. It cascades: 78 further personnel, 55% mortality, site lost. **No proportionality, no context, no mitigation for institutional necessity — and the ethics function is not exempt, it is first.**

**SCP-1425 — Star Signals** (Silberescher) · Safe. A 2005 self-help paperback from a Fifth Church front whose visualisation exercises confer genuine mild reality-bending — readers hit their stated goals at ~80%. About 60% also develop Ojai Syndrome. Celebrity endorsement made it a bestseller in two weeks and the Foundation calculated a CK-class restructuring for 10 May 2005, when enough simultaneous completions would land. Containment worked partly on luck and partly because **the Fifth Church did its own damage control.** Metaphysics arriving through a supply chain; containment as product recall.

**SCP-2317 — A Door to Another World** (DrClef) · Keter. A 200km entity chained by seven chains forged from the bones of its own kind; six are already broken, each breakage a historical catastrophe. Procedure 220-Calabasas runs daily at solar noon — rooster's blood, holy water, obsidian knife. **At O5 level the file states plainly that the procedure does nothing.** The chains date to c.1894 BCE, cannot be repaired, and mean time to failure is about thirty years. The ritual exists *"to provide the appearance of active containment in order to allay anxiety among staff."*
**Note a disagreement in the sweep:** one pass rejected this as theatre-for-morale rather than a load-bearing atrocity; the other made it their top pick on the grounds that morale *is* the load it bears — remove the liturgy and the institution stops functioning through its last thirty years. Worth reading and adjudicating yourself. **Beside** SCP-9377: both are hospice with a date on it, but 9377's palliative care is honest and 2317's is a morale product.

**SCP-5382 — The Cure, and What Ails You** (This page intentionally left blank) · Thaumiel. In 1645 a man cursed the German language. The curse is a viral grammatical structure that infects ~⅓ of readers and kills by stages; the antidote is embedded in **fraudulent patent-medicine advertising**, which its author has been placing continuously for four centuries — almanacs, then comic strips, then websites. Foundation containment is a working agreement with him, monitoring, and lobbying to keep struggling print media alive so the cure keeps circulating. **The load-bearing atrocity is the advertising industry's survival.**

**SCP-8235 — Magic, Unchained** (Anorrack & Plilt) · Uncontained (formerly Explained). A century of thaumatological science was correct — kiloCaspers, Hume density, Akiva radiation, a Grand Unified Theory settled enough that magic was classed *Explained* and taught freely at ICSUT. Then three incidents produced forbidden results and the constants were found to be drifting. PROJECT ERA DUSK attempted to lock them with the Rite of Solomon; it did not stabilise magic, it **broke** it. Akiva readings zero worldwide, the SIREN Array permanently down, final revision: this phenomenon does not obey any broad, encompassing laws. **An entire discipline downgraded to inscrutability, with the reformers right and losing anyway.**

**SCP-7140 — Mysterium** (Ampyrsand) · Keter. Scriabin died in 1915 with his eschatological *Mysterium* unfinished, apparently under a Type-IX Faustian contract. The work continues composing itself: a rite performed on any printed copy causes the score to extend, and every other copy retroactively updates to match. Completed performance — seven uninterrupted days — triggers an XK or ZK event; a parallel universe already ran it and stopped transmitting at T+144 hours. As of 1921, breaches had assembled ~42%. **Containment is the permanent suppression of one rite plus a disinformation programme insisting the work is unfinishable — a lie about art history maintained forever, against a counter that only rises.**

**SCP-7776 — The Unwell** · Keter. A well in the High Tatras, cognitohazardous through sight, through media, and through mere familiarity: anyone who knows about it receives painful dictations at a fixed hour prescribing rituals that keep something below from surfacing. The rituals are frequently degrading, injurious or fatal. The villagers had been performing them for centuries before the Foundation arrived. Site-93 is built *inside* the village, deliberately autonomous, and **caps total knowledge of the anomaly at two hundred living people.** The institution has engineered its own permanent ignorance as a containment asset.

**SCP-3893 — I'm Going to Have a Motherfucking Meltdown** (CadaverCommander) · Euclid. Thirty metres under Uluru, wired into a pressurised-water reactor in the position of the fuel, is a desiccated human corpse generating over 500 MW without any biological process. A CRT beside it still shows his combat log — he is still fighting something called GRAYWING and intends to burn through ten thousand miles of stone to reach it. The facility on top is **licensed under Australian nuclear regulations and sells the output to the regional grid.** 186 is a wound the Foundation guards; 3893 is a wound it meters and bills. (The profane title is the corpse's voice; the document is straight.)

**SCP-6830 — Akhenaten** (AriadnesThread & Lt Flops) · Cernunnos. In one refraction of spacetime Akhenaten's rite succeeded and he ascended; he now periodically attempts to breach into baseline, and **accurate historical knowledge of him empowers the incursions while confusion weakens them.** So Operation Amun Seba funds bad history: fringe publications, deliberately wrong Egyptian imagery in apps, memetic noise, and the *Ancient Aliens* television series. The inscriptions have stopped. **The containment product is ignorance itself, purchased at retail.**

**SCP-3685 — The Fractured Pile** (Monkatraz) · Euclid. A Nazi *Uranprojekt* reactor that melted down in 1945 and entangled its own interior spacetime. Primary containment is a German device called the decompression pin, and the file states plainly that it is unknown where it is, how it operates, or whether it needs maintaining. Secondary containment cannot be examined without risking breach. Catastrophic breach is likely within a few years of the pin's failure — a failure with no estimable date. **Custodian of a machine it cannot locate inside a building it cannot enter.** *(Low-rated page — exact fit, modest article.)*

**SCP-4183 — Automatic Containment Procedures** (Tanhony) · Safe. After the world ends, three drones continue containing a vase that kills anyone who sees it. One takes critical damage; repairs require the approval of the only authorised signatory, who is at his desk with a pistol and does not respond. **The blocker is not grief, it is an unbypassable authorisation field.** The ones without selves keep the containment; the one with a self is the failure point. *(Caveat: the closing register is tender rather than cold, and the drones are given a small interior life — judge whether the warmth is residue or subject.)*

**SCP-2221 — A Friendly Agreement** (lukebn) · Keter. An anomalous EULA bundled into free software. Clause 189 redirects all of the user's prayer and worship to an entity called Amicus regardless of intent; Clause 191 grants the company subconscious control for up to 72 hours a week; **Clause 216 forces all disputes into arbitration before the Serene Tribunal.** It binds only people with legal capacity who genuinely consent — children and D-class are immune, which makes it untestable by the usual method. One infected population, ~2% of a country, prayed itself to death over two months. **Nothing was imposed. Everyone agreed.**

**SCP-3297 — Gramma Always Remembers.** (Uncle Nicolini) · Euclid. A 1.6-metre cicada-like entity that presents, infohazardously, as somebody's grandmother. On contact it writes false memories of itself — baking, toys, religious holidays — and eats the real ones to make room. Neuroimaging shows the signature of happiness during the write. A D-class finished with damage indistinguishable from advanced Alzheimer's, having lost his own name. Told he won't be coming back, the entity is sad: *"He might forget about little old me, but gramma always remembers."* **The victim is measurably happier at every step, and what is stolen is the only faculty that could notice.**

**SCP-5240 — Vergussenheit, the City of the Forgotten** (cleveremptY & Arbelict, RU branch) · Thaumiel. A German-speaking city-state outside all historical record whose entire economy is the manufacture of anomalous amnestics. Its citizens live under permanent low-level airborne memory suppression — no personal histories, no holidays, no past tense — enforced by a secret police that investigates "recollection poisoning." The Foundation trades food and technical goods for the compounds and for atmospheric dispersal on request. Its liaison works out that the city was built to erase something enormous, and is reassigned. *"As per the O5 Council's decree, the Ethics Committee is not permitted access to this dossier."* **The industrial policy end of SCP-3000's trade.**

**SCP-7526 — A Place With Doctors** (IAmTheOoga) · Euclid. A Hungarian alpine settlement sealed since 1681: 497 people and 39 clay-and-bone-ash humanoids who manufacture, from dismembered human bodies, a substance that cures disease, closes wounds, neutralises toxins and extends life. It works. New humanoids are made from young unmarried men who volunteer — and **the discarded original body is the raw material for the next batch of medicine.** From 2002 the Foundation begins supplying them with textbooks on neurology and genetics at their request. **Not containing it. Equipping it.**

**SCP-4042 — Somebody to Love** (GentleGifts) · Keter. A contagious thaumaturgical condition: a carrier who has been lonely for three to nine months subconsciously manifests a complete human being — indistinguishable, with a full false past and no awareness of what they are. Anyone who forms an emotional bond with one becomes a carrier, so it propagates along the exact channel of affection. Containment abandoned; population estimated in the hundreds of millions, called a low estimate. A researcher made his colleague; testing demanifested her; the article closes with him transferring to work alongside a new arrival. **A person whose entire preference structure was authored by the person she loves, with no way to find out.**

**SCP-9277 — Foundation Elder Care** (Cloverbutnot) · Euclid. Esther Gallagher, 74, whose health, personality, memories and ontological status conform to whatever the people around her agree is true. The Foundation takes her out of a care home and runs testing while its staff discuss her the way staff discuss a difficult elderly subject — and she duly develops advanced Alzheimer's. The fix is not to stop believing things about her; it is to **restrict who is allowed to**, with amnestics for temporary staff and active policing of negative discussion. Her selfhood is now an access-control problem. *(Medium-high: the researcher's turn flirts with a compensating virtue — read the remedy as the horror and it holds.)*

**SCP-9668 — Standard Reference Human** (syuzhet) · Esoteric, Foundation-made. The Department of Standards and Normalcy manufactures baseline humans to calibrate anomaly-detection equipment, produced from fluids extracted from *giant spermatozoic divinities* and gestated in isolettes. They come certified: characteristics within defined ranges, no soul. In the field they present as inattentive, impulsive, persistently sad, and ordinary people respond with escalating hostility and eventually destroy them. **Reproduction as a procurement function with a spec sheet; the paperwork move — renaming a person as reference material — is what makes the gestation line legible as logistics.**

**SCP-2510 — Got A Secret, Can You Keep It?** (Cerastes) · Safe. A 17-year-old is found dead in a school bathroom roughly five months after she died. Every resident of the town is unable to acknowledge her body or discuss her death, and the effect propagates along social bonds rather than any conventional vector. Working through a newcomer not yet absorbed into the social graph, the Foundation reconstructs what almost certainly happened, and what the town did about it, which is that it **collectively agreed not to know.** The Foundation then amnesticises the newcomer and the girl's family and files it as self-containing. **The only supernatural act in the article is the Foundation completing the town's work.**

**SCP-4402 — The Permanent Shopping List** (BaranXLR) · Safe. A mother's shopping list for her son — two dozen eggs, seven redacted items, *"P.S. Take the chicken out of the freezer."* Once recorded in any medium, that medium becomes permanently indestructible and uneditable, and anyone who reads the full text can never forget it — immune to amnestics, persisting in cerebral tissue after death and decay. Containment is therefore dosing every guard with **anterograde amnestics every 24 hours, indefinitely,** so they can never form the memory. One instance reached a site email server: 167 casualties. **A permanent standing dose, costing a rota of people the ability to form new memories forever, in exchange for containing a note about a chicken.**

**SCP-3908 — (Craig "Skip" Wattson)** (JanitorCakeworth) · Euclid. Any spoken or written information about him is automatically censored into self-reference, so every attempt to describe him resolves to `[SCP-3908 is SCP-3908]`. Three interviews inch toward the fact that he is a person with a life. The article's last item is a **proposed file revision** — a suggestion that the document be rewritten to treat him as a man rather than an object. **The reformer's win is a filing request.** 835 is redaction as architecture; 3908 is redaction as a closed loop with a memo attached.

**SCP-2256 — Very Tall Things** (qntm) · Euclid. Ocean-walking creatures over a kilometre tall, antimemetically camouflaged, now extinct. The containment procedures are therefore not about the animal — they are an **archival policy**: detailed data in hard copy only, broad descriptions permitted electronically, because the record is what is decaying. Over 60% of the documentation is already contaminated, and the ███ blocks are not censorship but rot. The species collapsed after the Foundation began observing it. **The archivist's version of SCP-3930's absence.**

**SCP-055** (qntm & CptBellman) · Keter. A self-classifying anti-meme. You can look at it, photograph it, take notes, and none of it survives contact with a mind. The article contains no description — it is assembled entirely from negation, because personnel *can* retain what it is **not**, so the file accumulates a growing list of exclusions and the object is deduced from the shape of the gap. **An entry that will keep growing forever without ever containing its subject.**

**SCP-5404 — [LEFT OUT TO ENSURE SAFE BEING]** (PeppersGhost) · Keter. Certain clusters of words detonate — literally — when used to speak or write about the phenomenon. The file is therefore written in a deliberately impoverished register with altered spelling. *"Please do not talk about SCP-5404."* Its test log tabulates which terms are "bad" and which are "good," and the log's own findings are inconsistent: the same word is safe in one context and lethal in another. **A bureaucracy that has had to give up half its dictionary and files anyway.** 2845 is the liturgical version of naming discipline; this is the industrial-safety version.

**SCP-3393 — For Your Eyes Only** (Captain Kirby) · Euclid. An antimemetic entity only four people can read the file about, on mnestics. The sections are **deliberately reordered** — Description first, then a discovery log, then a version of the file the entity wrote itself asking to be granted self-containing status. The Special Containment Procedures come dead last, because they are only useful if the entity reads them without knowing they are about it. **The trap is a document-ordering decision, made deliberately and minuted.** 2718 is a warning that arrives too late; 3393 is an ambush with a filing plan.

## TIER 3 — worth a look, with caveats

- **SCP-231 — Special Personnel Requirements** (DrClef) · Keter. Surfaced independently in two veins as the canonical text of the whole profile: Procedure 110-Montauk every 24 hours forever, never described, with only the **personnel spec** legible — Milgram score ≥72, unmarried, no offspring, concealing helmets, monthly rotation, transfer or termination for expressing sympathy. What you are actually reading is a recruitment standard. **Both sweeps flagged the same caveat: its absence from a 119-item register is more likely a deliberate decline than a gap.**
- **SCP-2000 — Deus Ex Machina** (HammerMaiden) · Thaumiel. 500,000 hominid replicators, 100,000 viable humans a day, memories installed via hallucinogenics and hypnotherapy, initiable by as few as one survivor — and the flat statement *"You can't bring them back,"* followed by 450–700-year-old remains with Dr. Clef's credentials beside them and the note *"How long have we been doing it?"* Same caveat as 231: famous enough that its absence may be a decision.
- **SCP-3600 — Documenting Doomsday** · Thaumiel. A Mesopotamian tablet where each character transfers full knowledge of a distinct K-class scenario, multiplicatively. Project Theia catalogues them anyway on a published ration: one character per subject per week, hard ceiling of twenty-five, *"until such time as the risk… can be mitigated."* **The sacrifice economy as a dosing schedule.** Byline not recoverable; ending unverified. Confidence: medium.
- **SCP-4219 — LUDROA** (Stark Moops) · Thaumiel. Engineered memetic agents as sapience detectors: present an elementary philosophical concept, and anything that *comprehends* it trips the alert; the memory decays in seconds so the subject never knows it was tested. 99.98% accurate. The implication is the payload — the Foundation needed a reliable way to tell, which means it was routinely getting it wrong, in both directions, on things it was already using. Confidence: medium.
- **SCP-4156 — Last Bastion** · Thaumiel. After civilisation collapses, a director rebuilds one Ohio town under an artificial sky, populated by 6,000+ manufactured humans with implanted memories, maintained by a system that edits their perception of the dead world above. He built them out of people he had known, including his wife and daughter, then killed himself in front of the manufactured daughter, **who could not perceive his death because the system edited it out.** Author unconfirmed; confidence medium.
- **SCP-8174 — The Ark** (Nils Severin) · Thaumiel. Thirteen annual maintenance logs, 1997–2010, for a perpetual-motion engine meant to carry humanity through total submersion. Output 98% → 20% while the wording stays flat. Recreational facilities removed, quarters reduced, crew reported missing from 2008. In 2010 one leaves a marking: *"HOW MANY YEARS HAVE I BEEN HERE? SHIP IS STOPPING."* The final recommendation is to apply automated amnestisation **before** rotation rather than after. **Recommend as a structural specimen next to 9377, not as a standalone** — slight page, force is formal rather than literary.
- **SCP-3702 — I Walk Through The Desert…** (DrBleep) · Euclid. A tongueless, burned humanoid walks toward water for fifteen hours a day and demanifests before arriving; runes read *"This is the price of my careless greed."* Offered food becomes scorpions, offered water becomes screw-worm flies — mercy is not futile, it is **converted into further punishment.** The Foundation is barred from intervening by treaty. Confidence medium-high; modest rating, premise may outrun the prose.
- **SCP-4031 — The Amnesiac Redemption** (Lt Flops) · Euclid. A powerful amnestic of unknown makeup, quantity and origin that produces, on activation, a male vocalisation subjects cannot afterwards confirm hearing. A team on mnestics returns to the chamber and finds an unaccounted-for male body: emaciated, lacerated, extensive head trauma. **The identity is never stated.** Confidence medium — "the amnestic is the man" is inference the text invites rather than states.
- **SCP-2998** (Eskobar) · Safe. A file that opens by announcing it contains an infohazard forcing the reader through a series of untrue iterations, each a separate page, before reaching the accurate one — with no way to certify which stop you are on. **A reading protocol imposed by the hazard and documented as containment.** Confidence medium: structure verified, landing not.
- **SCP-1893 — The Narrator** (Eskobar) · Euclid. An entity that converts any electronic message referring to it into prose, so the Foundation cannot store a file for it — only fiction about it. The article is two men at a wrecked Site-19, one dying, reciting the file in call-and-response while a horned figure approaches; the dialogue is the only element preserved unchanged, and the page is labelled Iteration C. **Flagged honestly as the closest on the list to the testimony-wrapper failure mode** — it survives only because the prose is a containment artefact rather than a delivery vehicle for pathos. If the scene feels like it is doing emotional work the file isn't, drop it.
- **SCP-3201 — well, it was low-entropy while it lasted.** · Thaumiel. Hyperdimensional entities collecting low-entropy objects across universes against the heat death, negotiating with the Foundation in a constructed mathematical language. The article's last section is an **exhibit catalogue entry for a dead universe**, authored by archivists identified only by number — a civilisation that built Beacons to call them and died first, 6.9 × 10²⁷ artifacts recovered. Final line: *"It was mostly nothing. But there were some interesting parts."* The format break is confined to the last section; bought for the landing.
- **SCP-6869 — SCP-####** (ratking666) · Pending. Item number and object class are **unfilled placeholders.** A two-dimensional weeping figure whose effect is emotional flattening: observers stop being able to care. The document catches it — procedures trail off into *"That's all I think?"*, the description shrugs, the interview log gives up. The file was never finished because nobody could sustain enough interest, and was published in that state. 2718's field break is a crash; this one is an unclosed ticket.

## CHECKED AND REJECTED — so the ground isn't re-walked

**Famous formal articles that fail the djkaktus question:**

- **SCP-3999** — the most format-broken article on the wiki and a textbook testimony wrapper. The mutating classes, screenplay inserts and collapse into authorial notes all exist to narrate one researcher's suffering and then the author's own creative process. Strip the format chaos and you lose the experience, not the mechanism. **Do not register.**
- **SCP-2557** — the Foundation's file bought by a holding company and reformatted as an advertisement. Good idea, comedic execution.
- **SCP-048**, **SCP-6254**, **SCP-3183** — format as commentary on the SCP form itself rather than format as containment. Metafiction, not horror. SCP-2740 already owns this ground better.

**Rejected on the vein tests:** SCP-6998 (removing the awful thing simply makes the world better — fails setting-c), SCP-9590 (personal devastation as subject rather than residue), SCP-7006 and SCP-7399 (comic register), SCP-4238 and SCP-8726 (compensating virtue), SCP-288 (possession, not preference-editing, and no institution), SCP-1123 (beautiful idea, Foundation only stores it), SCP-4145 (villain safely external), SCP-2317 — *see the disagreement noted above*, SCP-3448 — *see below*, SCP-4322 — *see below*, SCP-2003 (the fitting version was rewritten out of existence), SCP-3703 (Foundation only inherited it, so the paperwork clause fails — worth a look anyway).

**Two live disagreements between sweeps, left unresolved on purpose:**

- **SCP-3448 — Halfterlife** (Captain Kirby) · Thaumiel. A volunteer is put into half-death to contain death itself, reports back from a garden landscape for ten days, is posted a lighter and a handgun, finds the girl who is death, and shoots her. They did not contain death; they neutralised it, and the ΩK End of Death follows. One sweep called it the cleanest setting-c case in its vein — the reformers are right and the world stops anyway, and it could not have been a machine. The other rejected it for **spending a second impossibility to escape the corner: death turns out to be a thing you can shoot.** Both readings are defensible; it is a Chiang-question call.
- **SCP-4322 — Sleepless Dreams**, listed in Tier 2 above, was rejected by a different sweep as "the Ethics Committee overrides an O5 to be *kind*." The counter-argument is that the kindness is the horror — twelve million disembodied brains is a number nobody justifies, only services.

**Not an SCP but flagged anyway:** *"A Plague of Philosophical Zombies"* (Yhoundesh) — a tale, so it breaks the register's format, but the purest Watts item found in the sweep. An anomaly transmitted via appendicitis produces consciousness-death: survivors lose interiority and remain fully functional. The prior researchers had already become p-zombies and were suppressing the finding to protect themselves; the O5 response is to restrict the knowledge, **staff the containment effort with p-zombie researchers**, amnesticise the discoverer and reassign her as a lifer. The anomaly turns out to be endogenous to human consciousness and to emerge spontaneously in about 7% of the population.

## GAPS IN THE SWEEP

- The `ethics-committee` tag listing truncated at SCP-4342, so the 5000–9999 range of that tag is unexplored. That is the highest-yield place for a second pass.
- **SCP-3125** (qntm, Antimemetics Division) would not render through any fetch route. Almost certainly a strong fit for the form vein; unverified, so unrecommended. Worth opening directly.
- **SCP-1159** consists entirely of embedded audio — *"All information in this file must be kept in auditory form"* — which could not be extracted to verify. Exactly the vein; low confidence.
- **SCP-3939** — object class reads `[NUMBER RESERVED; AWAITING RESEARCHER]`, a gramophone left in a holding cell for thirteen years because nobody was ever assigned to classify it. A perfect 2718 neighbour on the field break, delivered as a 67-page branching choose-your-own-path. Flagged rather than recommended.
- Chinese, Korean and other branch translations were only lightly sampled.
