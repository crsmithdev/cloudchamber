### 1\. Project PARAGON — the "Demon" cycle (antediluvian canon)

**What it is.** PARAGON is presented as an internal Foundation project dossier, framed by a memorandum from **Dr. Shannon Lancaster, Project Director**. Its remit: "there are powerful forces from the ancient world moving in ours… We have a duty — not only to protect our future — but to preserve our past." The hub is a heavily-styled document with collapsible ACCESS DOCUMENT panels for Timeline, Antediluvian Peoples, Locations, Entities of Primary Concern, and Events. Rating \+409.

**Deep chronology (as the hub gives it).** Roughly 400,000–600,000 years ago, before the Great Flood, three humanoid races share the Earth:

- ***Homo aeturnus***, "Children of the Sun," inherently thaumaturgic, descended from **Adam El Asem**, the First Man. They built **Audapaupadopolis** ("City of the First Father").  
- ***Trans stellaris***, the Faeries, older than human life, tied to nature-thaumatology through **Gaia** and **Titania**, "a goddess of stars and the granting of wishes." Fae individual names are permanently, anomalously lost ("nomenclatively compromised").  
- ***Homo nocturnus***, the **Children of the Night** — SCP-1000, canonically Bigfoot — created by Titania as a limited imitation of early humans, and subjugated by the Fae for millennia.

The Fae, trying to kill Asem, compel the Children of the Night to cross the sea and annihilate him; Audapaupadopolis falls. **Cain (SCP-073)** and **Able (SCP-076)**, Asem's eldest sons, are expelled just before. The third son **Seth (SCP-4840-A)** survives, and later gives Asem's star-iron crown to a man named Harrian the Elder, naming him **Apollyon — "King Over The Darkness."** From that crown comes the dynasty of the **Sky Kings** of Apollyona, who rule "Olde Europ" for perhaps two hundred generations and war constantly with the **Daevites**.

**The inciting crime.** Sky King **Sarrus VIII von Apollyon** sails the largest army yet assembled to the Faerie continent under four legendary knights — **La Hire the Fierce, Lancelot the Conqueror, Hector the Stalwart, Ogier the Faithful**. He sacks the Fae holy city, kills their sovereign, and takes the last living Fae religious noble, a nameless princess (the **EROS entity**), as hostage. On the voyage home she curses the House of Apollyon; a storm burns the flagship and drags Sarrus VIII to his death. His son **Sarrus IX** buries her alive in an iron tomb in a pit.

**The Demons.** The curse works through the knights, transfiguring them one by one into "Primeval Demons." This is the spine of the series:

**The Great Profanities (SCP-4812, "Wrath").** Three more curse-born entities, each aimed at a wider target: **Vinuvinex / the Profane Restrictor** (4812-E, a metal heatsink humanoid that froze Sarrus IX's daughter), **Lamenellant / the Profane Adamant** (4812-K, a winged scorpion with a man's face — the only one still *uncontained*, apparently searching for EROS), and **YASH / the Profane Dark** (4812-S, "the Last Foe of Man," which swallowed the capital of High Apollyona whole and kills anyone who looks at it; likely an actual Fae god).

**Titania, three ways.** She is the load-bearing figure of the whole cycle and appears in three different states:

- As **deity** — Fae goddess of stars and wishes, designer of the Children of the Night.  
- As **SCP-2932, "Titania's Prison"** (+716, Thaumiel, in a Peruvian nature reserve): a vast organic domed penitentiary that Titania *built out of her own body*, powered by **SCP-2932-2, her still-beating heart**, at 8 bpm and failing. It is tended by **SCP-2932-A**, an ancient six-limbed insectoid Warden, the last of a skeleton crew, watching containment pods rupture one by one as the heart weakens. The Foundation is reduced to rotating doctors on six-hour shifts to keep a god's heart going.  
- As **SCP-6666, "The Dread Titania"** (+1046, Esoteric/Thaumiel, SCP-6000 contest runner-up): her **corpse** — a 9.2 km inverted tree suspended by its roots from the ceiling of a 52 km lithospheric void beneath the Amazon, biologically dead at the cellular level, with toxic fog pouring from the wound Hector opened. On the cavern floor: the ruined city of the Children of the Night, and *thousands* of dormant SCP-1000 instances paralyzed by that fog. Hector, sent by Sarrus IX to destroy the heart of the curse, found the dead goddess, **demanded a wish**, and the wish was the pollen that keeps her children asleep.

**Malidraug and SCP-6765.** Malidraug is a **Daevite** house/city-state, unusually well-documented compared to other Daevite records. Its Sorcerer King **Relivine** (born Relaea; Malidraui’s lords ruled as men regardless of birth sex) tutored **SCP-343 — "God"** — who served as his Grand Vizier before the Children of the Night destroyed the kingdom.
