# Theme grain — form reference

*Twenty-four lines lifted from the playbook's theme bank on 2026-09-03, when
that bank was retired in favour of `extracted/themes.jsonl`. They are kept for
their **shape** and nothing else: one sentence, a mechanism and a turn, no
proper nouns, self-contained. `pipeline themes --brief` samples them as
few-shot so a drafting session has the grain in front of it.*

*This is not a bank. Nothing draws from it, nothing is generated from these,
and they are not themes the slate is meant to use — the content of the old
bank was retired deliberately. If a line here reads as a good idea, that is
incidental.*

- A god manufactured by belief rather than described by it: worship pours a shape and something unrelated fills it, then grows into the shape.
- A lesion induced deliberately and reversibly, by request, and argued for as a matter of fairness.
- A world ended by signature rather than by anyone present: authorised, countersigned, filed under a precedent, and nobody in the chain ever sees a body.
- Amnestics that fail in the worst direction — the terror stays, the cause is deleted.
- An antagonist whose competence at helping you is visibly the competence it acquired doing the opposite, and which will say where it learned it, unprompted, afterward.
- An intake that sorts arrivals into three careers and one consumption, by graders not told which is which.
- An obligation installed rather than signed — put inside the body, in the bone or the head, where it cannot be repudiated, resigned from or served out.
- Bodies returned in better condition than they died in and degrading fast, and the distress that causes belongs to the occupant rather than to the family.
- Faith arriving as a memetic hazard, so that belief and infection become clinically indistinguishable — and the file refuses to say which one the believer has.
- Harm done to a professional standard — the practitioner has preferences, refusals and off-nights, a word for a good result, and an audience to say it to.
- Malice as a by-product of ordinary pleasure accumulating past a threshold — nobody built it, everyone fed it, and it takes the feeders first.
- Malice conducted entirely in correspondence — warm, correct, complimentary, correctly addressed — so there is no register available in which to object to a letter.
- Not a lost faculty but an installed wall, and the absence is a fitting with a part number.
- One surviving chronology, compiled by a party to the events, and everything downstream cites it.
- Salvation bought by damning one volunteer permanently, authorised by committee, with the price acknowledged in the authorising document: *it isn't a fair price, but nothing is.*
- Staff who cannot describe the floor above them, and are not being kept from it — there is nobody to ask.
- The amnestic administered to the victim not as mercy but as maintenance: she is returned, on a weekly schedule, to the state in which the procedure still works.
- The body priced by yield rather than by person: so many usable units per donor, and a rate card.
- The clinician at the bedside is recovering an asset, and the dying man knows it and agrees.
- The discarded original body is the raw material for the next one, and this is stated in the manual.
- The inner voice as a receiver rather than a source, with a transmission schedule someone maintains.
- The register will certify any manner of death put to it, and the coroner asks it once per body.
- The strongest of the devout survive the compounding blessings and are promoted out of the species — the intended outcome, and it removes them from every protection the species had.
- Throughput exceeded years ago, deliveries unchanged, and no post from which both facts are visible.
