# Evaluation run — aiming the prompts at presence and people, and losing

Run 2026-09-23. The judges complain most about presence and people, and the
last change moved neither. This run adds clauses to the scene register and the
schedule's arrival requirement to aim at both, and judges three drafts a side
against the landed code. **The change is rejected.** It does not improve either
axis it targets, and it costs half as much again to draft.

## What changed

`c2027ad`, against `1583462`, in `app/pipeline/prompts.ts` only:

- **`sceneSignal`**: the withheld thing gets one act at a time in what a person
  senses; nothing names or explains it while it is in the room; no line exists
  to tell the listener what two speakers already know.
- **`scheduleListen`**: the arrival beat does three separate things a person
  present could see, and nobody there names what it is.

The clauses were derived from the judges' own notes on the three `fix` drafts,
not guessed at. On presence the notes reward a thing that is lingered over
("takes more time to detail") and punish one that is hurried ("rushes slightly
through", "briskly and analytically framed"). On people they punish "functional
exposition" and "explanatory speech".

The reasoning was that the `presence-in-room` screen asks *present or absent*,
so a one-sentence manifestation already passes it; the gap is duration, which no
layer stated. The reasoning was sound. The fix was not.

## The protocol

Brief `20260922134631-ad7c`, unrestricted, profile `listen`, three drafts a
side. The baseline arm is the existing `fix1-3`: the two commits between
`4c7e4b1` and `1583462` touch the drafting path but are all inert for this brief
(the claims screen needs a setting authority, `repeated_speech` is a rename in a
report, the ledger is pinned at chain root `0382`, the rest are refactors).
Matched pairs, four passes each, half in each reading order, Gemini 3.1 Pro,
GLM-4.7 and GPT-5.1, plus a `pres1`–`pres2` within-arm floor.

Two confounds were checked before judging and both are clear: the last beat
holds at 1.00 to 1.19 of the median (the mechanism that sank the caps change),
and the arms' mean lengths match at 10,222 and 10,237 words.

## The result

Weighted share to the new arm **0.39** over 36 passes. The floor run returned
0.62, but on 8 passes with judge weights of 0.00 and 0.25 it is too thin to size
the gap — the absolute scores carry this result, not the share.

| axis | pres | fix | mean diff |
|---|---|---|---|
| hook | 3.97 | 4.11 | −0.14 |
| **presence** | **4.34** | **4.43** | **−0.09** |
| **people** | **4.00** | **4.09** | **−0.09** |
| feeling | 4.34 | 4.43 | −0.09 |
| cost | 4.54 | 4.83 | −0.29 |
| ending | 4.11 | 4.60 | −0.48 |
| clarity | 4.31 | 4.26 | +0.05 |
| momentum | 4.00 | 4.23 | −0.23 |

Per matched pair, over all axes: −0.28, +0.18, −0.41.

**The two axes the change was written for both went down.** That is the finding.
The drops on ending, cost and momentum are larger, but only two of three pairs
carry them, so treat the magnitude as unsettled; presence and people need no
such hedge, because a change that targets an axis and does not raise it has
failed on its own terms.

## The cost

| arm | draft cost | drafting calls | scene calls | register rewrites |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| fix | $7.73, $6.89, $8.38 | 86, 82, 103 | 30, 30, 35 | 3, 2, 5 |
| pres | $10.28, $10.78, $13.44 | 118, 118, 157 | 36, 37, 49 | 7, 7, 11 |

Mean $7.67 → **$11.50, half as much again**. The mechanism is visible: 10
register rewrites across the old arm became 25 across the new one. More clauses
mean more screens fail, and every failure buys another scene call.

## What this says about adding clauses

This is the clearest evidence yet for the accretion problem noted in
`CLAUDE.md`. The clause was derived from real judge notes, aimed at a real gap
no layer covered, and checked against the two known confounds. It still lost,
and it made drafting 50% more expensive on the way.

Two lessons worth keeping:

- **A clause that reads well is not a clause that scores.** The new arrival
  prose looks exactly like what the notes asked for — the stacks breathe, the
  thing unfolds, the smell arrives, a face goes on, and nothing names it. Read
  by hand it is plainly better. The panel disagreed. A qualitative read of one's
  own change is worth nothing.
- **A clause has a blast radius.** This one was written for two axes and the
  other six moved, five of them down. A prompt is not a list of independent
  requirements; every clause competes with the rest for the model's attention
  and for the beat's words.

The next move is not another clause. It is to state each constraint once, at the
layer that enforces it, and to use this same protocol to find which of the
existing clauses are load-bearing.

## Not landed

`c2027ad` stays unmerged and the branch is removed. `main` keeps the prompts as
they were at `1583462`.
