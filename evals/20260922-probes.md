# Probes — the five cost and quality ideas that needed measuring first

Run 2026-09-22. Each idea from the recommendations was measured before it was
built. Two were built (they needed no probe), one was rejected on its
measurement, two on the record already in the store, and one holds but is not
adopted.

## Carrying the scenes as turns of one session — holds, not adopted

Today every scene resends the whole story so far in its user prompt, which is
written to the cache and never read. A probe of the same two calls as a
session instead:

| call | read from cache | written | cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| beat 1, a 22.5k-token prompt | 0 | 22,545 | $0.231 |
| beat 2, `--resume` of that session | **22,545** | 306 | $0.252 |

The second call paid $0.011 for what the first paid $0.225 to write. On a
twelve-beat draft that is about $1.30.

Not adopted here. It needs session persistence, which ADR-0001 rules out, and
it changes what the model sees: its earlier scenes become its own turns rather
than a block of text. That is a quality change, so it needs a judged run of
its own.

## One structure screen over the whole draft — rejected

One call over the twelve scenes of `ad7c`, against the twelve per-scene calls
already stored for the same draft:

| | per scene | one call |
|---|---|---|
| cost | about $0.76 | $0.33 |
| answers | 96 | 84 given, **16 of 100 missing** |
| agreement with the per-scene answers | — | **53%** |

Half the saving, and the answers are not the same answers. A screen that
disagrees with itself half the time cannot drive a rewrite.

## Tuning when auto repair stops — rejected

Over 45 auto runs in the store, by round:

| round | runs | total score fell | mean drop | mean calls |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | 44 | 35 | 12.0 | 32 |
| 3 | 38 | 23 | 6.4 | 39 |
| 4 | 23 | 13 | 4.0 | 45 |
| 5 | 11 | 5 | 2.6 | 52 |

Later rounds still lower the open score, and 35 of 45 runs already stop on the
floor rather than the round cap. There is nothing here to cut.

## Five-minute cache writes — not possible from the CLI

The CLI has no cache-TTL flag. Its one cache flag,
`--exclude-dynamic-system-prompt-sections`, is ignored with `--system-prompt`,
which every stage passes. Writes stay at the one-hour rate.

## Three schedules, keeping the first that passes the rules — not supported

Of 24 schedule steps in the store, 4 failed shape and were retried, and 11 of
the last 12 schedules named a paying beat. The failure this was meant to catch,
a cold open where a linear telling was wanted, is now a fixed axis that fails
shape and retries (`191a9bb`). The evidence does not support paying $0.52 a
draft for two more schedules.
