## Deviations from the protocol

Two. Both change what the numbers mean, so they come first.

1. **The seed is drawn, not typed.** Step 1 asks for a typed seed. This run takes
   one from the theme bank (`0207edafcb38`). No taste of the operator enters the
   run except the premise choice at the gate.
2. **The drafted round is the last, not the best.** Step 5 asks for the round the
   loop names as lowest. That round is `20260915210119-ccae` and it cannot be
   drafted. `Drafting.draft` (`app/pipeline/drafting.ts:139`) accepts only status
   `done` or `awaiting_check_gate`, and round 3 is `repaired`; line 147 then
   refuses a draw that holds accepted findings, and round 3 holds two. Those two
   accepted findings are part of why round 3 scores lowest: its fixes were applied
   to make round 4, never to round 3's own text. The run therefore drafts the chain
   tip `20260915211209-c264`, whose repairs are all applied and whose two remaining
   findings are open, not accepted.

Nothing else is hand-steered. No finding is accepted or dismissed by hand.

## Diagnosis of the series

110 → 101 → 40 → 45 → 56. The series falls, and it falls hard at round 3: findings
drop 19 → 6 and the total drops 101 → 40 in one round. Rounds 4 and 5 rise again,
which is churn, and patience stops the loop correctly after two rounds that do not
improve on round 3.

The comparison run is the nineveh chain `20260915012209-d52c` → `20260915165134-c3ae`,
before this afternoon's changes: 107, 116, 119, 99, 93, 97, 94, 111 over eight rounds,
flat, still open at round 11. That chain re-argued 18 of 62 findings (29%). This one
re-argues 4 of 60 (6.7%). The pinned ledger and the carried-forward fixes hold.

Cost: 12 model calls a round, against about 25 a round on the nineveh chain. Claims
are off here, because the run is unrestricted, so the saving comes from the lower
sampling, the in-place patching and the context gating.

## The three sentences

**Well.** The loop converged, the score fell by 64% in three rounds, re-argument fell
from 29% to 6.7%, and the round-1 outline found the chosen vignette's central cheat and
repaired it for free — a check carries quantity and never content, so the artefact
becomes a dictionary, the one book whose contents follow from its coordinates.

**Badly.** The `arithmetic` re-weight from 3 to 1 over-corrected, so the loop dismissed
real load-bearing arithmetic below the floor and the errors reached the finished story:
the outlay-against-income contradiction (`f-5c94653b`, score 6) stands in beat 4 as
*"something under one and three-quarter million"* against *"a little over four million a
year"*, and the first purchase is priced at nine thousand dollars where 4.1× annual
payment gives fifty-six thousand.

**The one change.** Put `arithmetic` back to weight 2, or weight it by whether the span
sits inside the outline's arithmetic section, because on a brief where the money is the
plot a demoted arithmetic finding is a plot hole with a rubric exemption.

## Also worth fixing

- **The protocol cannot draft its own best round.** See Deviations. Either let `draft`
  accept a superseded draw whose accepted findings were repaired downstream, or change
  step 5 to name the last round whose accepted findings are applied. The second is
  cheaper and truer: a round scores low partly because its accepted fixes are still
  pending.
- **A score-7 finding can never be accepted.** `f-8c98da8d` scores 7, at the floor, and
  names the vignette line *"The words arrive afterward, free, because nothing was ever
  altered."* It recurs in 1 of 2 samples, and `checks.keep_if = 2` holds it below the
  reporting bar, so `auto` never sees it and never accepts it. The defect goes into beat 1
  of the story unaltered. With `samples = 2`, recurrence is a coin flip that can veto the
  score outright.
- **A correct screen flag changes nothing.** The beat-4 ledger screen catches the first
  purchase price and supplies the right figure — *"nine thousand dollars"* → *"fifty-six
  thousand dollars, four point one times the annual payment"* — and the wrong figure
  still stands in the story. Screens report; nothing applies them. Gate 2 offers only
  `keep`, `rewrite <k>` and `pass`, so a one-number fix costs a whole scene.
