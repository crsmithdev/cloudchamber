## Shape

The channel cold-opens on its climax (the message that describes the room,
"it knows you can see it") inside the first 3 %, then backs up and narrates
one night linearly to that point at 78 %, then gives an aftermath and a home
coda: the dog, the reflection, the motion light. First person, past tense,
addressed to the listener, feelings named ("I felt something drop through
the floor of my stomach"), a closing moral stated in words. Its specificity
is domestic and bodily; its hardware is generic (a relay satellite, "a
frequency band it wasn't built for").

Our draft is five nights in strict order, third person present, close on
one specialist. No cold open, no presence, no body, no threat to her. The
horror is an inference she labels INFERENCE on her own legal pad: the
phantom is not an object but a practice, and the count of them is the count
of us. It ends before any confirmation, on an email to JAXA. Its specificity
is instrumental (SPICE kernels, J2000, Delta Cassiopeiae, Sagamihara) and
every number in it is the ledger's.

Neither is the better shape; they are different bets on the same title. The
channel bets on presence and tells you what to feel; the draft bets on
arithmetic and withholds the reading until half way.

## What the pipeline did well and badly

- **The ledger bound.** 13 flags, all patched as written, none left for gate
  2. The two event-horizon drafts on the old code had 26 to 29 each with
  most waiting at the gate. One run; the direction is right.
- **Repetition across beats.** Beats 5 to 8 restate the same realisation in
  near-identical sentences ("It is not an object. It is a practice." / "There
  is a practice." / "Eleven is how many of us there are." twice). The slop
  trigrams say the same. A sequential draft reads the story so far and
  restates it; nothing screens for a beat that re-says the beat before.
- **Theme-stated in 7 of 8 beats.** The structure screen flags it and
  nothing acts on it. The channel states its theme once, at the end.
- **The check loop stalled on one finding.** Three rounds at total 7, 7, 10
  on one relitigating or sub-floor finding; patience stopped it. The rounds
  bought nothing after round 3.
- **Cost.** 81 calls on the chain including the failed verifies and restarts,
  about 26 on the draft.
