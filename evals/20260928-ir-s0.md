# S0: a beat's slice in the scene ask does not cut the ledger flags

28 September. Experiment S0 of `docs/specs/2026-09-28-story-ir.md` §13.2.
The claim under test: a short, beat-scoped list of facts in the scene ask
binds where the whole ledger in the system prompt does not.

**The idea fails the kill rule.** One draft had more ledger flags than both
baselines. The other had fewer, but the fall was 0.44 and 0.39 a beat, under
the 0.6 bar.

## What changed

Commit `6daa334` on branch `ir-s0`:

The system prompt (`sceneContext`) is unchanged. No check, gate or bind
changed. `bun test`: 339 pass, 0 fail.

Two deviations from §13.2:

- The schedule prompt did not contain the ledger before this change, so a
  `<uses>` block cannot quote ledger lines without it. The ledger goes in
  the schedule's user ask.
- `branch --at-beat 1` without an instruction copies the source's schedule,
  so it does not replan. With `--instruction` it replans under an operator
  instruction, which adds a second variable. The runs used
  `Drafting.sibling` (the path `lab best` uses) from a scratch script. It
  pins the brief and the ledger, writes a new schedule, and keeps the
  source's `listen` configuration. Both new draws have the same
  `draft_config` as `e33a`.

## Kill rule

Kill if, on both drafts, ledger flags a beat and open flags do not fall by
more than 0.6 a beat against both baselines. The noise of one reading is
0.62 a beat (`evals/20260926-reference-bind-noise.md`).

| draft | flags a beat: fall vs `e33a`, `08aa` | open a beat: fall vs `e33a`, `08aa` |
|---|---|---|
| `8afa` | −0.69, −0.74 (a rise) | 0.22, 0.09 |
| `e8a1` | 0.44, 0.39 | 0.52, 0.39 |

No fall exceeds 0.6, so the idea fails. The baselines had 0.54 and 0.67 open
flags a beat, so a fall of more than 0.6 in open flags was nearly impossible.
Only the flag count could decide the rule.

The two drafts of one arm differ by 1.13 flags a beat. That is more than the
gap between either draft and the baselines. Draft variance is larger than
the effect under test, as `evals/20260924-draft-variance-dominates.md` found.
`e8a1` had the fewest open flags of the four draws (2), and neither S0 draft
had a claims contradiction on the prose. With two drafts, this does not
separate from noise.
