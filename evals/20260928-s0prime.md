# S0′: what share of gate 1 a type checker would take

28 September. Step 9 of `docs/reviews/2026-09-26-the-plan-from-here.md`;
§12.6 of `docs/specs/2026-09-28-story-ir.md`. $0: a read of the store.

**35 of 40 kept findings (88%), or 25 of 30 distinct defects (83%), are
typed-deterministic or model-on-a-slice. The kill bar was under half, so
§12, gate 1 as type checking on the IR, stays a live design.**

## Method

The kept findings of the derivation and ledger checks (`source: "check"`,
latest verdict `keep`, gate or draw): 199 across 122 draws. Every fourth by
finding-artifact id, 40 in all. One reader classified each from its
statement, evidence and replacement:

- **typed**: two values for one count, name, number or place; a sum; a
  time or calendar step. A table of typed symbols finds it at $0.
- **slice**: a rule, a knowledge line or a custody line. A model call on the
  symbol and the line that breaks it finds it, as L4 does for the plan.
- **text**: a reading of prose that no symbol states.

## Limits

- One reader and no second rating. Typed against slice is the split a
  second reader would most likely move; the share over the bar is not
  close either way.
- The classes say what a checker could catch if the IR held the value. Gate
  1 reads the brief's prose (vignette, outline, ending), and the plan check
  reads the schedule. A type checker takes these findings only once the
  brief's facts are lowered to symbols, which L1 does for the ledger today
  and not for the vignette or the ending.
