# Judge prompt — informed (given §0)

Used to separate house convention from horror craft. Run alongside the naive
judge on the same pairs: constructs the naive judge misses and the informed
judge catches are conventions the playbook must enforce, because a reader will
not supply them.

Give the judge §0 of `playbook.md` and nothing else from the playbook.

Question: **which better answers to §0?** Answer `A`, `B` or `TIE`, name the §0
property that decided it, and give a reason of at most twelve words. Same
discipline as the naive prompt, plus: §0 says several of its properties should be
carried, not all — a premise missing one is not thereby the weaker.
