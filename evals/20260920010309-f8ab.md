# Evaluation run — the Ark, listen, shaped at the draw

Run 2026-09-20 (night), the first draft that carries all four delivery
fixes from `evals/20260919-second-family-judge.md`: the paying beat asked
`presence-in-room` and `cost-in-scene` (8105520), `structure.register` as
a setting with the `listen` profile fixed to `signal`, the listen screen's
long-sentence share as a rewrite trigger, and `--shape listen` at the draw
(f9daa3a), which asks the premises for a story told aloud.

Motive: the first `listen` draft (`b6bb`) scored 3 of 9 on three
independent labs. This is the same template with the register fixed, the
two delivery questions armed, the length trigger armed, and the shape
carried back into the premise stage.

## What the judges asked for

All three wrote the same three things in their `needs` field, unprompted:

1. **Fewer voices, sharper.** "Vasha, Teyve, Mirjam, Pell … less like a
   single mind talking to itself" (GPT-5.1); "its cast reduced to four
   recurring voices" (Grok).
2. **Less arithmetic.** "Tickets, curves, percentages" (GPT-5.1); "shorter
   paragraphs … one clear external action per section instead of internal
   summaries" (Grok). The numbers measure it: 21.1 numerals per 1,000
   words against the pool's 3.2, the widest gap of any figure in the
   listen report.
3. **The uncanny thing onstage more often.** "Bringing the doorway figure
   or the unphotographable children onstage a bit earlier and more often"
   (GPT-5.1); "collide with the sterilization decision in the same scene"
   (Grok). The arrival is one beat of twelve.

**Person does not predict parity.** Both first-person drafts are at or
below the middle (5/9 and 3/9) and the top two are third. Every channel in
the pool is told by an I, and copying that has not helped us once.

**Quoted speech does**, on four of the five: 92.8 → 9/9, 45.5 → 8/9,
21.1 → 5/9, 0.8 → 3/9. The exception is `70ac`, which reaches 5/9 with
almost no dialogue at all. Five drafts is not enough to call this a law,
but it is the only column that orders the table.

**The numeral load is what separates `f8ab` from the signal drafts.** At
20.6 per 1,000 words it carries four times any other draft and
six times the pool, and it is the draft the judges said they could not
track. That is a measured defect with an obvious fix.

## Verdict

The shaped draw plus the fixed register makes `listen` a viable template:
3/9 → 5/9, cost and presence recovered, the prose inside the pool's range.
It is not yet as good as the plain `signal` draft (9/9) at the same job.
The remaining gap is not delivery. It is **cast, numeral density and
quoted speech**.

The shaped draw also paid for itself on cost: the chain hit the floor
after one repair and 37 ideation calls, against four to five rounds
before. One data point.

## Follow-ups

1. **A numeral budget on the listen screen**, with a rewrite trigger like
   the length one. 21.1 per 1k against 3.2 is the clearest measured defect
   in the draft.
2. **Push the quote density.** The one column that orders the five drafts
   is quoted speech, and `f8ab` sits at 20.8 against the signal drafts'
   44.9 and 88.4. A floor on it under the `listen` profile is one setting.
3. **A cast ceiling.** Three labs asked for fewer named speakers.
4. **The arrival more than once.** The template asks for one arrival beat;
   two labs asked for the withheld thing to recur.
5. The four open ledger flags are one knot: Noor Sarraf's age (eleven),
   her birth year, and the claim she is the youngest on a roster that
   includes infants. A `rewrite` at gate 2 would settle it.
