## The thing has a body

All five premises the shaped ask returned carried a thing that can act:
a crew pressed into soil and still moving; a crew member who is always
one more than the manifest; a sleeper standing dry-eyed and reaching; a
bare handprint on the outside of the hull in vacuum; something eating
the dead in the recycling galleries. Run 7's ask returned a blight.

Beat 1 opens on the smell of turned earth where soil is prohibited, and
the runner "leaning down the aisle in the shape of a waiting boy" is in
its job line. Beat 9, the paying beat, has the thing put a second
structure down beside her hand and cast her while she kneels: *"the
medium pushed a fine white braid into the negative of it, filling the
dish, filling the four channels of her fingers, taking the wall of her
thumbprint ridge by ridge."* The screen answered `presence-in-room`
present on the first pass. `PRESENCE_LINE` did not need to fire.

## Verdict

Asking for the thing's body at the premise stage did what three rounds
of rewrite lines could not: presence went from the worst axis in the
programme to a clean sweep on seven families. The cast and the visible
set piece moved quoted speech and body words to their highest values.
What the source keeps is the one thing no question yet asks: **beats in
which only one person speaks.**
