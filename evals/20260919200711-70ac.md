## What the pipeline did

- **The told shape produced the story the channels have.** A cold open
  on the worst moment, told before it is explained; the flight and the
  landing; the depot, the basin, the folded bag; the arithmetic; the
  arrival four metres away, replayed with names; the morning after, and
  the plate in her pocket. Nobody leaves and it still ends.
- **The arrival section of the outline was used.** The depot's second
  pass took Okonjo at the galley threshold on night 6 and the cost was
  Lena's eleven seconds of listening; the schedule put it in beat 8 and
  the scene wrote it.
- **The register rewrites fired on five beats** and the structure screen
  then found a body named in every beat. Beat 8's rewrite answered a
  restated flag that was the replay of beat 1 by design; the screen now
  leaves a told draft's repeats of beat 1 alone.
- **The last-beat questions landed on the wrong beat.** The told shape
  pays in the beat before the last and the last is the aftermath, so
  `presence-arrives` on beat 9 was absent by construction; under a shaped
  template the beat before the last is now the one asked.
- **theme-stated rose to 5 of 9.** The told register invites the narrator
  to say what she makes of it now, and the screen counts each such line.
  The flags are read, not acted on; whether the told shape should exempt
  them is open.
- **Long sentences are three times the pool's share** and the mean is
  two words over. The prose is still written for the page; the listen
  screen shows where.
- **not-X-but-Y is at 11 per 10k**, above the pool and level with the
  channel; the told register's "not frozen, I was not frozen" cadence
  reads as the construction.
