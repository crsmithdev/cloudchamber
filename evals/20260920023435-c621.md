# Evaluation run — the edge of the solar system, listen, with a numeral budget

Run 2026-09-20 (night), the first draft on a new channel and the first
under `screens.listen.numerals_max` (cae168e). Everything else is the Ark
run's configuration: `--shape listen` at the draw, the `listen` template,
`structure.register = signal`, the paying beat asked all four delivery
questions, the long-sentence share a rewrite trigger.

Motive: every result so far came from two Void Signal videos. This is a
Galactic Horrors seed judged against the Galactic Horrors video its title
comes from, to see whether the template travels.

## Deviations

1. **Nothing kept.** The draft waits at gate 2 (`20260920023435-c621`).
   All six drafts now wait there.
2. **Gate 0 was posted by hand, under the auto rule.** The draw was
   started `mode: manual` by mistake, so it parked at gate 0 instead of
   choosing. `autoGate` (`app/pipeline/draw.ts`) picks from the
   lowest-probability candidates; the same candidate was chosen
   explicitly. The row therefore records `gate_method: manual` and the
   choice is the auto one, 0.44 of 5. About 25 minutes of wall clock were
   lost; no model call was wasted.
3. **The source may be the weak half.** Asked what the Galactic Horrors
   story needs, all three labs criticised it in the same terms without
   being asked to: "generic space-marine clichés" (Gemini), "repetitive
   descriptions of corridors, resin, and shrines" (GPT-5.1), "fewer named
   characters and technical terms so a listener can track who is
   speaking" (Grok). A 9/9 against a source three labs call cliché is a
   weaker test than 9/9 against the Dyson sphere video. **Read this run
   as evidence the template travels, not as evidence it improved.**
4. **Every figure here is recomputed from the final scenes, not read from
   the stored report.** `runScreens` only writes the `slop` and `listen`
   artifacts when it is given every beat (`write.ts:247,253`), and a
   rewrite re-screens two beats, so the stored report is always the
   pre-rewrite measurement and the gate-2 reader sees stale numbers. On
   this draft the stored report said 7.8 numerals per 1k; the shipped
   draft is 5.5. Both write-ups were corrected. The defect is open — see
   the architecture review, candidate 2.
5. **The two delivery questions still have not fired.** The paying beat
   (8) answered `presence-arrives`, `cost-paid`, `presence-in-room` and
   `cost-in-scene` all present on the first pass, as on the Ark run.
   `PRESENCE_LINE` and `COST_LINE` remain untested in a live run.

## Verdict

The template travels. A Galactic Horrors seed under the same settings
reaches 9/9 where the Void Signal seed reached 5/9, and the axis the fix
targeted moved on both. But Deviation 3 stands: this source is the
weakest of the three we have judged against, and one run on one channel
does not separate "the draft got better" from "the source was worse".

The honest summary of the night: **cost is solved** (0/9, 1/9 on the last
two runs, from 8/9), **clarity and momentum respond to the numeral
budget**, and **presence is now the open axis**.
