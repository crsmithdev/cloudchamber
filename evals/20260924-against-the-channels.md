## Trench Crusade against Vox Mortis

Three new unattended draws (`draw --setting trench-crusade --shape listen
--auto`, then `draft --auto`): `f20a`, `9c02`, `de03`. Judged against Vox
Mortis "Observer" (`N3-KWeuzKko`, 9,165 words), and one against "Heretic
Legionary" (`n6Oe2iRVRnA`, 9,476 words).

48 of 48 passes went to our draft, in both orders and from every judge. The
halves agree to within 0.35.

The judges say why. On the page Vox Mortis reads as "a lore document for a
tabletop game": no dialogue, no character who chooses, and the same
adjectives (blood, iron, hell) over and over. The auto-generated captions
also give its hero five names (Tamir, Sam, Tame, Hame, Damir), and one judge
marked that down, although it was told to ignore transcription errors.

**So this measures the text only.** Vox Mortis is an audio production; its
voice, music and pacing are what a listener hears and the panel does not. As
text, our drafts are far ahead. As a listen, this says nothing.

## What it says about the instrument

At four passes a judge, the halves of a pair against a source differ by up to
0.50. On the register cut's pairs they differed by about 0.15. `GAP_MARGIN`
(0.15) is too tight for a single pair at four passes. Read one pair with
eight passes a judge, or read the mean over several pairs.
