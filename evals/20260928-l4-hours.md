## The miss

On draw `20260929004112-7715` a bind patch moved beat 4's hour from 02:05
to 04:20. The patch was right. The ledger states the crawl's pace (from
00:55, a yard every nineteen minutes), and the plan's beat 4 reads
`<when>02:05, the eleventh yard outward</when>`: at that pace the eleventh
yard falls near 04:24. The conflict was in the plan from the start, not
made by the ticked plan finding; so re-checking a plan after a fix (step 7
as written) would not have found it.

L4 missed it because its prompt said "Do not report dates, day numbers,
hours or anything about the calendar: a separate check reads those". The
separate check (L3) compares dates and day numbers among the symbols. It
does not read a beat's hour against a stated rate.
