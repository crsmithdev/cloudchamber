The prose is the most clipped of any run and the most out of band on
not-X-but-Y. **No judge mentioned either in fifteen passes.** The measures
see a real difference from the pool; the listener the judges stand in for
did not care. A short-sentence floor on the listen screen was considered
and is not built: it would be a screen chasing a number, not a listener.

## What the source still wins

**People, 13 of 15**, and the same sentence for the third run running:
"Story One gives distinct voices to Ela, her mother, Commander Enkelu and
Aubry while Story Two keeps Nuri and Behnam in short, formal exchanges."
Cast breadth, not dialogue quality. The most consistent signal in the
programme.

**Hook, 13 of 15** — the source opens on a destroyed planet, this draft on
a count one body over. A taste split between two judges.

**Momentum, 13 of 15** — Grok twice: "lingers on Nuri alone with the
entity for years". The long middle.

## What this says to do next

1. **A one-speaker question, or a cast floor.** People has now lost on the
   same judge sentence in runs 8, 10 and 11. `one-voice` asks whether two
   speakers sound alike and cannot see a story with two speakers. This is
   the one change the evidence keeps pointing at.
2. **Do not build a short-sentence floor**, and do not act on the
   not-X-but-Y rate, until a judge complains about either.
