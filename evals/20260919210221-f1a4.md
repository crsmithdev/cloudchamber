## Shape

The channel: a solar physicist notices a dip at 4:14 a.m.; disbelief,
disclosure, a mission; the crew touches a segment; a pulse that seems
to answer; the hopeful reading breaks on a wound in the sun; the sphere
is containment; a split decision; a brace and a body buy a gap; a
briefing where an official asks for "permanently secure" and is told
"I can't confirm permanent"; a coda on indifference at scale. Third
person past, close on one scientist, four named crew with one habit
each, dialogue throughout, the feeling named as it is felt.

The steered draft: an unaffiliated amateur sorts forty-one anomalies by
epoch at three in the morning in Bergen; a referee who thinks he is
probably right rejects him in a parking lot and would write the same
letter tomorrow; eleven years of Sundays; the sun goes soft enough to
look at; a seat on the only ship; his daughter says go today, once; a
lattice that is grown, not built; a shelf the shape of two hands; nine
minutes in which the proxy speaks to each of them in their own dead;
a pilot who reads the burn window to the second and stays; four
sealed accounts that agree on every timestamp and disagree on the one
sentence; a notary who weeps and says it was a recording of a voice.

The plain draft: the elegy's four people compressed into one crew, a
boot on a film five hundred nanometres thick, an engineer who works a
seam through an eleven-minute shadow and understands there is nobody
across the table, a machine that learns the growth rule and joins the
growth, and an administrator with a forty-watt lamp who wants one clean
paragraph.

Same bets this time. All three stories have the noticing, the ensemble,
the contact, the split, the cost and the return. The channel's are
told at greater length and with more repetition per event; ours put
more events per minute and name the feeling less often.

## What the pipeline did

- **The genre line is the knob for the channel's shape.** The profile
  shapes the draft; the premise stage samples at the corpus
  distribution unless the ask says otherwise. Five mission-shaped
  premises from one sentence in the genre line.
- **The signal schedule parsed first time on both,** twelve beats each,
  and the scenes wrote within it; the shape's beats (noticing, go today,
  contact, split, cost, return) are all present in both drafts.
- **The plain brief fought the template.** Nine open ledger flags on
  `2f03`, all the brief's centuries against the schedule's one mission.
  A shaped template wants a brief drawn for it; the steered chain's
  brief had two open flags.
- **The restated screen is wrong for a twelve-beat story with motifs.**
  Twenty flags a draft, nearly all callbacks a listener is meant to hear
  again ("whether you intend to be persisted", "hands in to the wrist").
  It no longer triggers a rewrite (a0e81c6). Whether it should flag at
  all above some beat count is open.
- **not-X-but-Y at 17.7 per 10k on the steered draft**, seven times the
  channel. The construction carries some of the best lines ("It was not
  triumph. It was nausea."); the judge did not mind. Not chased.
- **theme-stated in 5 and 8 of 12 beats.** The signal register names the
  feeling as it is felt; the screen reads the naming as a theme. As
  with told, the flags are read, not acted on.
- **The listen screen matches the pool** on sentence length and body
  words for both drafts, and beat 1 renders at 175 wpm.
