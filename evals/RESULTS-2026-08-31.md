# Run 2 — the generation arm

*What the harness was built for: generate premises from playbook variants, judge them blind, and see which sections earn their place. Read `README.md` first for the protocol and its limits.*

## Design

Five arms, six premises each, generators blind to each other and forbidden from reading `stories/`:

| arm | playbook given |
| :-- | :-- |
| `FULL` | complete |
| `A1_no_veins` | §2.18–2.21 removed (malice, violence, substance, transformation) |
| `A2_no_setting-a` | §5 removed |
| `A3_no_step3` | §1's impossibility / engine / turn step removed |
| `A4_no_banks` | §0 and §1 only — no §2–§5 at all |

All 30 generated premises and all 24 slate synopses were then rewritten into one uniform register by an agent blind to their provenance — same sentence count, same length band, zero em dashes — so that a judge could not sort them by hand. Specificity and vagueness were held as content, not style.

## Result 1 — generated against slate: **the test is invalid**

Generated premises beat the slate synopses **82%** of the time (opus 0.90, sonnet 0.72). That is not credible: these are one-paragraph pitches against developed, endorsed work. It is the self-preference effect the literature predicts, and it is worse than a nuisance here — the bias tracks low perplexity, so it rewards the expected, which is the opposite of the register.

Inter-judge agreement fell to 68% (it was 81–100% on the degradation set) and the decoy tie rate halved, 44% → 21%. Every indicator says the judges were straining.

**The general lesson, and the reason the degradation test works and this one does not: within-item comparison is sound, across-item comparison is not.** A degraded variant is the same text with one property removed and everything else held; two unrelated premises differ in a hundred ways, and taste and provenance dominate.

## Result 2 — arms against each other: the corrective

Pairing each ablation's premises against `FULL`'s puts generated text on both sides, so self-preference applies equally and cancels. Both orders, one judge, decoys interleaved. Decoy tie rate 25%, order consistency 100% — the judge was behaving.

| ablation | FULL wins | loses | ties | FULL winrate [95% CI] |
| :-- | --: | --: | --: | :-- |
| `A1_no_veins` | 10 | 2 | 0 | **0.83** [0.62, 1.00] |
| `A2_no_setting-a` | 2 | 6 | 4 | 0.25 [0.00, 0.55] |
| `A3_no_step3` | 2 | 6 | 4 | 0.25 [0.00, 0.55] |
| `A4_no_banks` | 0 | 6 | 6 | 0.00 |
| **pooled** | **14** | **20** | **10** | **0.41** [0.25, 0.58] |

**The full playbook is not distinguishable from its own ablations.** The pooled interval spans 0.5.

**The one clean positive: the four vein families are load-bearing.** Remove §2.18–2.21 and the premises get worse, 0.83 with a CI clearing chance. That is the newest material in the playbook.

**§0 and §1 alone were not beaten by anything.** `A4_no_banks` lost nothing to `FULL` in twelve judgements — six losses, six ties. The spine carries the work; the banks are, on this evidence, unproven.

## Result 3 — diversity, which needs no judge

Mean pairwise vocabulary overlap between an arm's own six premises. Lower is more varied.

| | overlap |
| :-- | --: |
| slate (24 human-endorsed) | 0.022 |
| `FULL` | 0.032 |
| `A4_no_banks` | 0.039 |
| `A2_no_setting-a` | 0.046 |
| `A3_no_step3` | 0.048 |
| `A1_no_veins` | 0.050 |

**The full playbook produces the most varied batch of any generated arm** — a point in its favour that the judge did not see, and one that speaks to the only criterion the owner has ever named. The slate is more varied still, which is the gap left to close.

Against that: four of the five arms independently produced a mercury premise and three produced a premise about the transbay tube. `A4_no_banks`, which had to invent its own material, produced neither. Subject convergence is not captured by the word-level metric above and should be measured properly.

## What this does and does not license

n is 12 judgements per arm, one generation run, one judge. That is enough to show the harness runs end to end, enough to establish that the veins earn their place, and **not** enough to conclude the other banks do not. "Unproven" is the finding, not "useless."

What it is enough for is deciding what to run next: more premises per arm, at least two judges on the arm-vs-arm comparison, ablations at the family level rather than the section level, and a proper subject-collision metric rather than a lexical one.
