## The taste profile, consolidated (v5)

Everything learned across eight cycles and 26 picks.

**1. The scary thing is an idea.** Cold, conceptual, systems-scale. Not a creature, not a stalker, not a jump.

**2. The long unattended schedule.** The master engine, present in all 26: something has been running longer than anyone alive, maintained by people who don't know they're maintaining it, and it keeps perfect time.

**3. The horror is institutional, never domestic.** Every rejection whose center was one relationship or one person's reckoning. Every pick centers an apparatus. Personal devastation is fine — as the last frame of a systemic story.

**4. The reveal is arithmetic, not intention.** Nobody is evil. Several people are voting responsibly on the information in front of them.

**5. The format must be complicit.** Not merely "an artifact with a reason to exist as one voice" — the artifact has to be structurally implicated in the horror. This is what separated cycle 4's picks from its passes with no reference to subject at all.

**6. The Acausal principle.** Build on real, obscure, checkable theory — decision theory, neuroscience, biology, physics, law — played at full complexity and never simplified for the listener.

**7. The Chiang test.** Could this hold if nothing supernatural happened, with the premise alone producing the dread?

**8. The Watts test.** Does it threaten the assumption that having a self is necessary, central, or advantageous?

**9. The apostate doctrine — structural, not confessional.** Vindicate the metaphysics, never the authority. The reveal costs the institution more than the apostate. The realness arrives on an axis nobody argued about. Indifference is the sharpest rebuke. Grief, not vindication, for anyone.

**10. Body horror and violence are in** as of cycle 6. Gore is earned when it's the accurate description of something real. Calibration on where "disturbing" becomes "cheap" is still unmeasured — cycle 8 pushed hardest and hasn't been critiqued on that axis.

**11. Range is a channel strategy.** The covenant governs how stories are written, not what they can be about. Three of cycle 3's picks have no tech company, no queer lead, and no San Francisco.

**12. Warmth is an opening move, not a destination.** Heady is a feature. Endings are losses, preferably at population scale.

**Standing caution:** the document-as-monster device is at capacity. Three cold document-monsters were passed in a single round.
