The plat map is printed at 1:100 and Yolanda has it laminated now, because of the dew.

Lot 14's contour lines have moved again. She knows the old shape by heart—two lazy parentheses cupping the northeast corner, a half-foot rise where the footing drain was supposed to daylight. This morning the parentheses have closed. They make an O, and the O is not centered on the survey monument. It is centered on her.

She walks the perimeter because walking the perimeter is what a title clerk does. Boundary by inspection. Adverse possession runs on open, notorious, continuous use, and she has been continuous for eleven days.

The frame stands stud-bare, roof trusses stacked in the mud like the ribs of something butchered before it could be assembled. No sheathing. No doors.
