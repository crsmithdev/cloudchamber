# A repair is a new draw in a chain, held to a ledger pinned once per chain

A repair does not edit a brief in place. It writes a new draw that points back to the one it repairs, carrying unchanged parts over and rewriting only what accepted findings touch. The brief's settled facts are extracted into a ledger once, at the chain's root. Every later check, and every scene of the draft, is held to that same ledger plus its amendments. A ledger extracted again each round would drift with the prose it is meant to hold still, and an in-place edit would lose the brief the findings were raised against.

## Considered Options

- **Regenerating the chosen vignette or the ending from the premise on each repair**: re-cast the story in the simulation.
- **Regenerating the ending on every repair**: a different ending each time, against a fixed last beat.
