# Drafts are judged by a panel from other model families, never by Claude alone

A draft is judged blind, in pairs, by five judges from other labs over OpenRouter: Gemini, GPT, Grok, Kimi and GLM. It is judged against a narrated story (parity), or against another draft (head to head). A Claude judge scored the same texts 13 of 13 where GPT-5.1 scored them 5 of 12. It named clarity as the largest gap, while all three outside labs then named cost and presence. The judges also pick the text they read first, four of five on every pass. So a head to head runs an even number of passes, half in each order, and only an axis that holds in both orders counts.

## Consequences

Judging costs money on a separate account. Against a narrated source every recent draft wins nearly every pass, so parity shows only a gross loss, and a finer comparison needs a head to head.
