# Premises are asked for as a stated distribution, sampled from its tail

The premises ask makes the model state each premise's probability as a reading of the seed, and requires all five inside one band: `tail` (0 to 0.1, the default), `off-centre` or `standard`. A premise outside the band fails the step. The product is the strangest defensible reading of a seed, and a model left alone returns the most typical one. Stating the distribution and sampling a band of it is the lever, and the default is the tail on purpose.
