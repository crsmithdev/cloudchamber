# Stories: one list, one pane that follows the story

Decided with Chris on 2026-09-30, from the canvas "Cloud Chamber story layout"
(the artboard "A, refined"). It replaces the ideate and write tabs of
`2026-09-10-four-tabs.md`. The check tab went on the same day (IR spec §15.13).

## Why

A draw is one story that stops for a person three times: at the premises, at
the plan gate and at gate 2. Everything between the stops runs on its own. The
store has no fork (0 of 44 draws), so one seed makes one story. Two tabs cut
that story into two lists, and a draw stood in ideate for ever and in write
from its choice.

## The shape

| Part | Holds |
|---|---|
| rail | stories, sources |
| list | one row per story: a repair chain's head, or a draw that nothing repairs |
| pane header | the name, the status, the draw's tools, and the versions |
| strip | the five stops: premises, brief, plan, scenes, report |
| pane body | the stop the strip selects, one at a time |

## Rules

1. The list groups each story by who acts next.

   | Group | Statuses |
   |---|---|
   | needs you | `awaiting_gate`, `done`, `awaiting_plan_gate`, `awaiting_draft_gate` |
   | running | any status the lifecycle calls running |
   | kept | `drafted` |
   | other | `failed`, `awaiting_check_gate`, and a draw superseded by a redraw |

   A row shows a five-part bar for the stops and one line for where the story
   stands. Archived stories stay out until asked for.

2. The strip is the only navigation inside a story. A click on a stop shows
   that stop. The stop the story waits at has a gold bar. A stop the story has
   not reached is inert. When a story is opened, the strip selects the stop it
   stands at.

3. Each stop reuses the pane that exists for it.

   | Stop | Shows |
   |---|---|
   | premises | the seed, the examples and the candidates, with choose and fork. It reads the chain's first round, which ran the premises |
   | brief | the brief as it lands, or the brief that stands, with draft |
   | plan | the plan gate at `awaiting_plan_gate`; after it, gate 2's plan view: beats, symbols, findings |
   | scenes | gate 2's story view |
   | report | the report PDF |

   Plan and scenes share one gate 2 pane, so ticks made in one survive a move
   to the other, and gate 2's own "fix the plan" moves the strip to plan.

4. The versions are chips in the header: the rounds of the repair chain, the
   draw this one branched from, and the draws branched or forked from it. A
   chip opens that draw.

5. The routes are `#story/<id>` and `#story/<id>/<step>`, and `#new` for the
   draw form. `#draws`, `#draw/<id>`, `#write/<id>`, `#check/<id>`,
   `#go/<id>`, `#develop/<id>` and `#draws/new` redirect.

6. The server is unchanged: the lifecycle still answers which actions a draw
   allows, and the UI keeps no status rules beyond the grouping in rule 1.
