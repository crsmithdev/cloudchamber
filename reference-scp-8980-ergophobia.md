# Fog Belt — Reference Register: SCP-8980 (Ergophobia: Without Regards)

FOG BELT — REFERENCE REGISTER ADDENDUM

SCP-8980 — "Ergophobia: Without Regards" (Yossipossi, SCP Anthology 2024\)

Not djkaktus, but registered anyway at Chris's instruction — one of the most subtly horrifying articles on the site precisely because it is so plausible.

Researcher Lillian Marley, 28, Level 3 Containment Specialist at Site-17, a computer scientist. Her supervisor, Head Researcher Christopher Byrnes, manufactures her anomaly: electronics malfunction around her in ways that humiliate her specifically — emails sent to the wrong recipients, critical files deleted, one slide of a presentation replaced with pornography. He gaslights her into believing she is anomalous. She consents to her own classification, reasonably, expecting the matter to resolve in days or weeks. She is contained for nine years. It ends only because Byrnes retires.

WHAT TO STEAL

Consent is the trap. She agrees to containment. Everything that follows is procedurally correct.

He never breaks a rule — he files. Escalating cruelty as documented, justified process: confinement, humiliating physical testing, separation from family, sensory abuse, amnestic administration. The Code of Conduct requires him to oversee her amnesticisation, so he is present, providing the list of targeted engrams. He selects what she forgets. He tells her what he has done to her immediately beforehand.

The paper trail exonerates him because he authored it. Every complaint was investigated properly and the documentation supported him. It always did.

The system's own remedy is good and useless. The May 2015 Ethics Committee review is thorough — it names the abuses, annotates his files' inaccuracies and obfuscations, finds the strip searches unjustified, and awards \~$470,000, free counselling, and reintegration support. And Byrnes was amnesticised as routine procedure, so there is no longer a person who did it. The remedy is correctly calculated and addressed to nobody who can be held responsible.

Trauma without narrative. She retains the bodily memory and loses the account of it — PTSD without knowing you were traumatised. She knows she was made to forget something and cannot know what. Her knowledge and career are gone.

The last line is the worst one. Her new therapist records her as a difficult case, extremely apathetic, resistant. Correctly observed, by someone doing their job.

Strip the anomalous layer and it survives intact: an NDA, a settlement, and nine years of a career.

TEST ALIGNMENT

Passes the setting-c test (is the awful thing load-bearing) and the setting-b test (institutions correct, not deluded) while being entirely mundane. No villain is required beyond one man with authority and good documentation habits — which is itself the point.
